Epic acceptance criteria: BW-097-AC-2, BW-097-AC-4

Implementation scope

  • Reuse assessment: Provided by Unreal or a plugin — native meshes/materials/lighting and Slate/UMG DPI scaling are available; MultiplayerSessions Menu.cpp demonstrates widget input but is not a reusable gameplay HUD. Reuse native widget/layout facilities and T03 capture ownership, adding only required module dependencies.
  • Reuse assessment: Missing — representative project models, equipment, terrain, target/hazard markers and readable camera UI. Add a bounded original or already-available legally usable reference asset set with recorded provenance, recognizable silhouette/equipment/alternate-form contrasts, simple animation or poses, terrain/floor changes and bright/dark lighting presets. Include representative occluding walls/roof/foliage and limited water/transparent/effect samples; full environment production belongs to BW-098/BW-099.
  • Provide independently scalable labels/UI and target/hazard indicators that remain identifiable without color alone throughout both views, orbit and transitions. Expose scene lighting/material/effect/quality settings and UI scale via existing Unreal editor/widget/settings controls. Keep all required markers when reducing cosmetic effects. Record a visual/accessibility review at actual gameplay distances and the T01 window/high-DPI matrix.
  • Respect VisualPresentationDefinition server-event ownership, nullable telegraph_id and TelegraphDefinition geometry/critical semantics if those boundaries are used; do not invent attack authorization or timing from animation. SpriteDirection preview strings remain unchanged; representative heading comparisons do not require implementing character creation. Use only T01 authorized public fixture data and T05 visibility removal when available; no marker may reveal excluded entities.

Out of scope

  • Full roster/production asset library, rig/export workflow, character creation UI, final environments/weather, real combat or release accessibility certification.

Dependencies

Hard prerequisites: BW-097-T03

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    Recorded stills/motion at overview, close and intermediate views demonstrate recognizable character/equipment/terrain silhouettes and readable target/hazard markers in bright and dark presets. Non-color shape/pattern cues remain visible under reduced cosmetic effects and occluding samples.

  2. AC 2

    At every tested window/aspect/backing scale, labels and UI scale independently of the world image; text, pointer controls, targets and playable floor remain usable. Unavailable high-DPI configurations remain explicitly unverified.

  3. AC 3

    The bounded asset/provenance/configuration list and Unreal UI paths reproduce the review. Camera/display settings change no collision/reach/speed/aim, no hidden marker is fabricated, and visual/telegraph contract encodings and required/nullable fields remain compatible.

Actions

  1. Start PIE (or rendered standalone with -game and -BriarwakeCameraReference), click the viewport, use 1 stationary / 2 axial / 3 diagonal and R reset. Wheel through overview/intermediate/close and hold left mouse to orbit. Compare Bright/Dark, equipment on/off, raised pose and alternate form through the shared Data Asset. Identify the moving character, stationary public target, four terrain steps and ramp at gameplay distances. Middle-button drag must no longer orbit; wheel zoom must continue while left-dragging.
  2. Enable Show Occluders and inspect wall/roof/foliage, toggle transparent water/glass and Reduce Cosmetic Effects. Identify the diamond target and striped-square hazard without relying on color, including overlap. Reducing cosmetics removes sparks only; required geometry and eligible markers remain. Opaque samples suppress occluded labels, a clear view restores them, and hidden targets supply no label. Missing cues or labels through opaque geometry are failures.
  3. Test 1280x720, 1920x1080, 1280x960 and available native 2x backing. Record actual client/backing dimensions, aspect, OS scale, quality and screen percentage. At UI scales 0.75, 1 and 2, text/glyphs/control sizes must change independently of world framing. Read wrapped text, scroll the panel, click its counter and verify wheel/left drag over it do not move the camera. Playable floor/targets must remain usable; clipping, unreadable text or misaligned pointer hits fail. Mark unavailable high-DPI configurations unverified. Interrupt a left-drag by leaving the viewport or losing focus; returning requires a fresh left press and no stale orbit.
  4. Test invalid roughness/light/UI values for visible last-valid restoration. Stop PIE, save/reopen a valid temporary preset and confirm persistence, then restore documented defaults. Retain tested revision/settings, labeled bright/dark endpoint/intermediate stills, short moving/orbit comparisons and the display/UI matrix with reviewer findings, failures and mitigations in bounded owned artifacts.
