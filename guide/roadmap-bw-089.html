Objective and scope

Demonstrate that the fully enabled class and profession state survives reconnects, transfer and conservative crash recovery without duplicated power or erased debt.

Scope: Cross-system reconstruction regression for statuses/ticks, long cooldowns, summon capacity, forms, songs, signature resources, damage debt and profession jobs. This certifies integration beyond the first handoff. Voluntary effective-level/original build selection is included in the reconstruction matrix.

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    A graceful transfer preserves permitted status expiry/next-tick/remaining budget, stable cooldown families, damage debt and current build under the declared cleanup/checkpoint policy.

  2. AC 2

    Destination reconstruction leaves exactly one owner summon budget; source pets, links, auras and unattended effects cannot continue granting output after authority ends.

  3. AC 3

    Form/song/resource reconstruction references the same canonical pools and budgets; reconnect/transfer cannot grant health, refill resources, erase debt or bypass cooldowns. Effective-level build selection and its original saved build survive reconstruction without eligibility or point-budget bypass.

  4. AC 4

    Profession jobs and associated receipts settle or resume only under their documented policy; a transfer rejected by a locked transaction cannot duplicate work or refund a committed craft.

  5. AC 5

    Unclean crashes demonstrate the documented conservative recovery point and encounter reset, preserving durable ownership while explicitly reporting lost transient combat state.

Verification and blockers

Completion evidence required: Required future proof: advanced-state reconstruction fixtures and multiplayer fault-injection runs for each enabled mechanic, including reconnect/transfer during a tick, summon replacement, debt payment and profession settlement.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ticket planning

Before creating or revising tickets, apply the mandatory reuse assessment. Inspect existing owners and evidence, then map every criterion to the additional work and verification required.

Use BW-089 as the parent epic. Link each future ticket to the AC links above and state the evidence it will produce. Split work into demonstrable behaviors; one criterion may require several tickets. Epic completion still requires every criterion, verified evidence and completed hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Simple handoff should be proven early. Requiring every future class mechanic first delays risk discovery; treating that initial proof as certification of later state is equally unsafe.

Related open decisions: Incremental topology and comparable branch orders
