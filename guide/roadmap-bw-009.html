Objective and scope

Resolve eligible targets and aiming from authoritative information.

Scope: Target lock/cycling, friendly selection, line/cone/ground/self modes, reach, line of sight and floor rules; visual controls integrate in BW-028.

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    Target cycling follows angular difference, distance and stable-ID ordering and excludes illegal hidden/dead/friendly/cross-floor candidates.

  2. AC 2

    Every admitted targeting mode resolves the required intent fields and rejects unused or invalid target data.

  3. AC 3

    Range, relation and geometry failures leave resources and cooldowns unchanged at acceptance.

  4. AC 4

    Ground placement rejects protected, inaccessible and closed-door locations; faction/group relation changes are rechecked when required.

Verification and blockers

Completion evidence required: Target-policy fixtures over all supported modes, relations and geometry failures.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ticket planning

Use BW-009 as the parent epic. Link each future ticket to the AC links above and state the evidence it will produce. Split work into demonstrable behaviors; one criterion may require several tickets. Epic completion still requires every criterion, verified evidence and completed hard prerequisites.
