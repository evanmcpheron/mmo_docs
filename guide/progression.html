Levels & talent points

Mechanics, governing rules, edge cases and acceptance criteria.

Exactly two points per level-up

At level 1, characters have zero talent points. Each transition to levels 2–60 grants one class point and one specialization point. These currencies cannot be exchanged. By level 60, the character has 118 points: 59 class points and 59 specialization points.

class_points_earned(level) = level − 1

specialization_points_earned(level) = level − 1

total_points_earned(level) = 2 × (level − 1)

Level Class points Specialization points Spendable specialization points
1 0 0 0
2 1 1 0; banked
9 8 8 0; banked
10 9 9 9 after selecting a specialization
20 19 19 19
40 39 39 39
60 59 59 59

The level-10 selection screen previews the new kit and offers a starter allocation for the nine banked points. It never spends points without confirmation. This interpretation honors every level-up rather than quietly limiting the request to levels 11–60.

New characters follow the beginner starting-area path from level 1 to level 10. Its required tasks and encounters provide enough experience without optional activities or repeated grinding. Level-10 specialization selection, practice and a final solo challenge lead into the next leveling area. Completing the introduction grants no extra talent points.

Ability progression

Shared class abilities unlock at levels 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 14, 22, 32 and 44. The first three specialization abilities unlock together at level 10; the remaining three unlock at 20, 35 and 50. This replaces the old 10/16/24/34/44/54 specialization schedule.

The defining resource loop and basic role kit must work immediately at level 10. A tank receives a single-target challenge, threat stance and mitigation regardless of optional talents. Every healer receives an affordable direct heal, a small group-recovery tool, an eligible cleanse and out-of-combat revival. Existing class abilities satisfy these requirements where possible; a role-service ability fills an actual gap rather than adding a duplicate button. These capability requirements are enumerated separately from the 330 inherited named abilities.

Auto-attacks, interact, cancel cast, pet recall and out-of-combat recovery are common actions rather than talent purchases. Class passives must explain their actual mechanical effect and never conceal equipment proficiency behind a late optional node.

Six tiers, with real opportunity cost

Each of the 49 trees contains 36 nodes. Most investable nodes have three ranks. Tiers II and VI each contain a mutually exclusive three-way choice; only one node from each of those groups can be selected.

Tier Points already spent in lower tiers Class minimum level Specialization minimum level Contents
I 0 2 10 Six three-rank nodes
II 8 10 10 Three exclusive one-rank transformations; three three-rank nodes
III 18 20 20 Six three-rank nodes
IV 30 32 32 Six three-rank nodes
V 42 44 44 Six three-rank nodes
VI 52 54 54 Three exclusive one-rank capstones; three three-rank nodes

A legal fully expanded route contains 18 + 10 + 18 + 18 + 18 + 10 = 92 ranks. With 59 points, a player leaves at least 33 purchasable ranks unselected, in addition to the four unselected exclusive alternatives. Buying every useful-looking passive is not possible.

Thresholds count ranks in strictly lower tiers, not the node being purchased, not points in another tree, and not banked points. Refunds validate the whole resulting graph. A capstone requires a selected tier-II transformation. Node-specific referenced abilities must already be learned. Some class transformations consequently open later than the tier's minimum; the UI must show that requirement.

Avoiding point bloat

Two points per level are a reward cadence, not a reason to double every percentage. Rank-based improvements stay modest. Major changes live in explicit choices: moving versus stationary casting, burst versus sustained output, concentrated versus distributed healing, one servant versus a squad, and personal damage versus party contribution.

Do not award talent points from repeatable quests, purchased items, professions or random drops. That would invalidate the fixed budget and make non-combat activities mandatory for character power. New expansions may add alternative nodes before adding more points.

Respecialization and loadouts

Store class-tree allocation, selected specialization, specialization-tree allocation, action bars and equipment-set references in each named loadout. Applying a loadout is one version-checked server operation. The server returns either a complete accepted build or explicit validation errors; partial changes are not allowed.

Reallocation is free outside combat and outside an active competitive match or locked encounter. In a dungeon, require the party to be out of combat and no encounter in progress. A three-second stationary application is interrupted by combat or damage. Town-only restrictions are unnecessary for combat experimentation.

Changing builds does not reset cooldowns, restore missing resources, erase delayed damage, clear a harmful effect, or refresh consumables. Preserve health percentage but never increase absolute current health: new_health = min(old_health, new_max_health × old_health / old_max_health). Preserve no more than the old percentage of mana/energy; newly introduced combat-generated resources start at zero. Defensive cooldown families survive changes even when an ability is unequipped. Remove old summons, auras and invalid action-bar entries atomically.

Scaling and experience

Suggested level-up requirement: xp_to_next_level(L) = round(100 × L^1.7) for levels 1–59. This is a tuning curve, not a promised leveling duration. Ordinary same-level creature XP begins at 20 + 4L; quests and activities use expected effort rather than only kill count. Award group XP using eligible participation, distance and encounter ownership. Do not give kill-credit XP for friendly summons or repeatedly created objects.

Character-level progression and profession skill are distinct. Crafting does not become the fastest repeatable combat-leveling method by default. Optional introductory profession tasks may grant normal one-time activity XP.

For voluntary level-synced PvE, use effective level for stats, eligible ability unlocks and point budget. Save the original build unchanged. Apply a separately saved downscaled build, or a deterministic starter build after preview; never remove arbitrary nodes mid-encounter. Ranked PvP has its own normalization policy.

Acceptance criteria

Every level must expose enough legal node capacity to spend all currently spendable points. The shipped structural test checks capacity against current ability unlocks, lower-tier gates, exclusive choices and prerequisites. A separate runtime test must prove respec cannot heal, erase cooldowns, duplicate buffs or preserve an invalid pet.
