Objective and scope

Let a player enter lower-level PvE with an explicitly previewed legal effective-level build while retaining their original progression.

Scope: Effective-level stat recomputation, eligible ability and point budgets, separately saved downscaled builds or deterministic starter fallback, entry/exit preview and restoration; rated PvP normalization remains separate.

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    Opt-in preview identifies effective level, eligible abilities, recomputed base attributes and separate legal point budgets; downscaling does not multiply the entire original stat sheet.

  2. AC 2

    The original build remains unchanged; selection uses a separately saved valid downscaled build or a deterministic starter build after preview, never arbitrary mid-encounter node removal.

  3. AC 3

    Entry/exit applies a coherent authoritative loadout and rejects invalid/in-combat transitions without partial state, free healing, resource refill or cooldown/debt deletion.

  4. AC 4

    Reconnect and exit restore the appropriate original/effective build and earned progression once; lower-level execution cannot use an ineligible ability or overspend talent points.

Verification and blockers

Completion evidence required: Effective-level stat/allocation and ability-access fixtures, opt-in/fallback UI demonstration, and entry/exit/reconnect tests preserving original build, progression, cooldowns and obligations.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ticket planning

Use BW-077 as the parent epic. Link each future ticket to the AC links above and state the evidence it will produce. Split work into demonstrable behaviors; one criterion may require several tickets. Epic completion still requires every criterion, verified evidence and completed hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: PvE downscaling preserves original progression and changes eligible unlocks, unlike rated level-60 normalization. It can be validated on a bounded fixture without waiting for the whole dungeon catalog.
