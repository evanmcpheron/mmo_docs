Phase objective and completion

Connect protected clients, move and resolve one visible action, then measure the small integrated workload.

0/12 complete · 0%

Phase status: Not Started. Complete only when all 12 epics below are Complete with verified evidence.

The integrated two-client action and early load probe pass; no durable player journey is claimed.

Integrated completion milestone: Two-client authoritative action. Its full prerequisites and integration evidence must also pass; individual components do not prove their integration.

Hard dependencies are required for completion. Recommended predecessors are scheduling preferences. Other phases are not global start barriers. How to read this roadmap.

Epics in this phase

  1. BW-004 · Dedicated connectivity and bounded command admission Not Started

    Connect authenticated test clients to a dedicated authority and reject invalid requests.

  2. BW-005 · Authoritative movement and world geometry Not Started

    Resolve movement from client intent against authoritative geometry and time.

  3. BW-006 · Derived attributes and bounded resources Not Started

    Resolve numerical character state without requiring a complete class roster.

  4. BW-007 · Entity lifecycle and recipient snapshots Not Started

    Give clients a coherent view of entities they are allowed to observe.

  5. BW-008 · Predicted movement and reconciliation Not Started

    Keep local movement responsive while the server retains the final position.

  6. BW-009 · Target relations and legal spatial intent Not Started

    Resolve eligible targets and aiming from authoritative information.

  7. BW-010 · Cast commitment and ability timing Not Started

    Resolve one authoritative ability lifecycle with precise failure semantics.

  8. BW-011 · Damage healing and absorb resolution Not Started

    Resolve effective combat results using documented numerical and accounting rules.

  9. BW-013 · Interest privacy and bounded state traffic Not Started

    Keep disclosure correct as relevance and entity counts change.

  10. BW-028 · Action controls and legible combat feedback Not Started

    Expose enough accessible interaction to test actual gameplay decisions early.

  11. BW-012 · First authoritative multiplayer action proof Not Started

    Demonstrate the smallest integrated movement-and-action loop before a full game journey.

  12. BW-083 · Representative early simulation and bandwidth probe Not Started

    Expose architectural bottlenecks using the first integrated multiplayer workload before broad content production.
