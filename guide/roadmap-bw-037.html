Objective and scope

Turn reserved materials into one exact useful output through a recoverable short craft.

Scope: A representative band-I manufacturing chain, station validation, result preview and short synchronous jobs; public orders and mastery are separate. Excludes adjacent gameplay families and production certification beyond this epic’s stated acceptance criteria.

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    The quote shows exact inputs, deterministic output, recipient, grade, binding, skill progress and required station.

  2. AC 2

    An accepted craft reserves owned versioned inputs; cancellation or precommit failure yields no output or skill.

  3. AC 3

    Commit consumes once, produces once and grants eligible skill once; retries return the existing receipt.

  4. AC 4

    A postcommit crash or full bag preserves the output in durable delivery; disconnected ordinary crafts do not silently become offline jobs.

  5. AC 5

    Unknown or unlearned recipes, insufficient skill, inactive professions and unauthorized stations reject before reservation; legitimate learning makes the same recipe available without bypassing other eligibility rules.

  6. AC 6

    Before the first craft and after recipe/vendor changes, the enabled price/conversion graph passes the shared admission gate; a crafted-output sellback loop is rejected before it can mint value.

Verification and blockers

Completion evidence required: Future real-store concurrency, reservation recovery and pre/postcommit crash tests, plus one usable crafted item demonstration.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ticket planning

Use BW-037 as the parent epic. Link each future ticket to the AC links above and state the evidence it will produce. Split work into demonstrable behaviors; one criterion may require several tickets. Epic completion still requires every criterion, verified evidence and completed hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: A small useful craft validates ownership and delivery without requiring marketplace listings or all profession content.
