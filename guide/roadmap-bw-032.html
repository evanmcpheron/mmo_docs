Phase: 4. First complete persistent player journey · Completion milestone: First complete persistent opening playtest

Objective and scope

Demonstrate that specialization and talent choices change decisions rather than only tooltip numbers.

Scope: Two legal builds within one specialization and a contrasting second specialization; bounded talent bindings only.

Dependencies and downstream work

Hard prerequisites

These epics must be Complete before this epic can be completed or meaningfully verified.

  • BW-024 · Talent legality and atomic build application Not Started
  • BW-031 · Representative five-player role encounter Not Started

Recommended predecessors

Scheduling guidance; these are not completion gates.

None.

Required by

These epics directly depend on this capability.

  • BW-073 · Combat, build and PvP balance certification Not Started

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    At least two legal builds in one specialization change at least two recorded decision dimensions across stationary, moving, interrupted, spread-target and short/sustained scenarios while retaining baseline role viability.

  2. AC 2

    A second specialization demonstrates its documented loop and weakness under equal role and equipment budgets.

  3. AC 3

    Positive and exclusion cases cover each enabled behavior-changing talent in the proof package.

  4. AC 4

    Changing the tested builds preserves cooldown, resource, harmful-status and summon restrictions.

Verification and blockers

Completion evidence required: Future build allocations, comparable action traces, human evaluation protocol/results and executed talent fixtures.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ticket planning

Use BW-032 as the parent epic. Link each future ticket to the AC links above and state the evidence it will produce. Split work into demonstrable behaviors; one criterion may require several tickets. Epic completion still requires every criterion, verified evidence and completed hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Prove meaningful branches on a small package before binding all 1,764 nodes. Structurally legal allocation examples are not this evidence.

Related open decisions: Measured balance, usability and content limits
