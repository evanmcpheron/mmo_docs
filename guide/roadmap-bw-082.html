Phase: 5. Bounded mechanics and safe world evolution · Completion milestone: Bounded state and world evolution proof

Objective and scope

Transfer a character between two authoritative processes while preserving one owner and committed economic state.

Scope: Explicit source suspension, checkpoint, one-use destination proof, epoch claim/revocation and destination snapshot for the currently enabled simple character state. Comprehensive advanced-state certification follows separately.

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    During handoff new source actions stop; pending economic operations settle or reject before the checkpoint, and committed operations remain durable.

  2. AC 2

    Only the bound destination can consume an unexpired one-use proof for its exact character/checkpoint/epochs; replay, wrong destination and stale epoch reject without spawning another owner.

  3. AC 3

    At every failure boundary one process/epoch retains or recovers authority; the source cannot mutate the character or grant rewards after the destination claim.

  4. AC 4

    Destination state preserves location, current build, long cooldowns and the documented enabled-state checkpoint policy, then reconstructs an owner-private snapshot and independently filtered public view.

  5. AC 5

    Source/destination crash, timeout and disconnect scenarios return the character to a known legal authority/location without duplicating inventory or reversing completed trades.

Verification and blockers

Completion evidence required: Required future proof: two-process fault-injection traces at each handoff boundary, duplicate/expired/wrong-destination proofs, stale-owner reward attempts and inventory/receipt reconciliation across retries.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ticket planning

Use BW-082 as the parent epic. Link each future ticket to the AC links above and state the evidence it will produce. Split work into demonstrable behaviors; one criterion may require several tickets. Epic completion still requires every criterion, verified evidence and completed hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Reserve scope and epoch contracts early, but postpone distributed handoff until local transitions and durable fencing can be observed. One-owner results are required; message delivery is not described as exactly once.

Related open decisions: Incremental topology and comparable branch orders
