Phase: 4. First complete persistent player journey · Completion milestone: First complete persistent opening playtest

Objective and scope

Deliver the first complete level 1–10 player experience using a declared representative playable roster.

Scope: Equivalent faction-separated starter layouts for an initial Warrior/Bard/Mage roster, bound early kits, guidance, solo graduation and a safe explicit exit; full roster acceptance is BW-052.

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    Fresh supported characters create, enter and complete the five learning stages with abilities at levels 1/3/5/7/9.

  2. AC 2

    Required quests and distinct guaranteed encounter XP reach level 10 before graduation without optional tasks or repeated grinding.

  3. AC 3

    Inventory/equipment, vendor, recovery, an optional party task and optional useful gather/craft practice are accessible within the compact journey; skipping group/profession activities never blocks the required solo path.

  4. AC 4

    Specialization preview spends nine banked points only after confirmation; tank/healer/damage graduation is solo-completable with friendly targets where needed.

  5. AC 5

    Death, shared targets, full bags and logout do not block progress; departure names a valid next objective/destination.

  6. AC 6

    The representative journey uses the selected 3D pixel-art camera and reviewed starter assets; scene occluders, projected targeting and independent UI remain usable, with final environment breadth and full-roster art certified later.

Verification and blockers

Completion evidence required: Future fresh-character end-to-end recordings, required-path XP report, role graduation tests and persisted interruption matrix.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ticket planning

Before creating or revising tickets, apply the mandatory reuse assessment. Inspect existing owners and evidence, then map every criterion to the additional work and verification required.

Use BW-039 as the parent epic. Link each future ticket to the AC links above and state the evidence it will produce. Split work into demonstrable behaviors; one criterion may require several tickets. Epic completion still requires every criterion, verified evidence and completed hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: A complete beginner experience requires coherent rewards and recovery, but need not wait for all 37 kits, the bazaar, raids or multi-server hosting.
