BW-097-T07 · Test water reflection/refraction methods and visibility-safe fallbacks

Implementation ticket for BW-097.

Not Started · Parent epic: BW-097 · 3D pixel-art renderer and camera feasibility proof

Epic acceptance criteria: BW-097-AC-5, BW-097-AC-8

Implementation scope

  • Add --case water to the existing composition probe with a bounded shoreline/pool fixture, underwater geometry, moving outlined/transparent actors, surface animation, foam/splash samples and toggles for candidate reflection/refraction and lower-cost methods. This proves feasibility; the river/lake/ocean/waterfall production matrix belongs to BW-099.
  • First evaluate whether shaders, animation, authored geometry and particles meet the T03-approved water cues. Record remaining unmet cues before any proposal for physical fluid simulation; implementing a fluid solver or adding swimming/weather gameplay is outside this ticket.
  • Exercise off-screen authorized objects entering the view, newly revealed/removed objects, camera turns/motion, water-surface crossings and intersecting transparent/outlined objects. Distinguish missing screen-space information from stale buffers and pass ordering; exercise the same cases on the chosen method and fallback rather than assuming any reflection technique handles them.
  • Extend T06 synthetic recipient-view fixtures so reflection/refraction, shadows and auxiliary cameras receive only authorized content. Keep excluded entities absent from effect passes even when their hypothetical position is off-camera, underwater or on another floor; do not widen EntitySnapshot data or create a second visibility policy.

Out of scope

  • Production water assets and exhaustive environment biomes (BW-099), physical fluid simulation, server water gameplay or a real AOI implementation.

Dependencies

Hard prerequisites: BW-097-T06

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    The review records which required water cues shaders/animation/geometry/particles satisfy and any remaining visual gap; a physical-simulation proposal requires a demonstrated unmet approved requirement.

  2. AC 2

    Chosen reflection/refraction and fallback have captured off-screen/newly-visible, moving outline/transparency and above/below-water cases. Missing information, stale samples and ordering artifacts have tested mitigations or approved visual alternatives on every claimed supported path.

  3. AC 3

    Authorized views remain readable without stale silhouettes; excluded synthetic entities contribute no reflection/refraction/outline/shadow cue, including off-screen and cross-floor cases. Unsupported renderer paths and blocking visual findings prevent acceptance.

Manual verification

Run from the Briarwake implementation repository. The commands, scenes and fixtures below are deliverables of this ticket and its prerequisites; these are unexecuted test instructions.

Launch command or scene

Working directory: GAME_ROOT, the briarwake/ repository root; every res:// path uses that project root. The rendering launcher/case below is a deliverable of these tickets, not an existing command.
python3 tools/verify_toolchain.py
python3 tools/run_rendering_probe.py --case water

Setup

  1. Use T03 water comparison requirements and T06 pass/view fixtures. This ticket supplies pool/shore/underwater geometry, animation/foam/splash samples, off-screen paths and method/fallback controls.

Actions

  1. Run water and compare shader/animation/geometry/particle candidates against the approved reference cues. Capture selected and fallback methods with water/effects isolated and combined.
  2. Move authorized actors out of view and back, spawn/remove them, cross the water surface with outlined/transparent objects, then replay slow/diagonal camera paths. Repeat with excluded/cross-floor fixtures and inspect auxiliary inputs and final buffers.

Expected results

  1. Both selected and fallback paths meet approved water/readability cues, with missing screen-space content handled by a tested mitigation or approved alternative.
  2. Excluded objects never appear indirectly and no removed object leaves stale output. Any unsupported path, artifact or unmet visual requirement has an explicit review outcome.

Reset

  1. Use the probe Reset control to restore the checked-in case settings, camera, actor path and effect toggles; quit the graphical process normally. No persistent gameplay state is created.
  2. Keep prior captures, failed results and reports intact. Repeat into a fresh launcher-owned artifacts directory; restore only deliberately modified disposable inputs, leaving source assets, canonical registries and existing user files intact.

Completion evidence and blockers

Required evidence: Retain water requirements/candidate assessment, method and fallback settings, pass/view matrix, off-screen/newly-visible and surface-crossing clips, synthetic exclusion checks and approved mitigation decisions.

Blockers: None recorded; see dependencies.

Recorded evidence: None. Planning does not establish an executed pass.
