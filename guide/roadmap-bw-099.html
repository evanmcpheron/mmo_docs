Phase: 8. Full progression, cooperative and competitive experiences · Completion milestone: Complete documented playable scope

Objective and scope

Carry the selected 3D pixel-art style through complete gameplay environments without losing combat readability.

Scope: Terrain, foliage, water, lighting, weather/ambient art and supported visual-quality tiers. These are presentation requirements, not new weather, swimming or day/night gameplay systems.

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    Approved outdoor/interior and bright/dark scenes use the shared art conventions; foliage coverage, shadow changes and camera motion preserve readable silhouettes and target markers without unstable flicker.

  2. AC 2

    Water and shoreline scenes retain legible characters, terrain boundaries, selection and relevant hazards across supported views and quality tiers; visual presentation never reveals hidden state.

  3. AC 3

    Atmospheric light, rain, dust and ambient effects remain visually consistent without masking targets or critical telegraphs; supported quality tiers preserve gameplay clarity and have measured costs.

  4. AC 4

    Roofs, trees, water and layered floors have verified occlusion and visibility behavior; concealed or cross-floor entities are not disclosed through the scene or targeting interface.

  5. AC 5

    Quality changes and cosmetic substitutions preserve critical telegraphs, selection, aim/ground previews and friendly-frame access in crowded scenes; reduced-flash/shake controls and readable dark presets are verified.

  6. AC 6

    Each supported tier has reproducible scene/settings captures and CPU/GPU/memory measurements including dense foliage, water, atmosphere and overlapping combat effects; expensive effects have bounded budgets and tested alternatives.

Verification and blockers

Completion evidence required: Versioned environment scenes, art-direction comparisons, visibility/accessibility recordings, measured resource budgets and a tested quality/platform matrix.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ticket planning

Use BW-099 as the parent epic. Link each future ticket to the AC links above and state the evidence it will produce. Split work into demonstrable behaviors; one criterion may require several tickets. Epic completion still requires every criterion, verified evidence and completed hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Build on established camera and asset conventions; full presentation and release capacity must evaluate complete gameplay environments.

Related open decisions: Camera, asset conventions and supported visual quality
