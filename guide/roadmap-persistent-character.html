Detailed epics

BW-014 · Status lifecycle control and trigger bounds Not Started

Objective: Apply persistent combat effects without stacking or recursive event exploits.

Scope: Refresh/tick rules, cleanses, control/DR, stealth/immunity projections and bounded trigger attribution; summon objects are separate.

Hard dependencies: BW-011, BW-013

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. DoT/HoT refresh preserves the next tick and 30% carry cap; shield and strongest-category rules prevent additive refresh exploits.
  2. Cleanse selects one legal effect in documented priority; no eligible effect means no accepted cost or cooldown.
  3. PvP duration caps, shared diminishing history, rolling allowances and boss immunity obey documented rules; rated control-break uses its shared 120-second cooldown and declared two-second category immunity.
  4. Trigger depth/child bounds, proc exclusions and no-reflect/redirect/echo recursion hold; hidden status sources remain redacted.
  5. Death/logout/transfer cleanup policies compose and preserve surviving expiry/tick/budget as declared.
  6. Two-client late join, interest re-entry and authorization-loss tests reconstruct current status/tick state without a missing earlier event, while public status allowlists and hidden-source redaction remain enforced.

Completion evidence required: Executed status edge fixtures, control histories, event traces and filtered projection captures.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Status and summon rules; Combat rules; Authority and networking

BW-015 · Projectile and ground-effect execution Not Started

Objective: Resolve traveling and spatial effects independently of target-acquisition method.

Scope: Swept collision, hit budgets, lifetimes, homing/straight/piercing semantics, ground placement/ticks and relevant visual state.

Hard dependencies: BW-010, BW-011, BW-013

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Fast projectiles cannot skip thin solid walls, closed doors or floor boundaries; piercing actor hits never imply wall penetration.
  2. Homing impact rechecks relation/existence, straight attacks retain aim and repeated hits obey finite target budgets.
  3. Ground effects enforce placement, tick timing, overlap/target caps and current hostile/friendly relations.
  4. Committed attacks that hit a wall do not refund costs; actual damage/healing uses the shared resolver. Joining or re-entering interest receives the currently relevant hazard, schedule and expiry through independently authorized owner/public views.

Completion evidence required: Geometry/collision and ground-tick fixtures with two-client hazard snapshots.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Combat rules; Execution contracts; Authority and networking

BW-016 · NPC engagement threat and reset Not Started

Objective: Make a bounded authored opponent engage, select actions and reset safely.

Scope: Finite NPC state/ability conditions, same-floor navigation, threat, leash and encounter-owned hazards; authored dungeon content is later.

Hard dependencies: BW-014, BW-015

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. NPC behavior traverses idle, engaged, leashing and dead with legal finite priority scheduling and bounded navigation.
  2. Effective damage, healing and consumed shields generate documented threat; taunt cannot engage a distant unengaged boss or affect players.
  3. Aggro changes at the documented melee/ranged thresholds and scripted targets return to normal threat afterward.
  4. Leashing clears encounter threat/hazards, restores home state and grants no reward or consumable refund; death emits one completion identity.

Completion evidence required: Executed threat, navigation, leash and reset fixtures with event histories.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Combat rules; Supporting gameplay

BW-017 · Death revival and solo recovery Not Started

Objective: Provide safe defeat and recovery without resetting committed obligations.

Scope: Atomic death cleanup, legal recovery destinations, food/rest baseline and encounter-shared revival charges; repair settlement connects in BW-026.

Hard dependencies: BW-016

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Zero health stops casts, invalidates pet commands, settles outstanding damage obligations and marks death once.
  2. Ordinary death deletes no items, levels or talent points; recovery cannot restore spent consumables or reset cooldown debt.
  3. Out-of-combat revival follows documented duration/resources, and encounter charge budgets reset only on a genuine reset/completion.
  4. Solo characters can recover without a required profession; illegal revival states or destinations fail with a visible reason.

Completion evidence required: Death/revival/reset fixtures, including repeat death messages and simultaneous revival requests.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Combat rules; Supporting gameplay; Status and summon rules

BW-019 · Account admission and exclusive character sessions Not Started

Objective: Bind real account authentication to revocable character authority.

Scope: Trusted opaque login/recovery adapter, transport protection, session expiry/revocation, single-owner epochs and permission checks; support workflows expand later.

Hard dependencies: BW-004, BW-018

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Only a verified unexpired scoped trusted proof admits an economic character; one-use proofs cannot be replayed.
  2. A second connection cannot operate the same character concurrently and stale sessions/epochs cannot issue successful actions or grants.
  3. Expiry, restriction, logout and recovery revoke the appropriate credentials and return actionable client states.
  4. Missing lifetime/rate/payload settings disable affected endpoints; credentials and provider secrets are absent from client logs and artifacts.

Completion evidence required: Account-adapter integration and adversarial admission/session tests, including simultaneous login and revocation.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Real identity precedes durable characters and shared assets; a restricted authenticated test session was sufficient for the earlier technical slice.

Related open decisions: Capacity, security and recovery budgets

Related documentation: Authority and networking; Supporting gameplay

BW-020 · Atomic character creation and selection Not Started

Objective: Create a legal durable character through the documented preview-and-review flow.

Scope: Faction/race/class eligibility, appearance choices, Unicode naming, account-scoped creation receipt, selection and safe initial entry.

Hard dependencies: BW-019, BW-006

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. The preview/review/selection flow preserves compatible choices, exposes unavailable classes with reasons and retains the draft after failure.
  2. All eleven races and twelve faction-race selections obey class restrictions; Humans have identical racial attributes on both sides.
  3. Naming fixtures cover documented normalization, grammar, realm-wide cross-faction uniqueness and retained reservations under a pinned Unicode revision.
  4. Concurrent creates claim a name and starting records atomically; retry returns the same character and invalid appearance choices cannot enter.

Completion evidence required: Creation UI review and naming/eligibility/atomicity integration runs over the legal catalog.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related open decisions: Engine, platform and deployment candidates; Concrete world and starter records

Related documentation: Character creation; Race and faction eligibility; Authority and networking

BW-021 · Inventory wallet and guaranteed delivery Not Started

Objective: Make personal assets durable and recoverable under contention and full bags.

Scope: Container placement, stack/split/move/consume operations, currency ledger, asset binding and pending reward delivery; exchanges use this common boundary later.

Hard dependencies: BW-020

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Concurrent asset changes validate owner/container/item revisions and cannot duplicate, overfill or produce negative stacks/currency.
  2. Bound, reserved or escrowed assets cannot be transferred through personal inventory operations.
  3. Full bags produce a durable claimable delivery; retry, restart and expiry of a loot corpse never destroy an awarded item.
  4. Every committed asset change has an attributable receipt/ledger entry and reconnect reads the same ownership and balance.

Completion evidence required: Inventory/wallet contention, restart and full-bag claim tests with reconciled ledgers.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Itemization; Authority and networking; Supporting gameplay

BW-022 · Equipment placement and derived loadouts Not Started

Objective: Apply legal item loadouts to authoritative combat state.

Scope: Equipment slots/proficiencies, bag-to-equipment ownership, adaptive item budgets, binding and comparison UI; repair/enhancement settlement is BW-026.

Hard dependencies: BW-021

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Equip/unequip atomically changes item/container/slot revisions and cannot leave an item equipped and in a bag simultaneously.
  2. Class armor/weapon restrictions and two-hand/off-hand rules reject illegal loadouts.
  3. Slot weights, grade multiplier, adaptive primary and reserved enchant budget apply once and match published examples.
  4. Replacing equipment updates authoritative derived stats and shows comparable gains/tradeoffs without adding hidden instrument/tool slots.
  5. Weapon/auto-attack output respects the normalized single/dual-weapon budget; faster swings cannot multiply the declared on-hit proc rate budget.

Completion evidence required: Equipment concurrency/eligibility fixtures, item-budget comparisons and loadout UI capture.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Itemization; Attributes; Interface and accessibility

BW-023 · Experience unlocks and specialization selection Not Started

Objective: Persist the documented level and role progression independently of the full content roster.

Scope: XP thresholds, level cap, class unlock schedule, banked specialization points, role training and level-ten selection.

Hard dependencies: BW-020, BW-010

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Level 1 begins with zero points; every level-up from 2 through 60 awards exactly one class and one specialization point.
  2. Pre-ten specialization points remain banked; level ten selection exposes nine earned specialization points without an extra graduation award.
  3. Abilities unlock at their declared levels and role training supplies mandatory tank/healer services rather than optional talents.
  4. Repeated XP/unlock operations and restart preserve one award and a coherent level/build revision; illegal or premature selections reject.

Completion evidence required: Threshold/award/restart fixtures and level-ten selection demonstration.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Progression; Character creation; Specialization contracts

BW-024 · Talent legality and atomic build application Not Started

Objective: Accept legal builds and apply their declared behavior as one versioned change.

Scope: Tree budgets/prerequisites/exclusivity, previews, respec restrictions, rollback/revalidation and safe-point application; exhaustive content binding is later. Named saved loadouts contain both allocations, selected specialization, action bars and equipment-set references.

Hard dependencies: BW-023, BW-022

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Both trees enforce rank, tier, prerequisite, exclusive-choice and 59-point constraints at every spendable level.
  2. Client previews never authorize a build; stale or invalid submissions preserve the previous active build and points.
  3. Accepted named loadouts atomically apply both tree allocations, specialization, action bars and validated owned equipment-set references after the three-second stationary application; stale, missing or illegal equipment references and combat/damage interruption preserve the previous loadout.
  4. A content change requiring refunds/revalidation preserves stable IDs and restores a legal build rather than retaining obsolete effects.
  5. Application obeys party/encounter/match restrictions, never increases absolute current health or restores missing resources/cooldowns/debt, and removes old summons/auras/invalid action entries atomically.

Completion evidence required: Positive and exclusion talent fixtures, stale build contention runs and a migration/refund scenario.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Build application needs the equipment ownership/slot boundary as well as progression. A legal talent allocation cannot authorize a stale or foreign equipment-set reference.

Related documentation: Progression; Execution contracts; Interface and accessibility; Talent catalog

BW-025 · Participation credit and durable loot awards Not Started

Objective: Turn one legitimate encounter completion into one eligible durable reward.

Scope: Engagement snapshots, contribution, open-world claims, loot selection/claim-pass and personal reward delivery; skinning rights remain separate.

Hard dependencies: BW-016, BW-021

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Engagement membership/bounds plus effective damage, healing, prevention, tanking or support qualify credit; late joins and ineffective farming do not.
  2. Encounter/creature-generation and recipient identity grant once across duplicate deaths, retry and restart.
  3. Guaranteed/probability/weighted loot modes follow declared tables; shared claim/pass persists one shuffled order and never rerolls on delivery retry.
  4. Full bags retain awarded delivery and corpse expiry cannot delete it; leash/reset awards nothing.

Completion evidence required: Credit edge cases and persisted loot/delivery concurrency runs with reconciled completion identities.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Supporting gameplay; Itemization; Authority and networking

BW-026 · Vendor repair and enhancement settlement Not Started

Objective: Support basic preparation and item upkeep through durable personal services.

Scope: Buy/sell, repair/durability, enhancement/upgrade eligibility and confirmation; crafting-service production and recrafting are separate.

Hard dependencies: BW-022, BW-017

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Vendor quotes use declared price/eligibility configuration and stale, forged or unaffordable purchases cannot alter assets.
  2. Ordinary PvE death reduces equipped durability by five percentage points; zero durability removes combat benefits while preserving ownership and repairability, and rated PvP applies no durability loss.
  3. Repair, legal upgrade and enhancement application commit payment/materials and item revision together or leave all unchanged.
  4. Confirmation/comparison discloses output and costs; binding, reserved enhancement budgets and incompatible categories cannot be bypassed.
  5. Repair quotes use the explicit NPC-purchase-value/missing-durability formula and configured minimum; undamaged equipment costs zero and missing price/coefficient configuration rejects the quote.
  6. Admission of vendor values checks the current enabled recipe/conversion graph for guaranteed material, currency or progression loops; invalid or missing required prices cannot enable a route. Craft sellback stays below guaranteed NPC-purchased input cost.

Completion evidence required: Vendor and item-service atomicity fixtures plus durability and comparison UI demonstration.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related open decisions: Prices, sources and conversion graph

Related documentation: Itemization; Economy rules; Interface and accessibility

BW-027 · Reconnect checkpoints and conservative crash recovery Not Started

Objective: Restore a character without duplicating authority or clearing obligations.

Scope: Disconnect grace, clean logout, owner-private reconnect baselines, cooldown/status/debt checkpoints and documented unclean encounter reset.

Hard dependencies: BW-017, BW-021, BW-024

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. In-combat disconnect retains the configured grace behavior without invulnerability or new player/pet commands; reconnect claims the existing character.
  2. Reconnect restores durable assets/build/progress and surviving cooldown, status and damage obligations without duplicate rewards.
  3. Logout and crash recovery apply declared cleanup/reset policies, never inventing exact replay of uncheckpointed combat ticks.
  4. Expired/stale sessions and simultaneous reconnects leave one authoritative owner; the public snapshot is filtered separately from owner-private restoration.

Completion evidence required: Injected disconnect/crash/reconnect fixtures at cast, status, reward and build boundaries with reconciled ownership.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Recovery is proven before a complete persistent journey. Exact tick replay is not required by the documented conservative recovery policy.

Related open decisions: Incremental topology and comparable branch orders

Related documentation: Authority and networking; Status and summon rules; Supporting gameplay

BW-029 · Scoped communication and player reporting Not Started

Objective: Provide bounded communication and abuse controls as soon as testers interact socially.

Scope: Local/private/party/raid/guild routing, rate/restriction checks, mute/block/report and moderation history; group/guild channels activate only with authoritative membership.

Hard dependencies: BW-019, BW-013

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Local/private messages obey authoritative visibility, recipient restrictions, mute/block and configured spam limits without revealing hidden players.
  2. Group/guild routes require current authoritative membership when those systems are enabled; absent membership cannot be forged.
  3. Reports preserve relevant context and authorized moderation actions/restrictions are auditable without exposing secrets.
  4. Reconnect and duplicate messages cannot bypass restrictions; denied sending returns an understandable result.

Completion evidence required: Routing/privacy/rate-limit fixtures and an auditable report-to-restriction test.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Core communication can proceed independently of combat. Full party/guild routing is integrated when those memberships exist.
