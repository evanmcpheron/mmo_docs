Phase objective and completion

Combine reusable encounter rules with durable identity, progression, equipment and reconnect behavior.

0/14 complete · 0%

Phase status: Not Started. Complete only when all 14 epics below are Complete with verified evidence.

The initial combat/character reconstruction proof passes; later mechanics add their own reconstruction cases.

Integrated completion milestone: Recoverable persistent character. Its full prerequisites and integration evidence must also pass; individual components do not prove their integration.

Hard dependencies are required for completion. Recommended predecessors are scheduling preferences. Other phases are not global start barriers. How to read this roadmap.

Epics in this phase

  1. BW-014 · Status lifecycle control and trigger bounds Not Started

    Apply persistent combat effects without stacking or recursive event exploits.

  2. BW-015 · Projectile and ground-effect execution Not Started

    Resolve traveling and spatial effects independently of target-acquisition method.

  3. BW-016 · NPC engagement threat and reset Not Started

    Make a bounded authored opponent engage, select actions and reset safely.

  4. BW-017 · Death revival and solo recovery Not Started

    Provide safe defeat and recovery without resetting committed obligations.

  5. BW-019 · Account admission and exclusive character sessions Not Started

    Bind real account authentication to revocable character authority.

  6. BW-020 · Atomic character creation and selection Not Started

    Create a legal durable character through the documented preview-and-review flow.

  7. BW-021 · Inventory wallet and guaranteed delivery Not Started

    Make personal assets durable and recoverable under contention and full bags.

  8. BW-022 · Equipment placement and derived loadouts Not Started

    Apply legal item loadouts to authoritative combat state.

  9. BW-023 · Experience unlocks and specialization selection Not Started

    Persist the documented level and role progression independently of the full content roster.

  10. BW-024 · Talent legality and atomic build application Not Started

    Accept legal builds and apply their declared behavior as one versioned change.

  11. BW-025 · Participation credit and durable loot awards Not Started

    Turn one legitimate encounter completion into one eligible durable reward.

  12. BW-026 · Vendor repair and enhancement settlement Not Started

    Support basic preparation and item upkeep through durable personal services.

  13. BW-027 · Reconnect checkpoints and conservative crash recovery Not Started

    Restore a character without duplicating authority or clearing obligations.

  14. BW-029 · Scoped communication and player reporting Not Started

    Provide bounded communication and abuse controls as soon as testers interact socially.

Open an epic for its full scope, dependencies, acceptance criteria, verification and ticket planning.
