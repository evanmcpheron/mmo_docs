How to read the roadmap

Evidence baseline

Game implementation is 0% complete. Every implementation epic and ticket is Not Started; phase and milestone progress derive from those records. No gameplay system, client, server, build pipeline, rendering pipeline, character, map, database, plugin integration or operational infrastructure is implemented or verified. Completed design contracts and passing documentation checks are not implementation evidence.

Each epic is one coherent, demonstrable capability with a permanent BW identifier, scope, hard prerequisites, acceptance criteria and required evidence. Phases group integration objectives derived from the dependency graph; they do not require every independent branch to wait for the previous phase.

Navigate from the dashboard to a phase index, then open an individual epic page. Every epic has direct links to its prerequisites, downstream work, recommended sequence neighbors and numbered acceptance criteria. Existing dashboard and phase epic bookmarks lead to the same canonical epic page.

Hard dependencies must be Complete before the dependent epic can be completed or meaningfully verified. Recommended predecessors improve risk reduction or integration efficiency without becoming technical gates. Direct dependencies are preferred; prerequisite closure supplies inherited requirements.

The displayed sequence is a reasoned engineering recommendation, not an immutable technical fact. Numerical IDs do not imply priority. Reorder an epic only after checking its dependencies and integration gates; keep its ID and anchor stable.

An epic reaches Complete only when every acceptance criterion has passing, revision-specific runtime/content/operational evidence and its hard prerequisites are Complete. Criteria define observable behavior rather than internal methods. Failure scenarios are part of completion. Status does not advance automatically when a document or fixture specification is written.

Milestone requirements include their transitive hard prerequisites. Explicit integration/certification epics test connected behavior on compatible builds. Phase completion means every member epic is Complete; it does not replace those integrated proofs.

Foundations certify the state families in their tested package. An epic introducing another family must extend the applicable recipient-view, reconstruction, security and operational checks before admitting it. This never certifies a future family in advance; full-roster and release certification still cover their entire declared scope.

Presentation uses actual 3D characters and environments, Unreal materials and post-processing with a pixel-stable angled orthographic camera. SpriteDirection preserves canonical preview-heading values; no sprite-sheet production mandate follows. Every render setting and acceptance bound awaits fresh prototype evidence.

Documented requirement
An explicit gameplay or integrity rule in the feature reference; preserve it regardless of the recommended schedule.
Technical constraint
A real limitation such as recipient-safe value replication or one active owner; a tool capability is not evidence that the game meets it.
Hard dependency
A capability needed to complete or meaningfully verify another epic, represented by an explicit ID edge.
Recommended ordering
A judgment about risk, testability, integration or maintenance; independent work may move earlier.
Assumption
An unverified working expectation, recorded with a resolution gate rather than treated as a fact.
Open decision
A future implementation/configuration/content choice that current evidence cannot responsibly settle.

Dependency analysis and research

Review all epic objectives, scopes, acceptance criteria and dependencies against the canonical gameplay reference, complete catalog families and Unreal Engine 5 C++ authority/lifetime boundaries. Preserve engine-independent responsibilities and stable IDs. Establish a blank native project, then verify the already acquired replication plugins before dependent transport and movement. Keep renderer feasibility, content production and real-store recovery probes independently schedulable. Trace every feature family through initial integrated play, full-roster certification and production evidence; distinguish hard technical prerequisites from risk-reducing recommendations.

Research basis: Governing design decisions; Unreal Engine 5 C++ foundation; Authority and networking; Execution contracts; Statuses and summons; Creation and beginner journey; Profession requirements; Economy; Interface and accessibility; Supporting gameplay; Validation and evidence; Readiness and remaining work; Canonical definitions and resolved history; Ability inventory; Talent inventory; Representative recipes; Selected 3D pixel-art direction; Rendering, camera and input; Rendering verification. All class and profession pages, canonical structures and shipped catalogs were considered together; existing implementation-order advice was evaluated as a hypothesis.

Architectural sequencing decisions

These are engineering judgments constrained by documented behavior. Where alternatives are comparable, changed evidence may justify a new order without changing IDs.

Authority and the first network proof

Chosen sequence: Create the native C++ project, establish rule/connection ownership, verify the supplied plugins, then validate two independent clients with authoritative movement and a direct action before the full combat roster or persistent journey.

Alternatives and tradeoff: A local rules experiment can help numerical work but cannot expose connection ownership, recipient disclosure or actual dedicated-server lifecycle and latency. Headless rule fixtures can proceed alongside transport once the native baseline exists.

Basis: Server authority is documented. The size and placement of the first integrated proof are recommendations intended to discover boundary errors at low content cost.

BW-001, BW-002, BW-100, BW-004, BW-005, BW-012 · Authority and networking; Supporting gameplay; Validation and evidence

Replication grows with executable semantics

Chosen sequence: Verify the acquired plugin/native replication relationship first. Reuse the validated facilities for movement/lifecycle and owner/public views, then extend executable state families with recipient-safe reconstruction.

Alternatives and tradeoff: A universal final replication layer before gameplay exists guesses state semantics. Broadcasting all state and hiding it in the client leaks information. Rich spatial tuning can wait for measurements, while privacy and hazard relevance cannot.

Basis: Epic documents native replication and owning-connection mechanisms. The supplied plugins’ actual APIs, platforms, authority limits and relevancy behavior remain unknown until BW-100; replication never substitutes for authorization or durable ownership.

BW-100, BW-007, BW-013, BW-014, BW-015, BW-027 · Authority and networking; Execution contracts

Prediction before broad usability judgments

Chosen sequence: Add prediction/reconciliation after authoritative movement and acknowledgments, before interpreting broader movement/combat playtests.

Alternatives and tradeoff: Prediction is unnecessary for numerical headless tests or a tightly scoped authority proof. Deferring it until a large content roster risks tuning hazards around zero-latency assumptions. Unlimited historical rewind is neither needed nor part of the baseline.

Basis: The target network impairment scenarios are documented; an acceptable correction budget and supported platform envelope must be measured and approved.

BW-008, BW-012, BW-083, BW-090 · Authority and networking; Validation and evidence

Small durability proof early; live assets later

Chosen sequence: Test durable ownership, version conflicts, receipts and crash boundaries early in parallel with simulation; require them before real inventory, rewards and exchanges.

Alternatives and tradeoff: Persisting every tick increases cost without the promised exact combat replay. Waiting until all combat is complete postpones the most consequential conservation/concurrency risks. A purely in-memory economy cannot certify restart safety.

Basis: Economic acknowledgments must be durable; conservative combat checkpoints/reset are explicitly sufficient. A small conventional relational deployment is the documented starting recommendation, not a hard requirement for independent microservices.

BW-018, BW-019, BW-021, BW-025, BW-027 · Authority and networking; Economy; Validation and evidence

Stable boundary contracts, incremental binding

Chosen sequence: Preserve IDs, versions, authority, coordinate scope, explicit rejection and compatible wire values first. Bind only enabled records and expand concrete content with executed fixtures. A versioned author/import route produces these packages; migration/admission are distinct consumers of that route.

Alternatives and tradeoff: Freezing every future final payload delays gameplay learning on unauthored content. Inventing disconnected local models now creates integration debt. Existing complete contracts constrain behavior without claiming their handlers exist.

Basis: All 29 recorded contract questions are resolved; 329 ability and 882 inherited talent bindings remain runtime/content work. Documentation completeness is not implementation readiness.

BW-002, BW-024, BW-051, BW-091, BW-095 · Governing design decisions; Execution contracts; Readiness and remaining work

Commitment before effects; representative mechanics before volume

Chosen sequence: Prove legal targeting, cost/timing commitment and numerical resolution before mass ability expansion. Validate statuses, hazards, signature resources and representative advanced state as distinct capabilities. Combine the representative families in one measured multi-client probe before broad cohort certification.

Alternatives and tradeoff: Implementing all classes first repeats mistakes across hundreds of bindings. Requiring every effect family before one network action overextends the first experiment. Mandatory level-ten roles already need signature state, so deferring every advanced resource until after the starter is also unsound.

Basis: Reservation/release/interrupt behavior, bounded triggers, role services and reconstruction are explicit requirements; grouping families into small risk probes is the scheduling judgment.

BW-009, BW-010, BW-011, BW-014, BW-031, BW-040, BW-044, BW-045, BW-051, BW-096 · Execution contracts; Statuses and summons; Supporting gameplay

Two meanings of playable

Chosen sequence: Use a minimal technical multiplayer proof first; retain the level 1–10 beginner area as the first complete persistent player journey. Prove a representative supported kit before all-roster graduation certification.

Alternatives and tradeoff: Making the full beginner journey the first experiment combines identity, economy, XP, quests and content before authority is checked. Waiting for all 12 classes before any beginner playtest delays useful usability evidence. The representative slice is explicitly incomplete roster evidence.

Basis: The beginner requirements mandate solo completion, equivalent faction opportunities, normal unlock levels and enough required XP without optional grinding. They do not mandate that no smaller technical experiment precede them.

BW-012, BW-033, BW-039, BW-052 · Creation and beginner journey; Supporting gameplay; Validation and evidence

Local scopes before distributed handoff

Chosen sequence: Establish zone/floor/instance identity with geometry, build explicit local transitions, then prove a two-process ownership handoff and later full advanced-state reconstruction.

Alternatives and tradeoff: Fleet orchestration and seamless travel before an instance works add deployment complexity without a measured target. Deferring epochs/namespaces until production creates duplication/disclosure rework. Small local instances remain a valid independently testable stepping stone.

Basis: One owner, one-use transfer proof and safe economic settlement are documented; the separate timing of local and distributed delivery is a recommendation.

BW-005, BW-080, BW-081, BW-082, BW-089 · Authority and networking; Supporting gameplay

One asset boundary; parallel service branches

Chosen sequence: Build inventory/wallet/delivery before exchanges. Direct craft, commissions, direct trade, marketplace and guild bank reuse that boundary but are not forced into a single sequential economy bundle.

Alternatives and tradeoff: Requiring the bazaar before commissions is unnecessary: customer escrow can use the same durable ownership without a market listing. Independent custom wallets or guild-bank mutation paths create conservation risks. Profession breadth does not block stable combat.

Basis: Bound customer components never belong to the crafter; all transfer channels share eligibility, revisions and receipts. Branch order is flexible once these invariants are executable.

BW-021, BW-037, BW-055, BW-056, BW-058, BW-064 · Economy; Profession requirements; Crafting orders

Operational capability follows the value at risk

Chosen sequence: Start with reproducible headless runs, bounded test admission and diagnostics. Add real provider security, privileges and verified restore before persistent external play; prove updates/migrations before ongoing expansion, then certify capacity and incidents on full workloads.

Alternatives and tradeoff: A production fleet before evidence delays learning. Leaving security and recovery until release makes persistent playtests unsafe and their results difficult to interpret. Architecture may remain a small deployment until measured capacity requires change.

Basis: No concurrency/SLO/recovery objective has already been achieved. Chosen budgets and real restore/rollback exercises are completion evidence, not a checklist of infrastructure products.

BW-003, BW-018, BW-083, BW-084, BW-086, BW-087, BW-088, BW-090, BW-093, BW-094 · Authority and networking; Supporting gameplay; Validation and evidence

Renderer feasibility before asset volume

Chosen sequence: Run a bounded 3D pixel-art camera/material/pass proof alongside headless development, establish reusable art import conventions before starter production, and certify environmental effects and complete assets before final presentation and capacity.

Alternatives and tradeoff: A bounded Unreal material/camera probe before asset-volume production reduces import, pass-order and input-projection risk. Use stock-engine mechanisms first; engine modifications require a measured unmet requirement and a maintenance decision. No earlier visual or performance result is credited.

Basis: The established visual target uses real-time 3D models, a pixel-stable angled orthographic camera, toon materials, outlines, water and atmosphere. Unreal rendering-path, post-process/translucency and platform feasibility require fresh revision-specific evidence. Canonical simulation and wire values remain unchanged.

BW-097, BW-098, BW-028, BW-039, BW-099, BW-074, BW-090 · Selected 3D pixel-art direction; Rendering, camera and input; Rendering verification

Independent technical checks. Epic documentation was reviewed on 2026-10-20: dedicated-server build/cook workflow, native replication, object lifetime and C++ automation. These establish candidate facilities, not verified Briarwake capabilities or MMO capacity. The supplied-plugin/native relationship must be verified before dependent work. External sources require internet; local roadmap reading and documentation validation do not.

Final dependency review

  • Moved the durable ownership/recovery probe into the first review group so database correctness can be tested alongside simulation instead of waiting for a full role encounter. It has no combat dependency.
  • Kept privacy in the initial entity projection and added richer relevance bounds before the first integrated action. Prediction follows authoritative movement but is a recommendation for the technical proof, not a fabricated prerequisite of numerical combat.
  • Introduced a representative signature-resource probe before role and starter integration; full pets, forms, maintained support and damage-debt families precede roster expansion, rather than every first playable interaction.
  • Separated a representative beginner playtest from full-roster level 1–10 certification. Optional crafting and grouping remain optional to a player, while the documented introductions still need integration evidence.
  • Separated local travel and instance identity from cross-process ownership. Simple handoff is tested before final advanced-state recovery; the first handoff does not certify mechanics introduced afterward.
  • Removed global phase barriers and kept social, profession and economic branches independently actionable. Recommended predecessors express integration convenience; only observable prerequisites are hard edges.
  • Required provider security and a real restore before persistent external testing, while early headless fixtures and restricted technical sessions stay small. Final capacity and incidents wait for representative full workloads.
  • Retained separate full-game integration and release gates. Completion percentages describe epic counts only; undefined capacity, balance targets or launch-content bounds cannot be replaced by optimistic status updates.
  • A competent team could move some profession/social work earlier or defer a simple handoff until larger zones exist. This order favors earlier conservation/ownership learning; it makes no claim that those comparable branch orders have a unique optimum.
  • All retained epic responsibilities were reviewed as future Unreal C++ capabilities. Engine-independent gameplay, content and failure criteria retain stable IDs. Core rules stay in C++; Blueprints and editor assets must not create a second authority. Explicit exclusions keep adjacent families and later certification separate.
  • BW-001 creates the blank native project, targets and reproducible build route; BW-002 now requires that executable baseline. BW-100 verifies already acquired plugins before BW-004/BW-005. Renderer, content and real-store probes do not depend on unverified network capabilities.
  • Character/Pawn movement and native prediction are evaluated before custom systems; plugin interaction is verified first. Unreal relevancy and supplied capabilities serve canonical recipient policy without assuming automatic privacy, database persistence, authentication or MMO scale.
  • Initial connectivity includes loss, stale-session rejection and fresh-session re-entry. Durable reconnect remains BW-027 after ownership, inventory and build prerequisites. All nine milestones derive status from epic closure; documentation checks do not advance them.
  • The independent audit retains the rated-entry/build-selection decision as open and preserves the content production, economic conservation and combined-family integration gates. Every current epic has a generated assessment linked to its objective, criteria and verification obligations.
