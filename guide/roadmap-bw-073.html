Phase: 8. Full progression, cooperative and competitive experiences · Completion milestone: Complete documented playable scope

Objective and scope

Evaluate the complete playable roster with recorded scenarios and explicit uncertainty.

Scope: Solo/role encounter output, all 37 specialization routes, PvP matchups, sustain/control/mobility and faction composition; numerical tuning remains versioned.

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    All 37 specializations have at least two tested legal builds changing at least two decision dimensions while retaining role viability; equal-budget output, survival, control, mobility and ally contribution traces expose their intended tradeoffs.

  2. AC 2

    Solo time-to-kill and healer sustain are measured against prototype expectations; provisional 5% output outliers receive documented investigation.

  3. AC 3

    PvP analysis includes melee uptime, line of sight, pets, healer interruption, pick/exclusion rates and skill/role-matched outcomes.

  4. AC 4

    Human build evaluations identify unusable/filler branches and exploit incentives; the enabled release package records resolutions rather than declaring schema counts balanced.

Verification and blockers

Completion evidence required: Future reproducible simulation/runtime reports, human playtest evidence, balance change history and approved enabled-content manifest.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ticket planning

Use BW-073 as the parent epic. Link each future ticket to the AC links above and state the evidence it will produce. Split work into demonstrable behaviors; one criterion may require several tickets. Epic completion still requires every criterion, verified evidence and completed hard prerequisites.

Related open decisions: Measured balance, usability and content limits; Rated entry eligibility and normalized build selection
