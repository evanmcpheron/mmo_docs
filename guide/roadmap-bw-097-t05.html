Epic acceptance criteria: BW-097-AC-5

Implementation scope

  • Reuse assessment: Provided by Unreal or a plugin — APlayerController deprojection/tracing and UWidgetLayoutLibrary viewport geometry/DPI methods are available. Extend T01 controller/camera input ownership with one world-content-rectangle conversion and reuse it for entity selection and ground previews; never infer canonical floor identity from a raw trace Z value.
  • Reuse assessment: Implemented and verified — FContentContracts, FAbilityCommand and DecodeAbilityCommand in ContentAdmission/AbilityCommand.cpp already preserve ability_command_v2 shape/nullability and strict rejection. Reuse this owner and canonical extractor/reference closure for any serialized fixture boundary. Do not add a second command decoder, validator or production targeting implementation.
  • Reuse assessment: Missing — project world picking, floor-scoped GroundTargetPreview and visibility fixture projections. Add finite known anchors for two overlapping floors plus zone/instance mismatch, occluding geometry, UI/letterbox capture and authorized/excluded/revoked entity cases. Supply public fixture projections to the camera while keeping excluded records out of that view entirely, not merely hidden on a local mesh. Do not pass FixtureWorldContext private npc_states/parties to presentation; its existing empty-world probe is not a general populated gameplay world.
  • Preserve WorldPoint zone_id/instance_id/floor_id and continuous foot-anchor position with an explicit reference-only conversion between authored tile widths and Unreal transforms. GroundTargetPreview requires ability_id, point, legal and findings; legal is advisory. A test-only intent sink compares preview/confirmed intent against known anchors via existing FAbilityCommand without committing a cast. ability_command_v2 keeps all three target keys present with unused values null; aim_point stays Vector2 in authenticated fixture scope, never a client-selected floor/actor or a new wire field.
  • Across follow, scroll/projection handoff, orbit, resize/DPI and occlusion, reject outside-content/UI-captured/invalid/no-hit/foreign-floor or unauthorized entity input observably; clear stale previews/selections on capture/authorization/floor/view-target loss. Camera changes cannot expand the supplied authorization envelope or mutate route/collision/aim. Server route/range/visibility validation, actual casts, live replication and full controls remain with BW-005/BW-009/BW-013/BW-028; this isolated proof certifies only the camera boundary. Document this active-view/picking boundary so BW-028-T01 reuses its coordinate conversion and adds live action controls/server checks instead of maintaining a parallel conversion.

Out of scope

  • Live authentication, interest filtering, production movement/targeting/cast execution, damage/resources, a new RPC/protocol and reimplementing future BW-009/BW-028 controls.

Dependencies

Hard prerequisites: BW-097-T03

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    Known same-floor anchors yield the expected scoped WorldPoint and preview/confirmed point within a recorded world-space tolerance throughout follow/zoom/orbit/projection handoff/resizing/high-DPI. The intent sink decodes canonical ability_command_v2 with unchanged keys/nullability; no camera-only action emits or commits gameplay intent.

  2. AC 2

    UI/letterbox input, invalid/no-hit geometry, nonfinite coordinates, wrong zone/instance/floor and excluded entity IDs produce no selectable entity or confirmed target. Rejection is attributable, pending state clears and fresh valid input recovers. Canonical malformed/missing/ambiguous/unknown-field inputs are rejected through the existing reader.

  3. AC 3

    Excluded entity records/IDs/positions never enter camera render/marker/picking data, logs or captures. Revoking a previously authorized projection removes its mesh/marker/selection/preview as applicable. Zoom/orbit/occlusion cannot restore it, expose another floor or modify route/facing/aim; retained evidence states that a fixture is not a connected privacy test.
