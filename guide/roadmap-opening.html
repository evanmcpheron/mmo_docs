Phase objective and completion

Deliver an initial supported beginner and role slice with security, support and restore gates for external persistent testing.

0/16 complete · 0%

Phase status: Not Started. Complete only when all 16 epics below are Complete with verified evidence.

The representative beginner/role playtests use a common compatible build, and provider security plus actual restore evidence permit external persistent play. The starter slice integrates the reviewed 3D art workflow and camera.

Integrated completion milestone: First complete persistent opening playtest. Its full prerequisites and integration evidence must also pass; individual components do not prove their integration.

Hard dependencies are required for completion. Recommended predecessors are scheduling preferences. Other phases are not global start barriers. How to read this roadmap.

Detailed epics

BW-098 · Reusable 3D asset and animation production workflow Not Started

Objective: Make consistent characters, equipment and starter scenery producible through a repeatable reviewed art workflow.

Scope: Source models/textures, rigs, attachments, animation/material conventions, render-resource packaging and a representative starter kit; gameplay records retain their canonical authority and full-roster production is certified in BW-074.

Hard dependencies: BW-095, BW-097

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Reviewed source assets reimport reproducibly with stable presentation references, scale, pivots, foot anchors, material settings and finite resource budgets; missing/incompatible assets produce actionable findings before package publication.
  2. A representative character, equipped weapon/armor, alternate appearance and combat form preserve attachment alignment and readable silhouettes across approved camera views; preview and in-world rendering use compatible assets and materials.
  3. Locomotion, casts, impacts, interruption, death and form changes follow authoritative event/time samples after prediction/reconciliation; model animation never changes hit geometry or attack release.
  4. A compact terrain/prop/foliage kit and approved palette/value/material examples demonstrate the selected style in the starter view, with low-quality and occlusion variants; contact/shadow treatment does not mask legal targets or hazards.
  5. An accepted and rejected art edit demonstrate resource validation, reviewed diffs, retained prior output and declared source/license provenance; the art package references existing gameplay content without adding a competing gameplay schema.

Completion evidence required: Versioned source/imported asset package, import and rejection logs, art conventions, representative actor/equipment/form motion captures and starter-kit comparison scenes at tested settings.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Establish reusable art production before the first complete starter journey; backend creation and isolated combat fixtures may use declared placeholder assets in parallel.

Related open decisions: Render configuration and supported quality envelope

Related documentation: Selected 3D pixel-art direction; Rendering, camera and input; Rendering verification; t3ssel8r visual research channel

BW-045 · Signature meters, marks and action sequences Not Started

Objective: Represent bounded gameplay state derived from successful actions rather than speculative client events.

Scope: Representative owner/target marks, charge/sequence/phase state and event-qualified generators; all-kit numerical bindings follow.

Hard dependencies: BW-014

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Representative owner meters, target marks and ordered action families obey declared caps, expiry, generation and spending.
  2. Rejected, cancelled, repeated, overhealing and ineligible self-owned actions cannot manufacture qualifying progress.
  3. One root action cannot grant per-target resources unless the explicit contract permits it.
  4. A released action consumes its selected marks/charges once and exposes the same canonical state to applicable tooltips/UI.

Completion evidence required: Future event-boundary and invalid-action fixtures covering Guard, target marks and a sequence/phase mechanic.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Mandatory level-10 roles already need signature state. Prove a small family before the first role slice instead of postponing every secondary mechanic as late content.

Related documentation: Signature state policies; Specialization loops

BW-043 · Maintained songs and bounded support Not Started

Objective: Make support measurable without stacking unlimited group amplification.

Scope: Song slots/replacement/finales, category caps, effect attribution and visible aggregate strength; no rhythm-input requirement.

Hard dependencies: BW-025, BW-028, BW-045

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Two ordinary song slots are available under the defined policy; a permitted third divides the same aggregate budget.
  2. Song replacement/overlap and finalization consume the declared state once and obey category caps.
  3. Effective allied contribution is attributable for encounter credit; unused or self-recursive support cannot farm rewards.
  4. Movement and latency do not introduce audio-timing requirements; UI exposes maintained slots and strength.

Completion evidence required: Future stacking/overlap/finale fixtures and group contribution traces with visible song state.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Bard song policy; Support roles

BW-030 · Party and raid membership Not Started

Objective: Let players form persistent-authority groups and inspect the capabilities they bring.

Scope: Invitations, leadership, removal, encounter locks, party/raid frames and capability inspection; reward settlement remains BW-025.

Hard dependencies: BW-019, BW-028, BW-029

Recommended predecessors: BW-025

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Only recipients accept pending unexpired invitations; retries return the same terminal membership result.
  2. Unauthorized leadership changes, over-capacity acceptance and disruptive encounter-time membership changes reject.
  3. Party and raid views agree with server membership and show missing role-service categories without requiring a named class.
  4. Party/raid communication routes only to current authoritative members and obeys the same mute/block/report and rate restrictions as direct/local channels.

Completion evidence required: Future invitation-race and permission tests, plus a multiplayer membership and role-inspection demonstration.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Groups and invitations; Capability parity

BW-031 · Representative five-player role encounter Not Started

Objective: Prove tanking, healing and damage cooperate in one readable server-owned encounter.

Scope: Warrior, Bard and Mage representative kits, mandatory level-10 role services, encounter telegraphs and one completed five-player test; full roster certification follows.

Hard dependencies: BW-017, BW-023, BW-030, BW-043

Recommended predecessors: BW-012

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. One tank, one healer and three damage players complete the authored encounter using shared-faction providers.
  2. Threat changes, effective healing, consumed prevention and interrupts produce the documented authoritative results.
  3. Every selected tank and healer has its mandatory role kit without optional talent purchases.
  4. A wipe clears encounter-owned state while preserving player cooldowns, debt, consumed items and committed rewards.
  5. The same encounter exercises a tank-facing cone, an interruptible cast, recoverable group damage, a moving hazard and a short priority-target phase with the documented readable setup; completion and wipe evidence cover their combined interaction.

Completion evidence required: Future recorded multiplayer run, role-service matrix and reset/failure fixture results tied to content revisions.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: An integrated role slice exposes threat, support and recovery errors before large catalogs multiply them; it does not require every specialization.

Related documentation: Level-10 role services; Encounter composition

BW-032 · Measured build and specialization proof Not Started

Objective: Demonstrate that specialization and talent choices change decisions rather than only tooltip numbers.

Scope: Two legal builds within one specialization and a contrasting second specialization; bounded talent bindings only.

Hard dependencies: BW-024, BW-031

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. At least two legal builds in one specialization change at least two recorded decision dimensions across stationary, moving, interrupted, spread-target and short/sustained scenarios while retaining baseline role viability.
  2. A second specialization demonstrates its documented loop and weakness under equal role and equipment budgets.
  3. Positive and exclusion cases cover each enabled behavior-changing talent in the proof package.
  4. Changing the tested builds preserves cooldown, resource, harmful-status and summon restrictions.

Completion evidence required: Future build allocations, comparable action traces, human evaluation protocol/results and executed talent fixtures.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Prove meaningful branches on a small package before binding all 1,764 nodes. Structurally legal allocation examples are not this evidence.

Related open decisions: Measured balance, usability and content limits

Related documentation: Playstyle evaluation; Build application

BW-033 · Durable quest objectives and rewards Not Started

Objective: Track authenticated gameplay objectives and award each completion once.

Scope: Quest prerequisites, objective credit, return interactions, tracker and resumable progress; authored starter tasks are BW-039.

Hard dependencies: BW-023, BW-025, BW-028, BW-095

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Accepted quests advance only on matching successful actor events; duplicate root events clamp to the required count.
  2. Combat credit respects encounter membership and actual damage, healing, prevention, tanking or support participation.
  3. Prerequisite and return requirements reject premature completion; repeated practice never repeats one-time rewards.
  4. Reconnect and full-bag completion retain objective progress and deliver the same awarded assets.

Completion evidence required: Future objective-kind fixtures, duplicate-credit tests and persisted completion/reconnect/full-bag demonstrations.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related open decisions: Concrete world and starter records

Related documentation: Quest contracts; Starter requirements

BW-034 · Profession enrollment and skill progression Not Started

Objective: Keep profession learning independent of combat level and talent currencies.

Scope: Two manufacturing slots, two gathering slots, three universal life skills, skill 1–300 and deterministic progress accounting. Training and declared acquisition sources grant durable recipe knowledge; deactivation retains learned recipes.

Hard dependencies: BW-021

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Selection enforces two manufacturing and two gathering slots while Cooking, Fishing and First Aid remain universally available.
  2. Difficulty-relative successful actions award 100/50/20/0 progress units at the documented thresholds and carry remainders.
  3. Cancellation, retries, salvage and material transfers grant no duplicate progress.
  4. Training and band access use authored fees/skill requirements without inventing a character-level gate.
  5. Recipe learning validates its declared acquisition source and eligibility, records unique character/recipe knowledge once across retry/restart, and retains it while the profession is dormant; an unauthorized grant cannot unlock a recipe.

Completion evidence required: Future enrollment, progression-threshold and durable retry tests using actual service records.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Profession slots; Skill progression; Durable recipe knowledge

BW-035 · Shared resource nodes and skinning rights Not Started

Objective: Let eligible players claim bounded resource outputs without duplication or stolen loot reservations.

Scope: Mining, Herbalism, Forestry and post-loot Skinning interactions; fishing timing is BW-036.

Hard dependencies: BW-025, BW-034

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. An ordinary node exposes three charges, a three-second channel and one claim per character per authoritative respawn cycle.
  2. Concurrent completion yields only available charges; a losing or interrupted claim consumes no tools or inputs.
  3. Respawn/replacement follows authored positive timing and active-node caps; clients cannot respawn resources.
  4. Skinning waits for eligible loot resolution/public grace and rejects encounter-reserved carcasses.

Completion evidence required: Future contested-node, restart/cycle, interrupted-channel and carcass-reservation scenario results.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related open decisions: Prices, sources and conversion graph

Related documentation: Shared access; Skinning

BW-037 · Quoted synchronous personal crafting Not Started

Objective: Turn reserved materials into one exact useful output through a recoverable short craft.

Scope: A representative band-I manufacturing chain, station validation, result preview and short synchronous jobs; public orders and mastery are separate.

Hard dependencies: BW-034, BW-095

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. The quote shows exact inputs, deterministic output, recipient, grade, binding, skill progress and required station.
  2. An accepted craft reserves owned versioned inputs; cancellation or precommit failure yields no output or skill.
  3. Commit consumes once, produces once and grants eligible skill once; retries return the existing receipt.
  4. A postcommit crash or full bag preserves the output in durable delivery; disconnected ordinary crafts do not silently become offline jobs.
  5. Unknown or unlearned recipes, insufficient skill, inactive professions and unauthorized stations reject before reservation; legitimate learning makes the same recipe available without bypassing other eligibility rules.
  6. Before the first craft and after recipe/vendor changes, the enabled price/conversion graph passes the shared admission gate; a crafted-output sellback loop is rejected before it can mint value.

Completion evidence required: Future real-store concurrency, reservation recovery and pre/postcommit crash tests, plus one usable crafted item demonstration.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: A small useful craft validates ownership and delivery without requiring marketplace listings or all profession content.

Related open decisions: Prices, sources and conversion graph

Related documentation: Craft lifecycle; Deterministic products

BW-080 · Safe local zone transitions and recall Not Started

Objective: Move a character between declared locations within one authoritative world process without bypassing travel or recovery rules.

Scope: Local zone/floor changes, protected destinations, legal return anchors and explicit loading feedback. Cross-process ownership handoff is a separate capability.

Hard dependencies: BW-010, BW-019

Recommended predecessors: BW-017, BW-027

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Only declared source/destination locations and authorized return anchors can be selected; stale, unknown, cross-instance or inaccessible destinations reject with a specific reason.
  2. Combat, prohibited objectives, locked crafting transactions and unavailable destinations prevent travel under the documented eligibility rules without spending a rejected action cost.
  3. A successful transition presents one destination character and the correct floor/zone visibility; stale source targets and unauthorized interest are removed.
  4. Recall observes its declared cast, cooldown and safe-anchor restrictions; cancellation and failed travel preserve the current authoritative character and committed economic results.

Completion evidence required: Required future proof: local transition and recall fixtures covering floors, protected areas, combat/crafting rejection, cancellation, destination failure and stale source messages, plus a demonstrated loading transition.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Zone, instance and floor identity must exist with movement; moving between local scopes does not require a fleet or cross-process transfer service.

Related open decisions: Incremental topology and comparable branch orders

Related documentation: Travel safety; World ownership; Return-anchor execution

BW-084 · Finite privileged support permissions and audit Not Started

Objective: Provide traceable support and administrative actions with the documented default-deny realm scopes.

Scope: Read-only support, moderation/restrictions, progression/inventory edits, economy freezes/reversals and release/restore authorization. Domain-specific recovery workflows remain separate epics.

Hard dependencies: BW-021, BW-023

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Support cannot mutate; moderator, game_master, economy_operator and release_operator can perform only their declared permissions within an explicit authorized realm/target scope.
  2. Missing grants/scopes, unknown permissions, client-supplied roles and attempts to grant unavailable authority reject without side effects.
  3. Every privileged mutation records immutable actor, action identity, target, reason, applicable versions and result; economic changes use canonical receipts/ledgers.
  4. Retried and concurrent grants preserve expected-version and idempotency rules; denied actions cannot create partial inventory, currency or progression edits.
  5. An authorized support user can investigate a character/action using permitted records without seeing credentials, opaque proofs or unnecessary personal data.

Completion evidence required: Required future proof: permission/realm matrix tests, unauthorized escalation attempts, duplicate/concurrent edit tests and inspectable redacted audit trails for each allowed mutation family.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Manual edits become a second exploit path unless they share ownership and receipt rules. This can be completed on the initial persistent slice before large social/economic systems exist.

Related documentation: Finite administrative roles; Security boundary

BW-085 · Economic incident visibility, freezes and reversal Not Started

Objective: Detect and contain economic corruption while preserving legitimate ownership and a complete audit history.

Scope: Receipt/ledger investigation, configured anomaly signals, visible service suspension, scoped asset freezes and authorized fraud reversal. Market balancing and general moderation are separate. Recurring economic reconciliation covers every admitted asset-mutation service.

Hard dependencies: BW-084

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. An authorized investigator traces an item/currency mutation through operation, owner, lineage, content and ledger versions, including duplicate delivery and settlement retry history.
  2. Configured monitoring exposes conservation exceptions, settlement backlog and suspicious repeated mutations with an identified measurement window; no unmeasured profitability or fraud claim is reported.
  3. Configured suspension or asset freeze rejects affected currently enabled economic operations with a user-visible reason while committed outcomes remain reconcilable; later market/crafting services pass the same checks before admission.
  4. A permitted reversal accounts for both items and currency through the canonical transactional path, records its relation to the original operation and cannot selectively rewind one side.
  5. Repeated reversal/freeze requests and concurrent normal operations cannot duplicate assets or conceal the immutable original audit history.
  6. A daily reconciliation report over a declared consistent interval accounts for authorized currency sources/sinks and zero-net internal transfers, identifies unmatched item/receipt/ledger/outbox operations, and exposes missing or failed reconciliation. Every later economic service joins this reconciliation before admission.

Completion evidence required: Required future proof: injected duplicate/settlement anomalies, investigation traces, freeze/unfreeze exercises and concurrent idempotent reversal tests against the real database. Later services must join the same incident checks before release. Include executed daily reconciliation with seeded discrepancies, matching interval/checkpoint records and visible failure handling.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Containment and conservation can be proven before the bazaar exists; adding a market or guild bank must extend this path instead of creating a new recovery authority.

Related documentation: Economic monitoring; Security and recovery; Audited operations; Daily reconciliation contract

BW-086 · Production identity recovery and security policy Not Started

Objective: Complete provider-backed authentication, recovery, revocation and explicit endpoint/retention policy for external persistent play.

Scope: Trusted adapter integration, transport security, credential/session lifecycle, configured limits, restriction/appeal authorization and bounded retention. Gameplay authority remains with existing domain owners.

Hard dependencies: BW-084

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. The configured trusted provider verifies opaque purpose/account/audience-scoped one-use login/recovery proof; invalid, expired, replayed and wrong-account proofs cannot acquire a character.
  2. Recovery and rotation revoke old handles and owner epochs before fresh control; retries return the stored recovery receipt and cannot authorize another account.
  3. Every published request family has explicit rate, payload and collection limits enforced independently of GCD; missing configuration disables that endpoint.
  4. Account/service traffic uses the chosen standard secure transport, secrets/proofs stay out of logs/public snapshots and configured expiry/retention rules are verified.
  5. Restrictions and appeals preserve original history; only authorized terminal review creates a separate audited action with reviewer/time, and revoked access cannot continue through an old connection.

Completion evidence required: Required future proof: chosen-provider integration and adversarial request tests, recovery/revocation races, expiry/retention checks, transport configuration evidence and redacted restriction/appeal audit examples.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: A trusted test adapter can prove early networking. Real external persistent characters require configured provider security; production values cannot be inferred from the documentation.

Related open decisions: Engine, platform and deployment candidates; Capacity, security and recovery budgets

Related documentation: Opaque authentication and limits; Recovery, appeals and support; Economic restrictions

BW-087 · Consistent backup and verified restore Not Started

Objective: Restore a recoverable economic checkpoint with reconciled character, item, wallet and receipt ownership.

Scope: Versioned backup manifests, artifact integrity, privileged restore, economic consistency and repeatable recovery evidence. Backup-file creation alone is insufficient.

Hard dependencies: BW-084

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. A backup identifies schema/content versions, all artifacts, matching lowercase SHA-256 digests and one economically consistent checkpoint.
  2. A restore rejects missing, corrupt or incompatible artifacts and cannot be marked completed before integrity and required receipt reconciliation pass.
  3. Restoring after committed and pending operations preserves canonical item/wallet ownership and reconciles receipts/outbox; currency cannot be rewound while purchased items remain unaccounted.
  4. Only an authorized scoped release operation performs restore; the audit links backup, action, reconciliation findings and completion time.
  5. An isolated recovery exercise measures the achieved recovery point/time against explicitly approved test objectives and retains blocking findings instead of claiming success from manifest validity.

Completion evidence required: Required future proof: an actual real-database restore into an isolated environment, corruption/incompatibility rejection, item/currency/receipt reconciliation and measured recovery results with approved objectives.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Require this before persistent external play, including a limited beginner playtest; valuable character state already needs recoverability long before production scale.

Related open decisions: Capacity, security and recovery budgets

Related documentation: Restore and rollback policy; Real-database failures

BW-039 · First complete beginner journey Not Started

Objective: Deliver the first complete level 1–10 player experience using a declared representative playable roster.

Scope: Equivalent faction-separated starter layouts for an initial Warrior/Bard/Mage roster, bound early kits, guidance, solo graduation and a safe explicit exit; full roster acceptance is BW-052.

Hard dependencies: BW-026, BW-027, BW-033, BW-035, BW-037, BW-080, BW-043, BW-030, BW-098

Recommended predecessors: BW-031, BW-032

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Fresh supported characters create, enter and complete the five learning stages with abilities at levels 1/3/5/7/9.
  2. Required quests and distinct guaranteed encounter XP reach level 10 before graduation without optional tasks or repeated grinding.
  3. Inventory/equipment, vendor, recovery, an optional party task and optional useful gather/craft practice are accessible within the compact journey; skipping group/profession activities never blocks the required solo path.
  4. Specialization preview spends nine banked points only after confirmation; tank/healer/damage graduation is solo-completable with friendly targets where needed.
  5. Death, shared targets, full bags and logout do not block progress; departure names a valid next objective/destination.
  6. The representative journey uses the selected 3D pixel-art camera and reviewed starter assets; scene occluders, projected targeting and independent UI remain usable, with final environment breadth and full-roster art certified later.

Completion evidence required: Future fresh-character end-to-end recordings, required-path XP report, role graduation tests and persisted interruption matrix.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: A complete beginner experience requires coherent rewards and recovery, but need not wait for all 37 kits, the bazaar, raids or multi-server hosting.

Related open decisions: Concrete world and starter records
