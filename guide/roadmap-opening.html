Phase objective and completion

Deliver an initial supported beginner and role slice with security, support and restore gates for external persistent testing.

0/16 complete · 0%

Phase status: Not Started. Complete only when all 16 epics below are Complete with verified evidence.

The representative beginner/role playtests use a common compatible build, and provider security plus actual restore evidence permit external persistent play. The starter slice integrates the reviewed 3D art workflow and camera.

Integrated completion milestone: First complete persistent opening playtest. Its full prerequisites and integration evidence must also pass; individual components do not prove their integration.

Hard dependencies are required for completion. Recommended predecessors are scheduling preferences. Other phases are not global start barriers. How to read this roadmap.

Epics in this phase

  1. BW-098 · Reusable 3D asset and animation production workflow Not Started

    Make consistent characters, equipment and starter scenery producible through a repeatable reviewed art workflow.

  2. BW-045 · Signature meters, marks and action sequences Not Started

    Represent bounded gameplay state derived from successful actions rather than speculative client events.

  3. BW-043 · Maintained songs and bounded support Not Started

    Make support measurable without stacking unlimited group amplification.

  4. BW-030 · Party and raid membership Not Started

    Let players form persistent-authority groups and inspect the capabilities they bring.

  5. BW-031 · Representative five-player role encounter Not Started

    Prove tanking, healing and damage cooperate in one readable server-owned encounter.

  6. BW-032 · Measured build and specialization proof Not Started

    Demonstrate that specialization and talent choices change decisions rather than only tooltip numbers.

  7. BW-033 · Durable quest objectives and rewards Not Started

    Track authenticated gameplay objectives and award each completion once.

  8. BW-034 · Profession enrollment and skill progression Not Started

    Keep profession learning independent of combat level and talent currencies.

  9. BW-035 · Shared resource nodes and skinning rights Not Started

    Let eligible players claim bounded resource outputs without duplication or stolen loot reservations.

  10. BW-037 · Quoted synchronous personal crafting Not Started

    Turn reserved materials into one exact useful output through a recoverable short craft.

  11. BW-080 · Safe local zone transitions and recall Not Started

    Move a character between declared locations within one authoritative world process without bypassing travel or recovery rules.

  12. BW-084 · Finite privileged support permissions and audit Not Started

    Provide traceable support and administrative actions with the documented default-deny realm scopes.

  13. BW-085 · Economic incident visibility, freezes and reversal Not Started

    Detect and contain economic corruption while preserving legitimate ownership and a complete audit history.

  14. BW-086 · Production identity recovery and security policy Not Started

    Complete provider-backed authentication, recovery, revocation and explicit endpoint/retention policy for external persistent play.

  15. BW-087 · Consistent backup and verified restore Not Started

    Restore a recoverable economic checkpoint with reconciled character, item, wallet and receipt ownership.

  16. BW-039 · First complete beginner journey Not Started

    Deliver the first complete level 1–10 player experience using a declared representative playable roster.

Open an epic for its full scope, dependencies, acceptance criteria, verification and ticket planning.
