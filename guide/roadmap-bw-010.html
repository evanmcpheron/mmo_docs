Phase: 2. First authoritative multiplayer interaction · Completion milestone: Two-client authoritative action

Objective and scope

Resolve one authoritative ability lifecycle with precise failure semantics.

Scope: Learned/build checks, reservations, GCD, cast/channel/instant timing, release revalidation and cooldown commitment; no full roster.

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    Acceptance verifies ability/build/equipment/life/control/target/resource permissions and reserves costs only for legal commands.

  2. AC 2

    Interrupted pre-release casts release reservations, preserve the started GCD and do not consume the ability cooldown under release-commit.

  3. AC 3

    Channels commit at start without refunds on cancellation; accepted instants commit cost and cooldown together.

  4. AC 4

    Movement cancellation, target loss, late input and duplicate commands yield declared outcomes with no extra cast or resource spend.

Verification and blockers

Completion evidence required: Executed instant/cast/channel fixtures covering valid release, rejection, interrupt and duplicates.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ticket planning

Use BW-010 as the parent epic. Link each future ticket to the AC links above and state the evidence it will produce. Split work into demonstrable behaviors; one criterion may require several tickets. Epic completion still requires every criterion, verified evidence and completed hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Timing and commitment rules precede expansion of effects and their replicated outcomes; animation never determines authoritative release.
