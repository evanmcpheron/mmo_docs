Phase objective and completion

Deliver shared economic and social branches, useful item operations and complete six-band profession content.

0/13 complete · 0%

Phase status: Not Started. Complete only when all 13 epics below are Complete with verified evidence.

Transactions and profession content pass their own contention and failure evidence; full economic relevance is evaluated in BW-072.

Integrated completion milestone: Transactional services and profession breadth. Its full prerequisites and integration evidence must also pass; individual components do not prove their integration.

Hard dependencies are required for completion. Recommended predecessors are scheduling preferences. Other phases are not global start barriers. How to read this roadmap.

Epics in this phase

  1. BW-054 · Personal storage and item mail Not Started

    Extend item access through the existing ownership and delivery rules.

  2. BW-055 · Version-confirmed direct trade Not Started

    Exchange permitted items and currency only when both players confirm the same offer.

  3. BW-056 · Faction-local fixed-price sell listings Not Started

    Support searchable offline selling through escrowed fixed-price listings.

  4. BW-057 · Reserved-funds buyer requests Not Started

    Match eligible sellers against funded purchase requests without creating a second wallet.

  5. BW-058 · Public and addressed crafting commissions Not Started

    Let customers safely commission exact outputs, including customer-bound challenge components.

  6. BW-059 · Versioned recrafting and enhancement services Not Started

    Change one existing item safely with exact customer-visible consequences.

  7. BW-060 · Cosmetic variants and neutral collections Not Started

    Deliver authored visual variants without adding combat or economic power.

  8. BW-061 · Portable profession field utilities Not Started

    Provide limited out-of-combat convenience while preserving public fallback services.

  9. BW-075 · Consumable effects and recipient treatment locks Not Started

    Apply owned consumables through bounded item-use and treatment rules.

  10. BW-076 · Conservative salvage and material conversion Not Started

    Transform or destroy owned materials without circular value or progression creation.

  11. BW-062 · Six-band profession and output certification Not Started

    Certify the complete profession roster and useful output families across progression.

  12. BW-063 · Guild membership and delegated permissions Not Started

    Let guilds manage members and ranks without granting authority they do not possess.

  13. BW-064 · Audited guild-bank allowances Not Started

    Apply shared asset guarantees and rolling member limits to guild holdings.

Open an epic for its full scope, dependencies, acceptance criteria, verification and ticket planning.
