Deliver shared economic and social branches, useful item operations and complete six-band profession content.

0/13 complete · 0%

Detailed epics

BW-054 · Personal storage and item mail Not Started

Objective: Extend item access through the existing ownership and delivery rules.

Scope: Personal bank movement and addressed item mail; no separate mail inventory or cross-faction economic bypass.

Hard dependencies: BW-022

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Bank moves atomically validate owner, stack/version, source and destination; full or stale destinations reject unchanged.
  2. Mail uses the same durable delivery and binding/faction eligibility as other transfers.
  3. Duplicate sends/claims and concurrent equip/trade/withdraw requests preserve a single owned instance.
  4. Opposing-faction alts cannot move prohibited currency/items through storage or mail.

Completion evidence required: Future bank withdrawal race, mail replay, full-destination and faction/binding transfer tests.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Shared binding eligibility; Mail delivery; Persistence races

BW-055 · Version-confirmed direct trade Not Started

Objective: Exchange permitted items and currency only when both players confirm the same offer.

Scope: Offer review/confirmation, range/combat/faction checks, atomic settlement and audit receipt.

Hard dependencies: BW-022

Recommended predecessors: BW-029

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Any offer edit invalidates both confirmations and the displayed immutable offer version changes.
  2. Commit rechecks ownership, binding, balance, item versions, range, faction and combat eligibility.
  3. Leaving range or disconnecting before commit consumes nothing; postcommit retries return the same result.
  4. Concurrent asset use cannot produce a partial trade, negative balance or duplicated instance.

Completion evidence required: Future paired-client trade demonstration and real-store offer-change, disconnect and conflicting-asset tests.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Direct trade; Shared item eligibility

BW-056 · Faction-local fixed-price sell listings Not Started

Objective: Support searchable offline selling through escrowed fixed-price listings.

Scope: Listing admission, browse/search, explicit partial fills, fees, expiry/cancel and durable settlement; buyer requests are BW-057.

Hard dependencies: BW-022

Recommended predecessors: BW-029, BW-055

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Only eligible owned items enter escrow before a realm/faction-local listing appears.
  2. Quotes show unit price, quantity, total and versioned fee policy; listing acceptance charges the nonrefundable fee once.
  3. Concurrent last-item purchases, cancellation and expiry yield one valid ownership/currency outcome.
  4. Partial commodity fills require buyer consent and preserve remaining stock; full-bag/retry delivery never repeats settlement.

Completion evidence required: Future market race tests, fee arithmetic/receipt evidence and offline-seller delivery demonstration.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Listing settlement reuses proven asset invariants. A direct-trade UI is useful prior evidence, but is not a technical prerequisite.

Related documentation: Bazaar model; Fees

BW-057 · Reserved-funds buyer requests Not Started

Objective: Match eligible sellers against funded purchase requests without creating a second wallet.

Scope: Wallet reservations, exact eligible fills, unfilled release and fill/cancel races.

Hard dependencies: BW-056

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Activation reserves full quantity times unit price in the existing wallet; spendable balance excludes all held funds.
  2. Each accepted fill consumes only its matching reservation, pays the seller less fees and creates delivery once.
  3. Cancellation/expiry releases only unfilled funds without crediting new currency.
  4. Concurrent fills, unrelated wallet debits and repeated cancellation preserve nonnegative balances and a single receipt.

Completion evidence required: Future real-database wallet-reservation concurrency and reconciliation results across partial fill/cancel/expiry.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Buyer-request invariant

BW-058 · Public and addressed crafting commissions Not Started

Objective: Let customers safely commission exact outputs, including customer-bound challenge components.

Scope: Public fixed-tip and private orders, exact quotes, atomic renewable claims, escrow, expiry and settlement; marketplace is independent.

Hard dependencies: BW-022, BW-037

Recommended predecessors: BW-038

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. The accepted order fixes blueprint/configuration, result grade, supplied/missing inputs, recipient, fee and expiry.
  2. Two simultaneous claimants produce one bounded renewable lease; uncommitted expiry/cancellation returns customer escrow.
  3. Bound cores remain customer-owned and output is created directly for the customer; crafters gain no copy, appearance or salvage rights.
  4. Commit/cancel/crash/retry races consume inputs, move commission and award skill exactly once, including full-bag delivery.

Completion evidence required: Future real-store lease and settlement races, bound-core provenance audit and 100-retry recovery test results.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Commissions depend on crafting and asset settlement, not bazaar listings; they can proceed independently once those shared guarantees exist.

Related documentation: Customer commissions; Failure cases

BW-059 · Versioned recrafting and enhancement services Not Started

Objective: Change one existing item safely with exact customer-visible consequences.

Scope: Grade upgrades, new-band recrafts, sockets/gems/enchant replacement, repairs and instrument configuration through protected service contracts.

Hard dependencies: BW-026, BW-038, BW-058

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Accepted work locks one exact instance/version against equip, trade, market and mail until resolution.
  2. Recraft preserves identity/history while incrementing version; new-band upgrades require declared materials and new equip level.
  3. Preview identifies replaced/destroyed enhancements and binding changes; missing confirmation or incompatibility rejects before consumption.
  4. Reserved enhancement budgets and the two major behavior-effect limit hold after repeated replacement and retries.

Completion evidence required: Future recraft/conflicting-use races, enhancement-budget tests and before/after lineage plus customer preview evidence.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related open decisions: Prices, sources and conversion graph

Related documentation: Recrafting; Enhancements; Service contracts

BW-060 · Cosmetic variants and neutral collections Not Started

Objective: Deliver authored visual variants without adding combat or economic power.

Scope: Inscription presentation glyphs, compatible visual replacements and account cosmetic unlock records.

Hard dependencies: BW-022, BW-037

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Only compatible authored variants can be selected; absent assets create no fabricated selectable option.
  2. Variant changes preserve collision, targeting, attack timing, item binding and combat budgets.
  3. Account-wide cosmetic access cannot transfer bound assets or bridge opposing faction economies.
  4. Crafted cosmetic output and unlock retries create only the permitted item/collection result.

Completion evidence required: Future visual compatibility, no-stat/no-collision and cross-faction collection tests with authored asset references.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Cosmetic boundaries; Inscription; Collection contract

BW-061 · Portable profession field utilities Not Started

Objective: Provide limited out-of-combat convenience while preserving public fallback services.

Scope: Crafted repair/preparation/station objects, placement, owner limits and preparation categories; no territorial construction.

Hard dependencies: BW-014, BW-037, BW-026

Recommended predecessors: BW-041

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Each field site permits three utility objects and one active preparation buff per category after five out-of-combat seconds.
  2. Utilities cannot block navigation, create combat collision, occupy objectives or exceed owner limits.
  3. Effects remain equal/weaker substitutes within category caps and both factions have equivalent functionality.
  4. A missing crafter or expired field object never prevents ordinary repair or instance exit.

Completion evidence required: Future placement/limit/category tests and multiplayer demonstration against public full-function station fallbacks.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Portable repair convenience is assessed against the already working public vendor/repair fallback; combat deployables remain a recommended reuse opportunity rather than a required implementation.

Related documentation: Field utility policy; Engineering

BW-075 · Consumable effects and recipient treatment locks Not Started

Objective: Apply owned consumables through bounded item-use and treatment rules.

Scope: Alchemy recovery/defense effects, food preparation, shared cooldown categories and First Aid channels/recipient locks; manufacturing outputs remain with crafting.

Hard dependencies: BW-017, BW-021

Recommended predecessors: BW-037

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Each admitted item-use timing policy commits one owned item and one permitted effect; duplicate requests and concurrent trade/equip use cannot create free or repeated output.
  2. First Aid follows the authored six-second interruptible channel and 60-second recipient treatment lock; damage interruption, concurrent healers and retries cannot bypass the lock or duplicate ticks.
  3. Food/preparation and consumable categories obey their authored caps and fixed level-band effects; old low-band supplies do not scale from a high-level consumer’s maximum resources.
  4. Mode restrictions, invalid targets and unavailable items produce explicit rejection; UI shows timing, shared cooldown and treatment availability before use.

Completion evidence required: Executed consumption, interruption, category and concurrent-treatment fixtures with item/receipt and effective-output traces; reviewed timing bindings for item-use policies.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Crafting a numerical item record does not prove that consuming it obeys channel, category and recipient restrictions. Validate item use separately before certifying all profession outputs.

Related open decisions: Concrete item-use timing bindings

Related documentation: First Aid; Cooking; Alchemy; Consumable/PvP restrictions

BW-076 · Conservative salvage and material conversion Not Started

Objective: Transform or destroy owned materials without circular value or progression creation.

Scope: Declared prospecting/refining/conversion and salvage paths, atomic consumption/output and source eligibility; authoring the full conversion inventory belongs to profession certification.

Hard dependencies: BW-022

Recommended predecessors: BW-026, BW-037

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. A declared conversion atomically consumes the exact eligible owned inputs and creates only its authorized output once, including retry, restart and full-bag delivery.
  2. Salvage destroys the source and returns at most 25% of eligible original common materials, zero rare/bound progression cores, zero skill XP and zero gold.
  3. Conflicting recraft/equip/trade/salvage requests yield one permitted item state; reservations or binding cannot be laundered through conversion.
  4. Admission rejects a recipe/vendor conversion graph with an unlimited guaranteed material, currency or progression loop, and retains the failing cycle evidence.

Completion evidence required: Real-database concurrent conversion/salvage fixtures and graph validation over declared recipes/vendor values, with loss/conservation traces.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: A meaningful material-loss/concurrency path can be certified on a small fixture set before the complete recipe catalog; final content must rerun the graph gate.

Related documentation: Salvage and vendor constraints; Profession safeguards; Prospecting

BW-062 · Six-band profession and output certification Not Started

Objective: Certify the complete profession roster and useful output families across progression.

Scope: All 16 professions, 240 mastery nodes and six material bands; expand the 257 representative recipes into the approved release inventory.

Hard dependencies: BW-035, BW-036, BW-059, BW-060, BW-061, BW-075, BW-076

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Every profession has validated bands 1–300, legal mastery routes and accessible basic training/tools on both factions.
  2. Manufacturing bands provide useful entry, repeat-demand, upgrade and service/tool outputs with exact numerical/binding contracts.
  3. Consumables, bags, tools, shields, instruments, gems, enchants and utility items obey their actual slot/category budgets.
  4. Rare/earned cores are excluded from material-saving effects; gathering, prospecting, salvage and conversion graphs cannot amplify materials or skill indefinitely.
  5. Non-crafters can obtain permitted combat benefits through trade or commissions; no profession grants private raid power.
  6. All 240 mastery nodes have explicit bounded runtime behavior and executed inclusion/exclusion evidence for their product family; training and authored acquisition routes populate retained recipe knowledge rather than making every recipe implicitly known.

Completion evidence required: Future approved output manifest, all-band/branch validation, conversion-cycle tests and executed crafting/consumption fixtures.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related open decisions: Runtime bindings and complete release inventory; Prices, sources and conversion graph; Measured balance, usability and content limits; Concrete item-use timing bindings

Related documentation: Profession roster; Band coverage; Recipe readiness; Recipe catalog; Alchemy; Blacksmithing; Cooking; Enchanting; Engineering; First Aid; Fishing; Forestry; Herbalism; Inscription; Jewelcrafting; Leatherworking; Mining; Skinning; Tailoring; Woodworking

BW-063 · Guild membership and delegated permissions Not Started

Objective: Let guilds manage members and ranks without granting authority they do not possess.

Scope: Guild records, invitations, rank policy, typed membership commands and guild communication; bank assets are BW-064.

Hard dependencies: BW-029

Recommended predecessors: BW-030

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Pending invitation expiry/accept/decline/revoke use authoritative time and one terminal result.
  2. Commands enforce exact payload/rank IDs and the caller cannot grant permissions it lacks.
  3. Membership/rank edits invalidate stale views and cannot bypass encounter or invitation constraints.
  4. Guild chat obeys membership, rate limits, mute/block/report and moderation history.

Completion evidence required: Future invitation/rank escalation tests, concurrent membership changes and moderated guild-chat demonstration.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Party invitations offer useful prior lifecycle evidence, but guild membership/rank authorization can be verified independently with authoritative encounter-lock context. Party/raid frames and formation are not guild prerequisites.

Related documentation: Guild policy

BW-064 · Audited guild-bank allowances Not Started

Objective: Apply shared asset guarantees and rolling member limits to guild holdings.

Scope: Deposit, item/currency withdrawal, per-rank policies, allowance reservations and immutable audit.

Hard dependencies: BW-022, BW-063

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Zero limits forbid the respective withdrawal kind and configured windows are positive.
  2. Allowance check/reservation, ownership transfer and audit commit together; concurrent withdrawals cannot exceed the limit.
  3. Only committed nonreversed outgoing units/currency count; retries, deposits and rank changes cannot refill allowance.
  4. Unauthorized/stale/conflicting withdrawals reject without lost assets or partial allowance consumption.

Completion evidence required: Future real-store simultaneous withdrawal/rank-change tests and replayable allowance/audit reconciliation.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Guild bank contract
