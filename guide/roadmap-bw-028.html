Phase: 2. First authoritative multiplayer interaction · Completion milestone: Two-client authoritative action

Objective and scope

Expose enough accessible interaction to test actual gameplay decisions early.

Scope: Rebindable movement/target/cast input, action bar, HUD, queue/cancel, telegraphs, status/resource/cast/rejection feedback and presentation hooks; final asset production is separate. Projected world picking, independent UI composition and occluder-aware cues integrate with the active render view; placeholder art can serve the first technical proof.

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    A player can target, preview/confirm/cancel ground intent, queue one eligible action within the documented window and see rejection reasons.

  2. AC 2

    HUD/action bars show authoritative resources, GCD/cast/cooldown and target state; predicted feedback corrects after rejection.

  3. AC 3

    Friendly-frame targeting has precedence and actionable hazards remain distinguishable without relying only on color.

  4. AC 4

    Keyboard bindings, text/UI scale and reduced visual effects can be exercised independently of world display settings; model poses and camera presentation never determine aim, collision or release.

  5. AC 5

    Pointer ground previews and selection map through actual viewport scaling, letterbox offsets and camera projection to the intended server floor/position; UI capture and invalid/occluded geometry have deterministic rejection/cancel behavior.

Verification and blockers

Completion evidence required: Keyboard/controller-if-supported interaction recordings and accessibility checklist with executed correction/rejection cases.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.
