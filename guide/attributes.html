Attributes & formulas

Mechanics, governing rules, edge cases and acceptance criteria.

The formulas on this page are original prototype rules. They resolve the missing mathematical foundation in v1. They have arithmetic tests, but have not been balance-tested in a playable MMORPG. Tune constants through versioned data rather than changing individual formulas casually.

Units and numerical precision

One world unit is one authored tile width. A 32-pixel tile is a rendering choice, not a combat constant. Distances are measured between server-owned foot anchors, adjusted by the declared collision radii. Movement is continuous; sprite orientation is quantized to eight directions only for display.

A proposed simulation rate is 30 ticks per second. Durations become ceil(seconds × tick_rate) ticks. Keep resource and intermediate combat values at fixed-point thousandths, carry fractions, and round health changes once at final resolution. Never allow client floating-point rounding to authorize damage, healing or item counts.

Primary attributes

Attribute Mechanical purpose
Strength Primary power for Warrior and martial Paladin specializations
Agility Primary power for Rogue, Ranger, martial Bard, martial Monk and physical Druid specializations
Intellect Primary power for spellcasters and all healer specializations
Stamina Maximum health
Spirit Resource recovery; does not grant an independent damage multiplier

For each attribute a:

base_a = 10 + growth_a × (effective_level − 1)

nonracial_a = max(0, (base_a + gear_a + flat_buff_a) × (1 + capped_percent_a))

effective_a = max(1, nonracial_a + fixed_racial_offset_a)

Primary growth is 3, stamina growth is 2, and all other growth is 1. No specialization uses Stamina or Spirit as its primary attack attribute. A healer Paladin uses Intellect; its shared weapon effects resolve through that specialization's power conversion rather than forcing a second Strength equipment set.

Racial offsets are applied last. Temporary percentage buffs cannot amplify them. Downscaling recomputes the base from effective level instead of multiplying the entire original stat sheet.

Derived values

Value Prototype formula

Maximum health (80 + 32L + 10 × stamina) × role_health_multiplier

Other health multiplier 1.00
Maximum mana 100 + 20L + 8 × intellect
Mana recovery in combat 0.005 × max_mana + 0.02 × spirit per second
Mana recovery out of combat 4 × combat recovery after six seconds without hostile participation
Energy / Focus 100 maximum; 10 + min(2, 0.01 × spirit) per second
Rage 100 maximum; active generators grant 8 on a successful eligible action; no passive in-combat recovery
Cadence (DPS Bard) 100 maximum; 8 per second; eligible generators can add 8
Haste-sensitive recovery Energy/Focus/Cadence recovery × (1 + haste); mana recovery and Rage are not haste-scaled

Healer specializations use Mana as the sustained resource. Minstrel adds Harmony as its secondary meter; Lifewalker adds Chi. Their DPS specializations keep their non-mana systems. This corrects the earlier risk of healers having unlimited throughput from free, quickly regenerating energy.

Secondary meters—charges, marks, Guard, Bloom, Harmony, Bond and similar mechanics—have separate class-page policies. They are not five separate full-size resource bars on every character. Where class and specialization describe the same meter with different names, use one stored resource and one UI meter, not two competing versions.

Damage and weapon normalization

normalized_weapon_damage = 2.0 × equipped_weapon_DPS

raw_damage = flat_amount + power_coefficient × P + weapon_coefficient × normalized_weapon_damage

Spell-only attacks use a weapon coefficient of zero. A weapon attack may use both terms. For dual wield, item budgets split the same total allowed weapon DPS across the two hands; do not add two full two-handed weapon budgets. Auto-attacks use actual weapon speed: swing_damage = weapon_DPS × swing_seconds. An attack-speed increase changes frequency, not the stored weapon DPS.

A default martial generator starts at 0.55P + 0.65 × normalized_weapon_damage. A normal cast-time spell starts at 1.10P. These are individual attack profiles, not complete rotation DPS estimates. Multi-hit actions split one authored total across hits; they do not repeat the full coefficient per visual strike.

Physical, fire, frost, arcane, radiant, decay and sonic are descriptive damage schools. Physical uses Armor. Other schools use Ward in the first implementation. Do not add six separate mandatory resistance equipment sets. Bleeds are physical and use Armor unless an explicit tested ability says otherwise. Player-accessible unmitigated “true damage” is excluded from the baseline.

Critical strikes and haste

Let K(L) = 10 + 2L.

critical_chance = min(0.35, 0.05 + 0.30 × critical_rating / (critical_rating + K))

haste = min(0.30, 0.30 × haste_rating / (haste_rating + K))

mastery_bonus = min(0.20, 0.20 × mastery_rating / (mastery_rating + K))

Direct damage and direct healing can critically strike for 1.50× in PvE and 1.25× in PvP. Periodic effects and shields do not critically strike by default. Mastery applies only to the specialization's declared output family, not globally to every effect, resource gain, crowd-control duration and pet component at once.

effective_cast_time = base_cast_time / (1 + haste)

effective_GCD = max(1.0 seconds, 1.25 seconds / (1 + haste))

Haste does not reduce every cooldown or extend every status. A cooldown requires an explicit haste_scaled flag. Damage-over-time duration and tick count remain fixed in the baseline; adding haste to them requires a deliberate budget change, not accidental extra ticks.

No random accuracy or hit-rating tax is included initially. A valid target-locked attack hits unless blocked by a declared defense, immunity or targeting failure. Skillshots use server geometry. Facing, line of sight and positioning provide counterplay without a second unexplained random-miss layer.

Armor, Ward and mitigation

K_defense = 100 + 25 × attacker_effective_level

armor_reduction = min(0.60, armor / (armor + K_defense))

ward_reduction = min(0.60, ward / (ward + K_defense))

Use only the applicable defense. Same-category defensive effects select the strongest; independently allowed reductions multiply. Combined reduction before shields is capped at 80%, excluding clearly identified temporary encounter immunities.

For example, 30% Armor reduction and a 20% defensive effect produce 1 − (0.70 × 0.80) = 44% reduction, not 50%. A separate 10% permitted reduction produces 49.6%, not 60%.

Active blocking protects a declared frontal arc and attack category. The starter block profile reduces eligible frontal physical damage by 50% for four seconds with an eight-second cooldown. It does not randomly block spells behind the tank. Active parry reduces the next eligible direct physical hit by 50% within two seconds; a talent must explicitly extend its attack categories.

Healing and absorption

raw_healing = flat_amount + healing_coefficient × P

Healer-role abilities use their full authored coefficient. Shared off-role heals normally use 0.35× that healer-equivalent coefficient. A specialization may intentionally budget stronger self-recovery, but cannot receive full healer throughput and full DPS simultaneously without a tradeoff.

effective_healing = min(missing_health, healing_after_modifiers)

Only effective healing generates ordinary healing threat or healing-derived resources. Overhealing cannot be farmed on healthy allies. Absorbs generate prevention credit when consumed, not merely when applied. Per-source shields normally cap at 30% of recipient maximum health; all ordinary shield capacity on one recipient caps at 50%. Reapplication follows the status stacking rules.

Healing critical rolls are independent per direct target. Area heals sort by missing-health percentage, then entity ID, within eligible recipients and target limits. This policy is server-side and visible in the tooltip.

Resolution order

Validate → select targets → compute base amount → apply source modifiers → roll eligible critical → apply recipient mitigation → consume recipient absorbs → transfer permitted residual damage → apply local delayed-damage mechanics → subtract health → resolve death → emit bounded triggered events.

Transferred damage is already mitigated. It cannot be reduced by the protector's Armor a second time, redirected again, or generate resources from another transfer. A protector's ordinary absorb may consume it, but immunity cannot turn a transfer into a free cancellation: an ineligible immune protector causes the transfer to fail and leaves damage on the original target.

Delayed damage is a debt, not prevention. It cannot be staggered again or inherit Armor again. Only an explicit debt-clearing ability can remove it. Leaving combat, disconnecting, changing form or loading a build does not erase the ledger.

Worked arithmetic

A level-60 caster with 180 primary attribute from gear has primary = 10 + 3×59 + 180 = 367, so P = 750.5. A 1.10P spell produces 825.55 raw damage. Against 800 Ward at attacker level 60, reduction is 800 / (800 + 1600) = 1/3. Noncritical damage is 550.37 before final rounding. A 1.5× critical produces 825.55 after Ward.

The same caster with a +2 racial Intellect offset has P = 753.5; the difference is approximately 0.40% before breakpoints and rounding. At level 1 without gear, P = 38; +2 primary adds 3 power, or approximately 7.89%. This produces the intended early-game distinction and much smaller late-game influence without a racial multiplier.

A tank with 268 Stamina at level 60 has (80 + 1920 + 2680) × 1.30 = 6,084 maximum health. The non-tank equivalent is 4,680. A +2 Stamina racial adds 20 health before the tank-role multiplier; the racial addition itself is not percentage-amplified in attribute calculation, although derived health naturally uses the role formula.

Balance measurements, not promises

Initial targets: ordinary equal-level solo enemies should usually take 8–15 seconds, healer resource pressure should be evident over sustained encounters, and avoidable hazards should reward movement rather than require racial advantages. These targets need actual class rotations, enemy definitions, gear distributions and network conditions. The included arithmetic checks cannot establish time-to-kill, build parity, faction parity or an MMO population capacity.

Before specialization selection

The class record defines the level-1 primary attribute. Warrior and Paladin start with Strength; Bard, Rogue, Ranger and Monk with Agility; Cleric, Mage, Necromancer, Druid, Shaman and Warlock with Intellect. At level 10, the selected specialization recalculates primary-stat growth without granting additional attribute points or a second simultaneous primary bonus.
