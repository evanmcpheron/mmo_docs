Phase objective and completion

Bind the intended class/specification/talent inventory and certify all legal beginner characters.

0/6 complete · 0%

Phase status: Not Started. Complete only when all 6 epics below are Complete with verified evidence.

Every cohort and early legal character journey is certified; isolated cohort passes do not replace the all-roster beginner gate.

Integrated completion milestone: Full-roster opening and kit certification. Its full prerequisites and integration evidence must also pass; individual components do not prove their integration.

Hard dependencies are required for completion. Recommended predecessors are scheduling preferences. Other phases are not global start barriers. How to read this roadmap.

Detailed epics

BW-047 · Martial and defensive kit certification Not Started

Objective: Certify the position, weapon, defense and sequence based specialization cohort.

Scope: Warrior/Rogue/Ranger/Monk/Paladin shared kits; their martial specs except Beastmaster/Lifewalker; Skald specialization. Talents are certified in BW-051. Completion requires every documented cohort ability, not a selected enabled subset.

Hard dependencies: BW-022, BW-043, BW-044, BW-041, BW-095, BW-096

Recommended predecessors: BW-032

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Every documented cohort ability has bound numerical, secondary-effect and resource behavior at its documented unlock level.
  2. Each specialization retains its stated weakness, mandatory role services and two observable contrasting routes.
  3. Stealth, active defense, movement follow-ups and weapon budgets reject invalid states without resets or free generators.
  4. Every enabled ability passes legal, invalid-target and reconnect/reconstruction fixtures, with content IDs recorded.

Completion evidence required: Future cohort manifest and executed per-ability fixture report covering Warrior, Rogue, Marksman, Pathfinder, Ironbody, Windfist, Oathkeeper, Crusader and Skald.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related open decisions: Runtime bindings and complete release inventory

Related documentation: Martial classes; Rogue; Ranger; Monk; Paladin; Bard

BW-048 · Offensive caster kit certification Not Started

Objective: Certify charge, affliction, elemental and paid-contract offensive loops.

Scope: Mage/Necromancer/Shaman/Warlock shared kits; Mage specs, Plaguebringer, Soulreaper, Stormcaller, Hellcaller, Pactbinder, Virtuoso and Inquisitor. Completion requires every documented cohort ability, not a selected enabled subset.

Hard dependencies: BW-046, BW-040, BW-041, BW-080, BW-095, BW-096

Recommended predecessors: BW-032

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Every documented cohort ability binds secondary effects and resource hooks rather than executing free-text intent.
  2. Charges, phase/element sequences, marks and paid clauses follow the single canonical resource policies.
  3. Control-immune enemies retain the explicitly budgeted damage/resource alternative; self-costs remain nonlethal within declared limits.
  4. Legal, invalid-target and reconnect fixtures pass for each enabled ability at its actual unlock.

Completion evidence required: Future cohort enablement manifest, resource traces and complete executed ability-fixture report.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related open decisions: Runtime bindings and complete release inventory

Related documentation: Mage; Necromancer; Shaman; Warlock; Virtuoso; Inquisitor; Ability catalog

BW-049 · Healing and prevention kit certification Not Started

Objective: Certify every healing specialization with sustainable resources and complete role services.

Scope: Cleric/Bard shared kits; High Priest, Oracle, Justicar, Minstrel, Grovekeeper, Spiritmender and Lifewalker. Completion requires every documented cohort ability, not a selected enabled subset.

Hard dependencies: BW-043, BW-046, BW-041, BW-042, BW-095, BW-096

Recommended predecessors: BW-032

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Every healer uses Mana plus only its declared signature state and has affordable direct/group recovery, cleanse and out-of-combat revival.
  2. Effective healing/consumed prevention qualify generators; overhealing and unused shields cannot farm state.
  3. Links, chains, wards, seeds and melee-assisted healing obey recipient caps, eligibility and unique-target policies.
  4. Every documented cohort ability has bound behavior and passes legal, invalid-target and reconstruction fixtures; both factions have common-class healer coverage.

Completion evidence required: Future all-healer service matrix, sustained encounter traces and executed ability-fixture results.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related open decisions: Runtime bindings and complete release inventory

Related documentation: Healing resources; Cleric; Bard; Druid; Shaman; Monk

BW-050 · Summon, form and deployment kit certification Not Started

Objective: Certify specializations whose identity depends on controlled entities or form state.

Scope: Druid shared kit; Gravecaller, Beastmaster, Wildguard, Feral, Starcaller, Totemist and Demonologist. Completion requires every documented cohort ability, not a selected enabled subset.

Hard dependencies: BW-040, BW-041, BW-042, BW-095, BW-096

Recommended predecessors: BW-032

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. All documented cohort abilities have bound execution/resource behavior and pass legal, invalid-target and reconstruction fixtures.
  2. One elite versus multiple servants shares one output budget; owner/pet coordination cannot gain from unattended or self-generated loops.
  3. Form and deployment choices retain documented movement, defense and capacity tradeoffs.
  4. Pet paths, recall, replacement, scope loss and encounter resets leave no unauthorized surviving entities.

Completion evidence required: Future cohort manifest, path/capacity/transition traces and executed per-ability fixture report.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related open decisions: Runtime bindings and complete release inventory

Related documentation: Druid; Gravecaller; Beastmaster; Totemist; Demonologist

BW-051 · Complete talent binding certification Not Started

Objective: Certify the documented class and specialization trees against their actual runtime kits.

Scope: All 49 trees and 1,764 catalog nodes, including inherited bespoke rules and shared handling contracts; balance evaluation is BW-073.

Hard dependencies: BW-047, BW-048, BW-049, BW-050

Recommended predecessors: BW-032

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. All 1,764 documented nodes map to explicit supported modifiers/triggers, including the 882 inherited bespoke bindings; no required unresolved behavior remains disabled or is executed as free text.
  2. Each behavior-changing node has an executed positive and exclusion fixture against referenced learned abilities.
  3. All levels admit legal spending within the separate 59/59 budgets, tier gates, exclusive groups and prerequisite graph.
  4. Refunds and loadout application revalidate the whole build and preserve cooldown/debt, state caps and invalid-summon cleanup.

Completion evidence required: Future node-to-handler readiness manifest, all-tree capacity results and actual positive/exclusion fixture execution report.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Bulk binding follows demonstrated loops and representative build proof; catalog size and schema validity cannot certify meaningful runtime choices.

Related open decisions: Runtime bindings and complete release inventory

Related documentation: Talent execution; Tree rules; Readiness; Talent catalog

BW-052 · Every-roster beginner certification Not Started

Objective: Prove the complete opening journey for every documented legal character choice.

Scope: All eleven races, both Human allegiances, twelve classes, level-1–10 ability/talent bindings and specialization graduations; full higher-level kit/tree certification remains separate.

Hard dependencies: BW-039, BW-040, BW-041, BW-042, BW-044, BW-046, BW-096

Recommended predecessors: BW-047, BW-048, BW-049, BW-050, BW-051

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Every legal race/faction/class combination creates the reviewed character with only authored legal appearance options.
  2. All 37 specializations graduate alone with functioning starting kits and an intelligible role lesson.
  3. Both factions meet required-path XP, equipment, vendor/recovery and optional profession learning goals.
  4. Fresh-player evaluation covers naming failure, help revisit, death, full bags, shared targets and resume without progression dead ends.
  5. Every early spendable point has legal bound choices; level-54 capstones and later abilities are not required to verify the beginner experience.

Completion evidence required: Future coverage matrix, all-specialization graduation fixtures and representative fresh-player usability recordings with findings resolved.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Full-roster starter verification needs early-kit bindings, not every high-level ability or capstone. Later cohort certification reuses these fixtures without blocking early player feedback.

Related open decisions: Concrete world and starter records; Measured balance, usability and content limits

Related documentation: Beginner testing ground; Roster restrictions; Appearance/naming
