Phase objective and completion

Bind the intended class/specification/talent inventory and certify all legal beginner characters.

0/6 complete · 0%

Phase status: Not Started. Complete only when all 6 epics below are Complete with verified evidence.

Every cohort and early legal character journey is certified; isolated cohort passes do not replace the all-roster beginner gate.

Integrated completion milestone: Full-roster opening and kit certification. Its full prerequisites and integration evidence must also pass; individual components do not prove their integration.

Hard dependencies are required for completion. Recommended predecessors are scheduling preferences. Other phases are not global start barriers. How to read this roadmap.

Epics in this phase

  1. BW-047 · Martial and defensive kit certification Not Started

    Certify the position, weapon, defense and sequence based specialization cohort.

  2. BW-048 · Offensive caster kit certification Not Started

    Certify charge, affliction, elemental and paid-contract offensive loops.

  3. BW-049 · Healing and prevention kit certification Not Started

    Certify every healing specialization with sustainable resources and complete role services.

  4. BW-050 · Summon, form and deployment kit certification Not Started

    Certify specializations whose identity depends on controlled entities or form state.

  5. BW-051 · Complete talent binding certification Not Started

    Certify the documented class and specialization trees against their actual runtime kits.

  6. BW-052 · Every-roster beginner certification Not Started

    Prove the complete opening journey for every documented legal character choice.

Open an epic for its full scope, dependencies, acceptance criteria, verification and ticket planning.
