Phase: 3. Recoverable combat and personal state · Completion milestone: Recoverable persistent character

Objective and scope

Make personal assets durable and recoverable under contention and full bags.

Scope: Container placement, stack/split/move/consume operations, currency ledger, asset binding and pending reward delivery; exchanges use this common boundary later.

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    Concurrent asset changes validate owner/container/item revisions and cannot duplicate, overfill or produce negative stacks/currency.

  2. AC 2

    Bound, reserved or escrowed assets cannot be transferred through personal inventory operations.

  3. AC 3

    Full bags produce a durable claimable delivery; retry, restart and expiry of a loot corpse never destroy an awarded item.

  4. AC 4

    Every committed asset change has an attributable receipt/ledger entry and reconnect reads the same ownership and balance.

Verification and blockers

Completion evidence required: Inventory/wallet contention, restart and full-bag claim tests with reconciled ledgers.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ticket planning

Use BW-021 as the parent epic. Link each future ticket to the AC links above and state the evidence it will produce. Split work into demonstrable behaviors; one criterion may require several tickets. Epic completion still requires every criterion, verified evidence and completed hard prerequisites.
