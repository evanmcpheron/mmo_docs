Phase: 9. Measured capacity and production acceptance · Completion milestone: Production-ready full game

Objective and scope

Demonstrate the intended game as connected player journeys and resolve release-blocking interaction and balance findings.

Scope: Release-candidate integration across characters, level 1–60, enabled roster, groups/PvP, world travel, economy/professions, social systems and presentation. This is evidence of integration beyond individual epic completion.

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    The release candidate passes a versioned journey matrix spanning both factions, creation through cap, role access, starter departure, group content, professions/economy, social operations and enabled PvP modes.

  2. AC 2

    All 330 documented abilities, all 1,764 talent nodes and the approved full profession/world inventory have current binding and execution evidence; legal/invalid/reconstruction and positive/exclusion fixtures cover required behavior, and no required roster entry remains silently disabled.

  3. AC 3

    Equal-budget build/role/faction and profession-relevance reports meet documented evaluation standards, record measured deviations and resolve or explicitly withhold release on approved blocking findings.

  4. AC 4

    Cross-system failure cases cover concurrent rewards/trades, full-bag delivery, death/logout/reconnect, transition during economic activity, guild permissions and encounter reset without duplicated assets or cleared debt.

  5. AC 5

    Human usability/accessibility sessions cover keyboard talent editing, friendly-frame healing, expensive craft confirmation, small-window reflow and crowded combat, with blocking findings resolved. The release render/asset manifest and supported quality configurations match the approved 3D pixel-art comparisons, with camera/picking, privacy, water/transparency and atmosphere regressions included.

Verification and blockers

Completion evidence required: Required future proof: release-candidate journey matrix, executed integration/fixture reports, balance/profession studies, accessibility sessions and a reviewed issue disposition tied to exact artifacts.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ticket planning

Before creating or revising tickets, apply the mandatory reuse assessment. Inspect existing owners and evidence, then map every criterion to the additional work and verification required.

Use BW-092 as the parent epic. Link each future ticket to the AC links above and state the evidence it will produce. Split work into demonstrable behaviors; one criterion may require several tickets. Epic completion still requires every criterion, verified evidence and completed hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Completed subsystem epics do not prove their interaction. Reserve a distinct integration gate, and do not substitute data counts or an unexplained subjective balance claim for measured evidence.

Related open decisions: Runtime bindings and complete release inventory; Measured balance, usability and content limits
