Executive summary

Unreal Engine 5 native C++ plan · no implementation credit

Briarwake is a new server-authoritative dedicated-server MMORPG. This is a design and roadmap review, not runtime certification. All implementation starts at zero. Exact current epic/ticket totals and progress are derived below and on the dashboard.

Audit coverage and method

Reviewed the complete registry, all feature families, canonical definitions and catalogs against C++ ownership, Unreal lifetimes/targets, supplied-plugin uncertainty, dedicated-server behavior, dependency/testability, persistent ownership, security, content production and operations. Engine-independent gameplay responsibilities and IDs remain valid. Native build, plugin, movement/replication and rendering instructions now define fresh future verification.

Epic documentation reviewed on 20 October 2026 establishes candidate mechanisms; no installed engine, platform, plugin API or implementation location is assumed. Foundation and networking define the technical boundaries. The external visual references describe appearance goals, not an implementation to reproduce.

Cross-system findings and dispositions

RA-001 · Content authoring has one explicit delivery owner

High · Addressed in plan

BW-095 owns canonical content production/import; BW-098 owns render assets. Runtime admission remains BW-002. A schema or editor alone does not certify enabled behavior.

RA-002 · Acceptance outcomes have explicit hard prerequisites

Medium · Addressed in plan

Equipment/build, quest/content and recovery prerequisites remain explicit. Native C++ setup now precedes executable ownership tests; plugin verification precedes connectivity and movement. Recommendations remain separate from mandatory edges.

RA-003 · Initial recipient tests do not certify future state

Medium · Addressed in plan

BW-007/BW-013 test only enabled state. Every later state producer extends privacy and reconstruction tests before admission; hidden state must not be replicated for client-side hiding.

RA-004 · Economic reconciliation remains observable

Medium · Addressed in plan

BW-085 requires reconciliation, visibility, freeze and reversal evidence. Server transactions, idempotency and durable acknowledgments remain canonical invariants independent of replication.

RA-005 · Enabled price and conversion graphs gate admission

Medium · Addressed in plan

BW-002 rejects missing values and unlimited guaranteed gain loops before affected routes enable. Concrete prices are future authored inputs; this reset invents none.

RA-006 · Combined state-family proof precedes broad kit expansion

Medium · Addressed in plan

BW-096 integrates representative summons, forms, maintained support, damage debt and periodic transformations under real multi-client impairment/workload tests before complete kit certification.

RA-007 · Rated entry and normalized build selection need a product decision

Medium · Open

Effective level 60 and the 118-point rated budget are specified. Actual-level admission and a below-cap normalized ability/talent build selection/restoration policy remain unresolved. BW-067 and open-rated-entry gate the affected work; no cap-only or temporary-unlock policy is invented.

RA-008 · Guild and economic branches retain independent prerequisites

Low · Addressed in plan

Guild membership need not wait for all party formation. Trade, commissions, marketplace and guild storage reuse the durable asset owner without forcing an unnecessary serial chain.

RA-009 · Representative role encounter includes required mechanics

Medium · Addressed in plan

BW-031/BW-032 include representative tank, healer, damage and maintained-support behavior. The representative beginner journey is distinct from all-roster certification and never makes optional player activities mandatory.

RA-010 · Recipe knowledge and mastery remain explicit

Medium · Addressed in plan

Profession enrollment, knowledge grants, activation/mastery and all-band output behavior remain in BW-034/BW-038/BW-062. Static recipe presence alone does not prove entitlement or crafting behavior.

Technical foundation and sequence

BW-001 creates a blank native C++ project, dedicated-server target and clean build/cook/stage route. BW-002 establishes executable ownership/admission. BW-100 verifies the already acquired plugins before dependent transport and movement. Existing Unreal movement/replication facilities are evaluated before custom alternatives. Fixture, rendering, content and durable-store probes can proceed independently where hard prerequisites allow.

Two-client connectivity, authoritative movement, current recipient baselines and one action form the first technical playtest. Transient re-entry is distinct from durable reconnect. Later class/profession, world, group, PvP and economy families remain represented, followed by full integration, measured capacity and production recovery. No global phase barrier is imposed on independent branches.

Outstanding decisions

RA-007 remains open. Additional technical inputs include supplied plugin identities/versions and licenses, UE5 revision/source build, compiler/SDK and target platforms, native/plugin synchronization and relevancy limits, canonical coordinate conversion, provider/store/hosting, render path, capacity and recovery budgets. See all resolution gates. Ordinary future prerequisites do not make every unstarted epic Blocked.

Documentation completeness does not resolve authored content, balance, performance or recovery risk. No implementation location is designated or required by the documentation.
