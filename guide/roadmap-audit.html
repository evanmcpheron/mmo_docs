Executive summary

Needs Attention — suitable for detailed planning with explicit gates

The existing strategy has broad, coherent coverage. Its early authority proof, incremental recipient-safe replication, durable mutation probe, representative beginner journey and separate full-game/release gates are defensible. Ten findings warrant targeted corrections: one High, eight Medium and one Low. Nine are resolved in the roadmap; rated entry/build-selection policy remains open. No Critical finding was identified.

Reviewed on 17 October 2026: 92 existing epics across nine phases. All existing IDs and phase-page bookmarks are retained. Two missing outcomes were added as BW-095 and BW-096, yielding 94 epics. Existing ID gaps BW-078/BW-079 are intentional gaps, not duplicate or missing references. Every epic remains Not Started: documentation validation is not gameplay completion.

The corrected roadmap can guide subsequent domain planning. Rated normalization detail is Blocked on the RA-007 policy decision; release readiness is not established. The other recorded platform, configuration, content, economy, timing and balance decisions have named future gates and do not block unrelated planning.

Audit coverage and method

The audited clean baseline is repository commit c30fd44c90e172157307edfc9c350e0d816cf162; the initial roadmap registry SHA-256 was f4266411f2af02d9e469c0da38099d3b69a3f02525fd711004d6de6a9164d2a8. Finding evidence describes that baseline; linked epics now show corrected text. This dated audit is a planning snapshot, maintained separately from live progress.

  • Read the complete roadmap registry: all 92 objectives, scopes, hard/recommended dependencies, ACs, evidence requirements and ordering rationales; all nine phases/milestones, ten sequencing decisions, 17 coverage families, eight existing decision groups, deferred scope and maintenance rules. Checked all 14 baseline generated roadmap pages against that source.
  • Reviewed all 49 feature-page families: 18 system/test chapters, 12 class pages, 16 profession pages and three catalogs. Class review covered all 37 specialization loops, resource policies and named ability inventories; profession review covered all 16 rosters, six bands, product families, mastery and interdependencies. The feature coverage table and per-epic assessment below link the delivery owners.
  • Cross-referenced the existing gameplay/data audit, terminology, provenance, the site overview and repository build/navigation conventions. The older supporting-gameplay sequence is treated as a recommendation to compare, not a rule that overrides demonstrated prerequisites.
  • Enumerated all 501 canonical contracts (81 Enums, 168 Interfaces, 252 Schemas), their authority/fields/references and five dependency groups in Data Inventory and the schema bundle. Examined relevant targeting, ownership, build/equipment, resource/status/summon, recipe entitlement, group/loot, public projection, token/checkpoint, security and operational invariants. Reviewed 65 shared definitions, 35 catalog/sample bindings and the 29 resolved contract-decision records as connected evidence.
  • Checked coverage against shipped catalog shapes/IDs: 330 named abilities, 1,764 talents in 49 trees, 37 playstyle contracts, 11 races/12 faction-race selections, 16 professions, 240 mastery nodes, 257 representative recipes/items, 55 materials, 63 execution profiles, 60 progression rows and 98 sample allocations. This is coverage and relationship review, not exhaustive manual balancing of every node or certification of every interaction.
  • Compared sequence alternatives by actual acceptance needs, ownership/conservation, disclosure, integration cost and milestone evidence. Kept recommendations distinct from hard prerequisites; evaluated the initial networking order independently. The new combined-mechanic probe addresses a specific gap between a direct-action workload and a full-roster load test, rather than imposing a general network-first or network-last rule.

External technical check: Unreal Engine documentation describes dedicated-server builds, actor and component replication, RPCs, ownership and relevancy. The project still requires independent security, privacy and workload validation. See dedicated servers, networking overview and owning connections.

Limits: There is no Unreal project, game implementation repository, installed replication integration, real database/provider or executed gameplay workload. All implementation epics remain Not Started. Documentation checks and external technique studies do not verify an Unreal client, dedicated server, rendering scene or deployment. Engine/toolchain, replication plugins and platforms still require verified selection.

RA-001 · Content authoring has no explicit delivery owner

Medium · Resolved · Completeness; scope and responsibility
Confirmed coverage gap. Affected: BW-002, BW-033, BW-037, BW-047, BW-048, BW-049, BW-050, BW-091, BW-095.

Issue: The baseline assigns package admission to BW-002, content migration to BW-091 and content inventories to feature epics, but none delivers the versioned editors or importers explicitly required by the feature reference. Producing data and safely admitting it are distinct outcomes.

Evidence: At the audited baseline, BW-002 covers boundary validation and BW-091 explicitly excludes content authoring; no epic scope or AC names an editor/importer workflow. Content tools explicitly requires that workflow for abilities, statuses, talents, items, recipes, NPC kits, loot and encounter triggers. Readiness gates separate authoring, structural checks and actual execution.

Consequence if unchanged: Bulk binding and world production could develop separate ad hoc data paths, postpone semantic diagnostics and repeatedly repair packages by hand. The stated tooling requirement could remain unowned at release.

Corrective action / disposition: Added BW-095 for a bounded versioned author/import/validate/export route, using canonical contracts and reproducible packages. Starter objective/crafting production and the four kit cohorts require it; the foundation milestone includes its evidence. A GUI suite and a new scripting language are not required.

Confidence and uncertainty: High. The omission is textual and the required outcome is explicit. The choice of editor versus importer remains an implementation decision.

RA-002 · Three acceptance outcomes lack hard prerequisites

Medium · Resolved · Sequencing and dependencies
Confirmed dependency gaps. Affected: BW-011, BW-015, BW-022, BW-024, BW-026, BW-061.

Issue: BW-015 resolves delivered combat effects without requiring the numerical resolver; BW-024 applies loadout equipment references without requiring equipment ownership/slot validation; BW-061 promises a public repair fallback without requiring vendor/repair settlement.

Evidence: Baseline hard-prerequisite closures omit BW-011 for BW-015, BW-022 for BW-024 and BW-026 for BW-061. Compare projectile/ground resolution, atomic named loadouts, EquipmentSet and full-function station fallbacks. These are observable integration claims, beyond static reference-shape checks.

Consequence if unchanged: A dependency-ready epic could be declared complete using a stub for the very capability its acceptance is meant to prove. Later integration would need to repeat or invalidate that evidence.

Corrective action / disposition: Added BW-015 → BW-011, BW-024 → BW-022 and BW-061 → BW-026. Clarified actual shared effect resolution and rejection of stale/foreign/illegal loadout equipment references. Existing positions already respect these edges, so no renumbering or broad reorder was necessary.

Confidence and uncertainty: High for the missing integration boundaries. Fixture-only geometry or reference parsing could precede these prerequisites, but cannot certify the declared complete outcome.

RA-003 · Initial interest acceptance can imply certification of future state

Medium · Resolved · Acceptance criteria; architectural integrity
Confirmed scope ambiguity; integration risk. Affected: BW-007, BW-013, BW-014, BW-015, BW-096.

Issue: BW-013 precedes status and projectile execution but its current cast/status/effect reconstruction criterion is unconditional. Read literally, it demands future semantics; read narrowly, later producers lack an explicit obligation to rerun that disclosure/re-entry check.

Evidence: Baseline BW-013 AC2 names status/effect state while BW-014 and BW-015 depend on BW-013. Adding them as reverse hard dependencies would create a cycle. Recipient disclosure and interest entry requires current-state reconstruction and explicit public status allowlists, including when a recipient missed the original event.

Consequence if unchanged: An early projection pass might be reused as evidence for later ticks/hazards, leaving stale state or private-source leakage undiscovered. Requiring all final replication up front would instead overextend the first proof.

Corrective action / disposition: Scoped the first interest proof to implemented state kinds, added a roadmap-wide requirement that each new producer extend applicable projection/reconstruction/security checks, and added explicit late-join/status and current-hazard evidence to BW-014/BW-015. BW-096 checks their interaction. The initial network order is retained.

Confidence and uncertainty: High that the wording was ambiguous; Medium that a team would otherwise miss the extension, since existing scope and final gates already imply integration.

RA-004 · Daily economic reconciliation is not an acceptance outcome

Medium · Resolved · Completeness; operational acceptance
Confirmed requirement omission. Affected: BW-085, BW-093.

Issue: Economic monitoring, investigation, freezes, reversals and restore reconciliation are covered, but no baseline epic explicitly delivers the required recurring daily conservation reconciliation or makes a failed/missing run visible.

Evidence: Economy security and recovery requires daily reconciliation. EconomyReconciliationReport requires a bounded interval, declared sources/sinks, ledger delta and unmatched operations. Baseline BW-085 ACs cover incident signals and reversals, while BW-093 is an incident rehearsal.

Consequence if unchanged: A release could have strong manual incident tools while routine ledger drift or an inactive reconciliation process remains undetected.

Corrective action / disposition: Extended BW-085 with executed daily reconciliation over a consistent interval, zero-net internal transfers, item/receipt/ledger/outbox discrepancy evidence and visible failure handling. Every later economic service must join it before admission; final incident evidence still verifies the connected system.

Confidence and uncertainty: High. Daily cadence comes from existing requirements, not a new audit target. Retention, alert thresholds and operational ownership remain part of the existing configuration decisions.

RA-005 · The economic loop gate is explicit only after the first persistent journey

Medium · Resolved · Sequencing; acceptance criteria; security
Confirmed early-gate omission. Affected: BW-002, BW-026, BW-037, BW-039, BW-076.

Issue: BW-076 explicitly validates the recipe/vendor conversion graph, but the earlier vendor and personal-crafting epics can enable a buy-inputs → craft → sellback route before that gate. The open-economics note states intent without making the early pass evidence explicit.

Evidence: Vendor/conversion safeguards requires no unlimited guaranteed vendor loop and craft sellback below guaranteed NPC input cost. Baseline BW-026 and BW-037 precede BW-039; BW-076 occurs in the later services phase and contains the graph acceptance criterion.

Consequence if unchanged: An external persistent beginner playtest could admit a guaranteed currency or material loop even though the eventual full-game graph check would reject it.

Corrective action / disposition: Made the shared admission gate explicit in BW-002 and required BW-026/BW-037 to pass it for the currently enabled prices/recipes before use and after relevant edits. Missing required values keep affected routes disabled. BW-076 retains runtime conversion/salvage and full declared-graph certification; it need not block the first simple craft.

Confidence and uncertainty: High for the acceptance omission. Actual profitability is untested and depends on unauthored prices, so this is not evidence that the supplied prototype currently contains an exploitable loop.

RA-006 · Broad kit expansion lacks a combined state-family proof

High · Resolved · Architectural integrity; feasibility and milestones
Confirmed milestone gap; proposed risk reduction. Affected: BW-040, BW-041, BW-042, BW-043, BW-044, BW-046, BW-047, BW-048, BW-049, BW-050, BW-052, BW-083, BW-089, BW-090, BW-096.

Issue: The early workload deliberately covers movement/direct actions. The extended-state milestone can pass separate family fixtures and a simple handoff, while combined summon/status/hazard/support/debt traffic is not a required measured proof until the full roster and services exist.

Evidence: Baseline BW-083 explicitly limits its workload to implemented direct actions; the extended-state milestone requires individual family epics but no combined workload. BW-089 certifies full advanced recovery and BW-090 measures the final game. Networking workload requirements and Representative load scenarios identify legal summon counts, overlapping effects and active scripted gameplay as material workloads.

Consequence if unchanged: Cross-family event ordering, recipient projections, interest churn or replication cost could invalidate shared foundations only after hundreds of kit bindings depend on them. This is a rework risk, not a measured capacity failure.

Corrective action / disposition: Added BW-096 as a bounded combined-mechanic multi-client impairment/workload proof after the state families and BW-083, before broad cohort certification and every-roster beginner certification. Added it to the existing extended-state milestone. It records a reviewed workload and constraints without setting a speculative population/SLO; BW-089/BW-090 keep final recovery/capacity responsibility.

Confidence and uncertainty: High that the prior milestone lacks this evidence; Medium on the magnitude of future cost. Extra fixture and measurement effort is the tradeoff for earlier shared-boundary validation. No measured bottleneck or preferred topology is assumed.

RA-007 · Rated entry and normalized build selection need a product decision

Medium · Open · Cross-document consistency; architectural decision
Confirmed policy ambiguity. Affected: BW-067, BW-068, BW-073, BW-094.

Issue: The required level-60/118-point rated budget is clear, but actual-level admission and the source of a below-cap normalized ability/talent build are not. Ordinary progression only earns points and abilities at their declared levels.

Evidence: Rated normalization fixes effective level 60 and 118 points; ordinary ability progression and earned point budgets constrain ordinary access. PvpNormalizationState stores a normalized allocation but defines no actual-level entry/build-selection policy. Baseline BW-067 addresses stats and ordinary-build preservation without settling this distinction.

Consequence if unchanged: Independent teams could implement cap-only admission, an under-budget lower-level entrant, or temporary full unlocks differently. Silent choices would affect parity, UI, ability access and restoration.

Corrective action / disposition: Added open-rated-entry and an explicit BW-067 acceptance/evidence gate. Human input must choose actual-level-60-only admission or specify below-cap admission and temporary build/ability/role-service selection and restoration. Both alternatives must preserve documented normalization and ordinary progression. No gameplay rule or schema was changed.

Confidence and uncertainty: High that the entry policy is not specified in the reviewed sources. This does not reopen or invalidate the 29 previously resolved schema-review questions; it is a separate product/integration decision.

RA-008 · Guild membership need not wait for complete party formation

Low · Resolved · Sequencing; scope and responsibility
Architectural tradeoff; unnecessary hard coupling. Affected: BW-029, BW-030, BW-063.

Issue: Guild membership/ranks hard-depend on complete party/raid membership even though their durable identities and permissions are distinct. Shared invitation semantics and encounter-lock checks are genuine reusable boundaries, but party frames and group formation are not guild outcomes.

Evidence: Guild responsibilities and GuildInvitation define authoritative invitation, scope, membership and encounter checks. Baseline BW-063 hard-requires BW-030 without a stated need for its party/raid views or composition inspection.

Consequence if unchanged: Independent social work is unnecessarily withheld when a party UI or role-inspection task is incomplete. No final-game correctness failure follows if the original order is used.

Corrective action / disposition: Changed BW-030 from hard to recommended predecessor of BW-063, retained authoritative encounter-lock context and left the displayed order unchanged. The invitation implementation may still be shared; the report does not require separate code paths.

Confidence and uncertainty: Medium. A future implementation may choose a common delivered membership service; if that becomes an actual interface dependency, restore the hard edge with its rationale. This is a scheduling judgment rather than a contradiction of gameplay.

RA-009 · The first role encounter can omit required integration mechanics

Medium · Resolved · Acceptance criteria; cross-document consistency
Confirmed acceptance gap. Affected: BW-031, BW-032.

Issue: The role encounter checks tank/heal/damage completion, interrupts and reset, but does not require the complete combined encounter that the combat reference says must test those roles. A mostly stationary fight could pass.

Evidence: What a group encounter must test calls for a tank-facing cone, interruptible cast, recoverable group pulse, moving hazard and short priority-target phase. Baseline BW-031 ACs do not require that combination; BW-032 then builds its playstyle proof on the encounter.

Consequence if unchanged: Movement, facing, priority changes, telegraph readability and support could remain individually plausible but fail together only during late dungeon or balance certification.

Corrective action / disposition: Added a BW-031 criterion that the same authored encounter exercise all five documented features, with combined completion and wipe evidence. Full common-class/no-Bard composition certification remains with BW-069 rather than being moved wholesale into this representative proof.

Confidence and uncertainty: High. The required combination is explicit in existing mechanics; no new encounter mechanic or arbitrary timing target was introduced.

RA-010 · Recipe knowledge is not explicitly granted or checked

Medium · Resolved · Completeness; acceptance criteria; data-contract integration
Confirmed entitlement-coverage gap. Affected: BW-034, BW-037, BW-038, BW-062.

Issue: The baseline covers profession slots, skill, mastery and recipe inventories, but does not explicitly require durable recipe acquisition or rejection of an unlearned recipe. Accessible training is not evidence of an authoritative learned-recipe entitlement.

Evidence: Recipe acquisition/access distinguishes basic training/vendor access and advanced patterns. KnownRecipeRecord is unique per character/recipe and survives profession deactivation; craft acceptance validates recipe eligibility. Baseline BW-034 has no recipe-learning outcome and BW-037 no unlearned-recipe negative case.

Consequence if unchanged: A client could request an otherwise skill-eligible unknown blueprint, or learned recipes could disappear on deactivation/restart. Catalog coverage alone would not expose either failure.

Corrective action / disposition: Assigned declared-source, idempotent durable recipe learning to BW-034 and explicit learned/active-profession/skill/station checks to BW-037. BW-062 now certifies acquisition routes and explicit bounded runtime behavior for all 240 mastery nodes. No new acquisition mechanic, point currency or schema was added.

Confidence and uncertainty: High for the missing acceptance cases. General authority/admission rules already support the intended implementation; the audit makes their profession-specific proof mandatory.

Remaining risks

  • Implementation and content volume: the 329 bespoke-review ability records and 882 inherited bespoke talent bindings remain future work. All 330 abilities, all 1,764 talents, additional mandatory role services and all 240 profession mastery nodes still need actual behavior evidence. Current prototype readiness flags remain unchanged.
  • Budgets and topology: no measured concurrency, latency, recovery objective or hosting cost exists. BW-083/BW-096 expose early constraints; BW-082/BW-089 test ownership/reconstruction; BW-090 approves only a measured full workload. A small modular deployment and explicit transitions remain reasonable assumptions; neither microservices nor seamless travel is mandated.
  • Family growth: each newly admitted state or economic service must extend recipient projections, checkpoint/cleanup rules, endpoint policy and reconciliation. Early foundation completion cannot certify later mechanics. BW-089/BW-092 must rerun integrated evidence on compatible release revisions.
  • Product and numerical uncertainty: rated-entry policy remains unresolved; item-use commitment/interruption timing is already an explicit open binding decision. Actual starter/world/raid content, vendor prices, control/encounter parameters, balance, faction-role availability and mature-economy usefulness require authored records and measured evaluations.
  • Evidence and delivery feasibility: complete schemas and passing documentation tests cannot establish gameplay correctness or a staffing schedule. The release gate must retain failed/missing evidence and withhold approval; no remaining risk is resolved by changing a progress label.

Coverage assessment

Pass for delivery ownership; Needs Attention for decisions and execution. All documented baseline feature families have delivery paths, including interfaces and cross-system work that were only implicit before correction. No required feature family is intentionally omitted. Housing, territory/sieges, neutral exchange, class/race/faction changes, long offline manufacturing, mandatory voice and seamless travel remain outside the baseline; named lore and detailed fictional geography are not invented.
