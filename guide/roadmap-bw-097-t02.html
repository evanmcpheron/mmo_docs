Complete · Parent epic: BW-097 · Orthographic camera and visual-readability proof

Epic acceptance criteria: BW-097-AC-2

Implementation scope

  • Reuse assessment: Provided by Unreal or a plugin — UCameraComponent exposes orthographic width, FOV and projection mode; USpringArmComponent provides offset, collision and lag facilities. Source inspection shows distinct projection modes, not proof of seamless mixed-projection interpolation. Reuse native transforms/projection data and validate a bounded transition experiment before choosing the simplest policy that meets the epic.
  • Extend T01 single camera/configuration owner with a character-following elevated orthographic default and wheel-driven lowering/closing into a behind-character perspective endpoint, reversible through intermediate zoom. Preserve focus and apparent scale at any projection handoff; do not call a projection-mode toggle or generic view-target blend proof of continuity. Keep all transition/framing/smoothing/focus/clip tunables available in Unreal UI.
  • Use T01 scene-only stationary/axial/diagonal routes and explicit frame/landmark observations to measure continuity at endpoints, intermediate samples, direction reversal and supported frame rates/windows. Record the actual chosen transition policy, numeric tolerances and resulting captures. Reject invalid tuning with the existing config owner. If native facilities cannot meet the requirement, retain the failed experiment and obtain a bounded implementation decision rather than silently relaxing the orthographic endpoint.
  • MovementState, WorldPoint and SpriteDirection remain compatibility constraints: camera transforms, cosmetic height and pixel effects never quantize or modify continuous planar/floor position or aim. SpriteDirection retains its eight case-sensitive preview strings and does not drive gameplay or camera yaw. No new camera protocol or movement owner is needed.
  • Execution 2026-10-22: implemented the existing MatchFramingAtHandoff policy with native orthographic/perspective projections, smooth pitch/focus/log-width interpolation and a narrow-FOV matched-distance dolly. Added handoff-FOV and orthographic clipping controls to the same validated Data Asset. The failed 70-degree experiment and the later corrected camera-derived APawn aim failure are retained; no custom view extension or new camera protocol was needed. Source and acceptance details are in the retained review.

Out of scope

  • Middle-mouse orbit (T03), world-picking implementation (T05), production movement/prediction, specialized pixel renderer and production asset creation.

Dependencies

Hard prerequisites: BW-097-T01

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    The default camera is genuinely orthographic and follows the controlled pawn; inward scroll reaches a close behind-character perspective with lower viewing angle, and outward scroll returns to the recorded overview. Focus/framing remain continuous without visible projection jumps at measured handoff/reversal samples.

  2. AC 2

    Stationary, axial and diagonal routes, rapid scroll reversals, endpoints and tested frame-rate/window variations preserve character framing and keep camera-only input from changing route state, facing, collision or continuous aim. Frame observations meet the recorded continuity tolerances.

  3. AC 3

    Changing zoom endpoints, widths/FOV, focus offsets, transition and follow controls through Unreal UI affects the same camera owner and survives save/reload. Invalid settings fail observably; no second tuning source or forced eight-direction aim is introduced.
