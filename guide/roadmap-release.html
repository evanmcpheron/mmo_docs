Phase status: Not Started. Complete only when all 4 epics below are Complete with verified evidence.

No required epic, unresolved release-blocking decision or missing mandatory evidence remains; controlled release acceptance is recorded.

Integrated completion milestone: Production-ready full game. Its full prerequisites and integration evidence must also pass; individual components do not prove their integration.

Hard dependencies are required for completion. Recommended predecessors are scheduling preferences. Other phases are not global start barriers. How to read this roadmap.
