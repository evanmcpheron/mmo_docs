Phase: 6. Complete roster and beginner certification · Completion milestone: Full-roster opening and kit certification

Objective and scope

Prove the complete opening journey for every documented legal character choice.

Scope: All eleven races, both Human allegiances, twelve classes, level-1–10 ability/talent bindings and specialization graduations; full higher-level kit/tree certification remains separate.

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    Every legal race/faction/class combination creates the reviewed character with only authored legal appearance options.

  2. AC 2

    All 37 specializations graduate alone with functioning starting kits and an intelligible role lesson.

  3. AC 3

    Both factions meet required-path XP, equipment, vendor/recovery and optional profession learning goals.

  4. AC 4

    Fresh-player evaluation covers naming failure, help revisit, death, full bags, shared targets and resume without progression dead ends.

  5. AC 5

    Every early spendable point has legal bound choices; level-54 capstones and later abilities are not required to verify the beginner experience.

Verification and blockers

Completion evidence required: Future coverage matrix, all-specialization graduation fixtures and representative fresh-player usability recordings with findings resolved.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ticket planning

Before creating or revising tickets, apply the mandatory reuse assessment. Inspect existing owners and evidence, then map every criterion to the additional work and verification required.

Use BW-052 as the parent epic. Link each future ticket to the AC links above and state the evidence it will produce. Split work into demonstrable behaviors; one criterion may require several tickets. Epic completion still requires every criterion, verified evidence and completed hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Full-roster starter verification needs early-kit bindings, not every high-level ability or capstone. Later cohort certification reuses these fixtures without blocking early player feedback.

Related open decisions: Concrete world and starter records; Measured balance, usability and content limits
