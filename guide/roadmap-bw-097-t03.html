Implementation scope

  • Use --case reference with a bounded selection from the user-provided ProjectTitan Unreal sample: a complete Adventurer in a compatible source idle, house, birch, market canopy/table, barrels, pottery/containers, produce crates, stool and authored cobblestone paving maps. This follow-up replaces the Moore RPG comparison after the user still found it hard to see. Build a coherent courtyard with natural relative heights, measured bases/supports and a clear character footprint. Record local export recipes, source/dependency and converted hashes; do not import production asset volume or port Unreal gameplay.
  • Use DOCS_ROOT/assets/graphics_concept.png as the principal appearance reference and record its hash and annotated crops. Compare character-to-prop detail, relative proportions, palette/value separation, depth/contact cues and lighting at the declared gameplay scale. Link the selected Holland and Roystan material and annotated t3ssel8r clip timestamps; do not infer capability from any reference.
  • Create a versioned review sheet with observable still/motion acceptance bounds, reviewed comparison regions, test settings, reviewer/date, approved deviations and blocking findings. Establish visual and provisional CPU/GPU/memory budgets with the user before accepting downstream comparisons; unresolved acceptance choices are explicit review gates, never fabricated numeric requirements. Subsequent tickets fill their relevant motion/effect results against this baseline.
  • If heading presets are exposed, reuse SpriteDirection exactly as eight case-sensitive strings north, north_east, east, south_east, south, south_west, west and north_west; exercise them as preview presets while retaining continuous actor heading. No complete CharacterPreviewState/appearance catalog or new art gameplay schema is needed.

Out of scope

  • Pixel-perfect reproduction, production character/equipment/environment pipelines (BW-098), final environment art (BW-099), broad concept recreation or automatic visual approval.

Setup

  1. titan-assets.json selects eleven exports; titan-layout.json owns fourteen instance positions, heights, yaw and supports. A copy-on-write clone includes Characters, Environment and Textures, excluding the map/external-actor population. Original Unreal files are never saved. GLBs remain ignored in artifacts/titan-export-work/exports. Unsaved PBR materials and conventional texture duplicates preserve original authored color/normal/packed maps while avoiding black/white virtual-texture export failures and preserving original samplers. Missing, changed, incomplete, black, uniform transferred or checkerboard assets are rejected.
  2. The complete Adventurer is 1.8m tall. Barrels are 0.85–0.9m, small props 0.25–0.5m, canopy 2.8m, house 4.8m and birch 5.2m. Pose precedes measurement; scaling is uniform. Bases align to ground or the actual top of their support. A flat original receiver uses Titan cobblestone textures; the paving export supplies only its material.
  3. Automatic captures cover day/warm/cool at normal ×1 (9m orthographic height) and diagnostic ×2 (4.5m), both focused at 1m. Reset preserves close/day/135°/first window; the initial diagnostic view is not normal-scale acceptance. T02 resolution/angles/window/HUD policy remains unchanged. Normal-{day,warm,cool}-{native,pixelated}.png and comparison-{native,pixelated}.png use the same world/camera/pose/materials/lights. Native capture freezes the displayed source frame, renders the shared World3D through a duplicate camera in a temporary viewport, then verifies exact original pixels and restored update mode. No toon/outline shader is active.
