Detailed epics

BW-099 · Stylized environment and effects integration Not Started

Objective: Carry the selected 3D pixel-art style through complete gameplay environments without losing combat readability.

Scope: Terrain and billboard foliage, stylized water, lighting presets, atmospheric depth, weather/ambient particles and quality tiers. These are presentation requirements, not new weather, swimming or day/night gameplay systems.

Hard dependencies: BW-098, BW-028

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Approved outdoor/interior and bright/dark scenes use the shared art conventions; foliage coverage, shadow changes and camera motion preserve readable silhouettes and target markers without unstable flicker.
  2. Water scenes retain correct object/edge visibility above and below the surface, shoreline depth and transparent ordering; the chosen reflection/refraction method and lower-cost alternative pass the same relevant-hazard and privacy checks.
  3. Atmospheric light and rain/dust/splash effects integrate with depth and pixel scale, avoid distracting intersections and have measured per-effect costs; simpler tiers retain the approved atmosphere and gameplay visibility.
  4. Roofs, trees, water and layered floors support a tested occlusion treatment; outline, shadow, reflection, refraction and particle passes consume only authorized views and never reveal concealed or cross-floor entities.
  5. Quality changes and cosmetic substitutions preserve critical telegraphs, selection, aim/ground previews and friendly-frame access in crowded scenes; reduced-flash/shake controls and readable dark presets are verified.
  6. Each supported tier has reproducible scene/settings captures and CPU/GPU/memory measurements including dense foliage, water, atmosphere and overlapping combat effects; expensive effects have bounded budgets and tested alternatives.

Completion evidence required: Versioned environment scenes, reference comparison and pass-order recordings, visibility/accessibility fixtures, per-effect profiles and a tested quality/platform matrix.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Build on proven camera/material and asset foundations; final presentation and release capacity must include these real effects rather than an empty render scene.

Related open decisions: Render configuration and supported quality envelope

Related documentation: Selected 3D pixel-art direction; Rendering, camera and input; Rendering verification; t3ssel8r visual research channel

BW-053 · Level 11–60 progression journey Not Started

Objective: Provide an authored, traversable route from beginner departure to the documented level cap.

Scope: Release-selected leveling maps, quests, NPC kits, loot and ability-unlock opportunities tested with a declared supported roster; all-roster integration is BW-073, with no invented zone count or lore.

Hard dependencies: BW-039

Recommended predecessors: BW-052

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. An approved content manifest identifies reachable level-band destinations, quest/encounter rewards and material access through level 60.
  2. Fresh characters progress beyond graduation without missing prerequisites, unavailable destinations or unbound required abilities.
  3. Unlocks at 14/20/22/32/35/44/50 and later talent gates have eligible practice and encounter opportunities.
  4. Required-path reward/credit and recovery checks pass throughout progression; optional professions do not become mandatory combat-point sources.

Completion evidence required: Future approved leveling-content scope, path/reward validation and persisted end-to-end progression replays to level 60.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Wider-world path/content validation can use the representative roster while other classes bind independently. Exact content volume remains an explicit release-scope decision.

Related open decisions: Concrete world and starter records

Related documentation: Progression rules; XP and scaling; Content production gate

BW-065 · Flagged world PvP eligibility Not Started

Objective: Permit explicit player conflict while protecting unflagged and safe-area characters.

Scope: Faction/flag eligibility, confirmed assistance, hostile-participation timers and protected areas; no territory or siege mechanics.

Hard dependencies: BW-017, BW-028

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Faction hostility alone never authorizes attacking an unflagged character on the normal ruleset.
  2. Assisting a flagged participant requires confirmation or explicit automatic-flag settings and applies the authored timer.
  3. Protected arrival/recovery scopes reject offensive actions, including indirect pet/area actions.
  4. Normal progression remains in unrated world combat and level/eligibility indicators explain rejection.

Completion evidence required: Future cross-faction flag/assist/safe-area matrix including indirect and lingering effects.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: World PvP policy; Faction signaling

BW-066 · Consensual nonlethal duels Not Started

Objective: Resolve bounded player duels without reward or reset exploits.

Scope: Mutual invitation, server boundary, surrender, grace, time limit and terminal result.

Hard dependencies: BW-017, BW-019, BW-028

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Only mutually accepted eligible participants enter with explicit positive invitation, boundary/grace and time-limit parameters.
  2. Defeat stops at one health and grants no competitive reward, cooldown reset or resource refill.
  3. Surrender/boundary timeout forfeit; disconnect waits the documented grace, ties draw and invalid protection/scope cancels.
  4. Terminal results and repeated final actions cannot restart the duel or apply ordinary lethal rewards.

Completion evidence required: Future lifecycle, timing-boundary and simultaneous-end fixtures plus paired-client duel demonstration.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Duel rules

BW-067 · Rated combat normalization Not Started

Objective: Apply the documented equal-budget rules without altering the saved ordinary build.

Scope: Effective level 60, 118-point budget, primary/health ceilings, proportional secondary distribution and effect/consumable disclosure.

Hard dependencies: BW-022, BW-024, BW-028

Recommended predecessors: BW-066

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Entry computes level-60 normalized primary/health and controlled secondary budgets while preserving proportional selected distribution.
  2. Illegal talents/effects reject or use the explicitly reviewed mode policy; the original ordinary build remains unchanged.
  3. Rated exclusions remove external combat consumables and uncertified set/proc effects; UI explains adjustments before play.
  4. Profession ownership, race cooldowns and challenge-item effects cannot add permanent power beyond the normalized budget.
  5. Before completion, approve the actual-level entry and normalized build/ability-selection policy recorded in open-rated-entry. Every admitted level uses a legal level-60 59/59-point budget; entry, exit and reconnect preserve ordinary earned progression/builds and cannot grant unauthorized ordinary points or ability access.

Completion evidence required: Future before/after budget tests over low/normal/challenge gear and representative complete builds, with visible rules disclosure. Include the approved entry/build-selection policy and executed admitted-level, exit and reconnect matrix; unresolved policy prevents completion.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Budget normalization can be validated with representative legal kits before the full catalog; roster-specific PvP balance remains a later certification.

Related open decisions: Rated entry eligibility and normalized build selection

Related documentation: Normalization policy; Rated consumables

BW-068 · Instanced five-versus-five objective matches Not Started

Objective: Run the first complete objective-match lifecycle and record each competitive result once.

Scope: Two equal five-player teams, one control point, authored capture/scoring/respawn rules, cancellation and immutable result; queue/ranking algorithms are not invented.

Hard dependencies: BW-017, BW-067, BW-081

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Preparing validates teams, safe spawns and all positive authored timers/score limits before activation.
  2. Uncontested presence captures continuously; contest pauses, empty presence clears unfinished capture and only present uncontested ownership scores.
  3. Score limit wins; timeout uses higher score or draw with no implicit overtime; invalid matches cancel without competitive reward.
  4. Respawn, disconnect and repeated terminal events respect protection, control and normalization rules without duplicate results/rewards.
  5. Any authored participation/reward grant checks current mode eligibility and commits once per match/recipient; cancellation grants no competitive reward and duplicate terminal events create no second grant.

Completion evidence required: Future automated objective timeline tests, result-idempotency tests and recorded ten-client match under adverse network conditions.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related open decisions: Measured balance, usability and content limits; Rated entry eligibility and normalized build selection

Related documentation: Objective policy; Match rules

BW-077 · Voluntary PvE level-synced builds Not Started

Objective: Let a player enter lower-level PvE with an explicitly previewed legal effective-level build while retaining their original progression.

Scope: Effective-level stat recomputation, eligible ability and point budgets, separately saved downscaled builds or deterministic starter fallback, entry/exit preview and restoration; rated PvP normalization remains separate.

Hard dependencies: BW-022, BW-024

Recommended predecessors: BW-081

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Opt-in preview identifies effective level, eligible abilities, recomputed base attributes and separate legal point budgets; downscaling does not multiply the entire original stat sheet.
  2. The original build remains unchanged; selection uses a separately saved valid downscaled build or a deterministic starter build after preview, never arbitrary mid-encounter node removal.
  3. Entry/exit applies a coherent authoritative loadout and rejects invalid/in-combat transitions without partial state, free healing, resource refill or cooldown/debt deletion.
  4. Reconnect and exit restore the appropriate original/effective build and earned progression once; lower-level execution cannot use an ineligible ability or overspend talent points.

Completion evidence required: Effective-level stat/allocation and ability-access fixtures, opt-in/fallback UI demonstration, and entry/exit/reconnect tests preserving original build, progression, cooldowns and obligations.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: PvE downscaling preserves original progression and changes eligible unlocks, unlike rated level-60 normalization. It can be validated on a bounded fixture without waiting for the whole dungeon catalog.

Related documentation: Voluntary level-synced PvE; Effective-level attributes; Build restrictions

BW-069 · Dungeon encounter and loot certification Not Started

Objective: Deliver the approved five-player dungeon set with composition-independent mechanics and durable outcomes.

Scope: Authored encounters, navigation, telegraphs, wipe/reset, reward tables and admission; individual role mechanics were proved in BW-031.

Hard dependencies: BW-051, BW-081

Recommended predecessors: BW-031, BW-053

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Every release dungeon uses the declared one-tank/one-healer/three-damage baseline with common-class capability coverage.
  2. Common-only, no-Bard, support-DPS and varied melee/ranged groups can solve required mechanics.
  3. Compact/distributed healing and each tank defense category have viable tested responses; immune bosses retain required resource loops.
  4. Wipes, reconnects and repeated completion events preserve reset policy, eligible loot and full-bag delivery without rerolls.

Completion evidence required: Future approved dungeon manifest, group-composition completion matrix and executed encounter/reward/recovery tests.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related open decisions: Concrete world and starter records

Related documentation: Composition requirements; Encounter budgets; Loot policy

BW-070 · Declared-size raid encounter certification Not Started

Objective: Deliver the approved raid scope without assuming an unmeasured population or mandatory exclusive class.

Scope: Authored raid-size decision, encounter/revival pools, role/capability coverage, shared-drop claims and visual readability.

Hard dependencies: BW-051, BW-081

Recommended predecessors: BW-069

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. The release manifest declares raid size and encounter budgets; completion evidence measures that size rather than extrapolating to untested larger groups.
  2. Neither faction requires an exclusive aura/class or profession-only buff to satisfy an unavoidable mechanic.
  3. Shared encounter revival charges, control immunity and encounter resets behave consistently under concurrent participants.
  4. Shared drops persist one shuffled claimant order and never reroll on delivery/reconnect; ordinary equipment remains personal.

Completion evidence required: Future raid-size review, composition-matrix runs, participant/loot records and measured group readability/load results.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Dungeons and a ten-player raid prototype are recommended lower-cost proving grounds. Neither every dungeon nor an arbitrary final raid size is a technical prerequisite; validate the declared scope.

Related open decisions: Concrete world and starter records; Measured balance, usability and content limits

Related documentation: Raid scope; Faction coverage

BW-071 · Race, faction and equipment parity evaluation Not Started

Objective: Verify the documented parity constraints across complete builds and gear sets.

Scope: All races/roles, fixed offsets, faction mandatory capabilities, adaptive gear and low/normal/challenge complete-set budgets.

Hard dependencies: BW-051, BW-059

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Level-60 equal-equipment/build role-output comparisons test highest/lowest racial offsets and the stated less-than-1% target.
  2. Early-level resource breakpoints, healing and survival are measured separately rather than inferred from one primary-stat example.
  3. Complete sets preserve grade/slot, adaptive primary, dual-wield, enhancement and major-effect budgets.
  4. Both factions demonstrate required tank/healer/cleanse/interrupt/revival coverage and report any role-availability risk.

Completion evidence required: Future versioned full-set simulations, runtime comparisons and common-provider capability reports with unresolved deviations preventing certification.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

BW-072 · Leveling and mature-economy relevance evaluation Not Started

Objective: Prove useful profession access and sustainable asset flow in fresh and established populations.

Scope: Level-band usefulness, material accessibility, vendor/conversion loops, market concentration, affordability and sources/sinks; no promised market prices.

Hard dependencies: BW-053, BW-057, BW-062

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Unfunded learners are tested against the documented less-than-30-minute first-useful-output target in each relevant band.
  2. At levels 10/20/30/40/50 every manufacturing profession meets two of slot improvement, distribution change and useful utility for customers.
  3. Both factions have essential materials/recipes and NPC repair/recovery fallback without enemy trade or rare-pattern monopoly.
  4. Fresh/mature scenarios record currency/material sources/sinks, margins, recipe usage, concentration and affordability; no unlimited vendor/craft/salvage cycle remains.

Completion evidence required: Future reproducible economic scenarios, real-user relevance tests, conversion graph results and reviewed metric reports.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related open decisions: Prices, sources and conversion graph; Measured balance, usability and content limits

Related documentation: Relevance tests; Economy monitoring; Safeguards

BW-073 · Combat, build and PvP balance certification Not Started

Objective: Evaluate the complete playable roster with recorded scenarios and explicit uncertainty.

Scope: Solo/role encounter output, all 37 specialization routes, PvP matchups, sustain/control/mobility and faction composition; numerical tuning remains versioned.

Hard dependencies: BW-053, BW-065, BW-066, BW-068, BW-069, BW-070, BW-071, BW-052, BW-032

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. All 37 specializations have at least two tested legal builds changing at least two decision dimensions while retaining role viability; equal-budget output, survival, control, mobility and ally contribution traces expose their intended tradeoffs.
  2. Solo time-to-kill and healer sustain are measured against prototype expectations; provisional 5% output outliers receive documented investigation.
  3. PvP analysis includes melee uptime, line of sight, pets, healer interruption, pick/exclusion rates and skill/role-matched outcomes.
  4. Human build evaluations identify unusable/filler branches and exploit incentives; the enabled release package records resolutions rather than declaring schema counts balanced.

Completion evidence required: Future reproducible simulation/runtime reports, human playtest evidence, balance change history and approved enabled-content manifest.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related open decisions: Measured balance, usability and content limits; Rated entry eligibility and normalized build selection

Related documentation: Evaluation targets; Numerical targets; Pruning criteria

BW-074 · Complete presentation and accessibility certification Not Started

Objective: Make every enabled gameplay flow readable and operable with the documented input/accessibility options.

Scope: Complete 3D pixel-art character/equipment/form and environment inventory, authored animation/audio/icons, creator/build/profession/social/PvP screens, scalable UI and clutter controls; foundations are BW-097/BW-098/BW-099 and BW-028.

Hard dependencies: BW-052, BW-054, BW-055, BW-062, BW-064, BW-068, BW-069, BW-070, BW-057, BW-065, BW-066, BW-099

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Every enabled flow supports remapping, independent UI scaling, readable labels and non-color state cues; supported platforms have declared controller navigation coverage.
  2. Creation and talent editing can be completed by keyboard, with draft undo/search and consequential tooltip previews.
  3. Healers can target friendly frames without clicking world actors; quick-cast retains held preview/cancel, projected ground intent stays aligned across supported windows and rejected actions show specific reasons.
  4. Thirty-player clutter, legal summon density, small-window reflow, bright/dark/wet scenes, foliage/roof occlusion, reduced flashes/shake and audio/subtitle alternatives are evaluated; critical hostile telegraphs stay visible at every supported quality tier.
  5. Animation and sound follow server outcomes/time and never authorize effects or hide unavailable market/craft assets.
  6. The enabled race/class/equipment/form, NPC, environment and cosmetic asset manifest is complete and matches approved reference scenes at gameplay scale; all supported renderer/platform/tier combinations have resolved visual regressions and accessible separate UI.

Completion evidence required: Future complete art manifest and reference comparisons, renderer/platform/quality and accessibility/input matrices, crowded-scene motion recordings, small-window captures and resolved human usability findings on exact builds.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related open decisions: Concrete world and starter records; Measured balance, usability and content limits; Render configuration and supported quality envelope

Related documentation: Accessibility; Readiness scenarios; Combat display; Selected 3D pixel-art direction; Rendering, camera and input

BW-089 · Advanced-state transfer and recovery certification Not Started

Objective: Demonstrate that the fully enabled class and profession state survives reconnects, transfer and conservative crash recovery without duplicated power or erased debt.

Scope: Cross-system reconstruction regression for statuses/ticks, long cooldowns, summon capacity, forms, songs, signature resources, damage debt and profession jobs. This certifies integration beyond the first handoff. Voluntary effective-level/original build selection is included in the reconstruction matrix.

Hard dependencies: BW-082, BW-051, BW-059, BW-075, BW-077

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. A graceful transfer preserves permitted status expiry/next-tick/remaining budget, stable cooldown families, damage debt and current build under the declared cleanup/checkpoint policy.
  2. Destination reconstruction leaves exactly one owner summon budget; source pets, links, auras and unattended effects cannot continue granting output after authority ends.
  3. Form/song/resource reconstruction references the same canonical pools and budgets; reconnect/transfer cannot grant health, refill resources, erase debt or bypass cooldowns. Effective-level build selection and its original saved build survive reconstruction without eligibility or point-budget bypass.
  4. Profession jobs and associated receipts settle or resume only under their documented policy; a transfer rejected by a locked transaction cannot duplicate work or refund a committed craft.
  5. Unclean crashes demonstrate the documented conservative recovery point and encounter reset, preserving durable ownership while explicitly reporting lost transient combat state.

Completion evidence required: Required future proof: advanced-state reconstruction fixtures and multiplayer fault-injection runs for each enabled mechanic, including reconnect/transfer during a tick, summon replacement, debt payment and profession settlement.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Simple handoff should be proven early. Requiring every future class mechanic first delays risk discovery; treating that initial proof as certification of later state is equally unsafe.

Related open decisions: Incremental topology and comparable branch orders

Related documentation: Checkpoint ownership; Status and debt recovery; Reconstruction fixtures
