Phase objective and completion

Complete level-cap journeys, group/PvP content, advanced recovery, balance and presentation evidence.

0/14 complete · 0%

Phase status: Not Started. Complete only when all 14 epics below are Complete with verified evidence.

All-roster, progression, economic, group/PvP and usability certification pass; BW-092 separately verifies their release-candidate integration. Environment effects and complete 3D pixel-art/accessibility evidence are included.

Integrated completion milestone: Complete documented playable scope. Its full prerequisites and integration evidence must also pass; individual components do not prove their integration.

Hard dependencies are required for completion. Recommended predecessors are scheduling preferences. Other phases are not global start barriers. How to read this roadmap.

Epics in this phase

  1. BW-099 · Stylized environment and effects integration Not Started

    Carry the selected 3D pixel-art style through complete gameplay environments without losing combat readability.

  2. BW-053 · Level 11–60 progression journey Not Started

    Provide an authored, traversable route from beginner departure to the documented level cap.

  3. BW-065 · Flagged world PvP eligibility Not Started

    Permit explicit player conflict while protecting unflagged and safe-area characters.

  4. BW-066 · Consensual nonlethal duels Not Started

    Resolve bounded player duels without reward or reset exploits.

  5. BW-067 · Rated combat normalization Not Started

    Apply the documented equal-budget rules without altering the saved ordinary build.

  6. BW-068 · Instanced five-versus-five objective matches Not Started

    Run the first complete objective-match lifecycle and record each competitive result once.

  7. BW-077 · Voluntary PvE level-synced builds Not Started

    Let a player enter lower-level PvE with an explicitly previewed legal effective-level build while retaining their original progression.

  8. BW-069 · Dungeon encounter and loot certification Not Started

    Deliver the approved five-player dungeon set with composition-independent mechanics and durable outcomes.

  9. BW-070 · Declared-size raid encounter certification Not Started

    Deliver the approved raid scope without assuming an unmeasured population or mandatory exclusive class.

  10. BW-071 · Race, faction and equipment parity evaluation Not Started

    Verify the documented parity constraints across complete builds and gear sets.

  11. BW-072 · Leveling and mature-economy relevance evaluation Not Started

    Prove useful profession access and sustainable asset flow in fresh and established populations.

  12. BW-073 · Combat, build and PvP balance certification Not Started

    Evaluate the complete playable roster with recorded scenarios and explicit uncertainty.

  13. BW-074 · Complete presentation and accessibility certification Not Started

    Make every enabled gameplay flow readable and operable with the documented input/accessibility options.

  14. BW-089 · Advanced-state transfer and recovery certification Not Started

    Demonstrate that the fully enabled class and profession state survives reconnects, transfer and conservative crash recovery without duplicated power or erased debt.

Open an epic for its full scope, dependencies, acceptance criteria, verification and ticket planning.
