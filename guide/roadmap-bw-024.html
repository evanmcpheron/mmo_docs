Phase: 3. Recoverable combat and personal state · Completion milestone: Recoverable persistent character

Objective and scope

Accept legal builds and apply their declared behavior as one versioned change.

Scope: Tree budgets/prerequisites/exclusivity, previews, respec restrictions, rollback/revalidation and safe-point application; exhaustive content binding is later. Named saved loadouts contain both allocations, selected specialization, action bars and equipment-set references.

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    Both trees enforce rank, tier, prerequisite, exclusive-choice and 59-point constraints at every spendable level.

  2. AC 2

    Client previews never authorize a build; stale or invalid submissions preserve the previous active build and points.

  3. AC 3

    Accepted named loadouts atomically apply both tree allocations, specialization, action bars and validated owned equipment-set references after the three-second stationary application; stale, missing or illegal equipment references and combat/damage interruption preserve the previous loadout.

  4. AC 4

    A content change requiring refunds/revalidation preserves stable IDs and restores a legal build rather than retaining obsolete effects.

  5. AC 5

    Application obeys party/encounter/match restrictions, never increases absolute current health or restores missing resources/cooldowns/debt, and removes old summons/auras/invalid action entries atomically.

Verification and blockers

Completion evidence required: Positive and exclusion talent fixtures, stale build contention runs and a migration/refund scenario.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ticket planning

Use BW-024 as the parent epic. Link each future ticket to the AC links above and state the evidence it will produce. Split work into demonstrable behaviors; one criterion may require several tickets. Epic completion still requires every criterion, verified evidence and completed hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Build application needs the equipment ownership/slot boundary as well as progression. A legal talent allocation cannot authorize a stale or foreign equipment-set reference.
