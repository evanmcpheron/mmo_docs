Phase: 3. Recoverable combat and personal state · Completion milestone: Recoverable persistent character

Objective and scope

Restore a character without duplicating authority or clearing obligations.

Scope: Disconnect grace, clean logout, owner-private reconnect baselines, cooldown/status/debt checkpoints and documented unclean encounter reset.

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    In-combat disconnect retains the configured grace behavior without invulnerability or new player/pet commands; reconnect claims the existing character.

  2. AC 2

    Reconnect restores durable assets/build/progress and surviving cooldown, status and damage obligations without duplicate rewards.

  3. AC 3

    Logout and crash recovery apply declared cleanup/reset policies, never inventing exact replay of uncheckpointed combat ticks.

  4. AC 4

    Expired/stale sessions and simultaneous reconnects leave one authoritative owner; the public snapshot is filtered separately from owner-private restoration.

Verification and blockers

Completion evidence required: Injected disconnect/crash/reconnect fixtures at cast, status, reward and build boundaries with reconciled ownership.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ticket planning

Use BW-027 as the parent epic. Link each future ticket to the AC links above and state the evidence it will produce. Split work into demonstrable behaviors; one criterion may require several tickets. Epic completion still requires every criterion, verified evidence and completed hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Recovery is proven before a complete persistent journey. Exact tick replay is not required by the documented conservative recovery policy.

Related open decisions: Incremental topology and comparable branch orders
