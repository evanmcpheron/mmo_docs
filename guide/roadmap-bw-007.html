Phase: 2. First authoritative multiplayer interaction · Completion milestone: Two-client authoritative action

Objective and scope

Give clients a coherent view of entities they are allowed to observe.

Scope: Spawn/despawn, revisions, initial baselines, owner-private state and visible public value projections; broad interest scheduling is BW-013.

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    Two clients agree on authoritative movement and entity identity after join, leave and re-entry.

  2. AC 2

    A new observer receives current state rather than depending on an earlier event it never saw.

  3. AC 3

    Stale or duplicate snapshots do not resurrect despawned entities or overwrite newer state.

  4. AC 4

    Owner resources and private recovery state are absent from another client’s public snapshots; unavailable references fail safely.

Verification and blockers

Completion evidence required: Two-client lifecycle traces including late join and stale snapshot injection.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.
