Objective and scope

Resolve effective combat results using documented numerical and accounting rules.

Scope: Primary effects, mitigation, criticals, healing, shields and bounded modifier order; recurring statuses and complex proc graphs follow separately. Bounded damage transfer uses the same effective-result accounting.

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    Identical seeded contexts yield the documented modifier order, rounding and effective damage/healing/absorbed amounts.

  2. AC 2

    Overkill and overhealing are recorded without increasing effective output, threat or reward contribution.

  3. AC 3

    Support attribution sums only to the actual support increment under ordered marginal accounting and never adds damage again.

  4. AC 4

    Resource/cooldown commitment and root action identity survive a rejected or duplicate result without extra outcomes.

  5. AC 5

    Redirected damage obeys its cap, receives no second mitigation and cannot redirect recursively; an immune protector leaves damage on the original recipient.

Verification and blockers

Completion evidence required: Numerical execution fixtures including caps, shields and multi-source support attribution.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.
