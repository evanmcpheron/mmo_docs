Supporting gameplay

Mechanics, governing rules, edge cases and acceptance criteria.

These mechanics are necessary to connect the character, item and profession systems. They are included as bounded gameplay contracts, not worldbuilding or a claim that a complete MMO service has been implemented.

The character creation and level 1–10 beginner area is the first complete player journey to bring these features together. Use that compact opening experience for repeat playtests before expanding the wider world.

Party, raid and loot ownership

Credit and selection: Combat credit requires engagement-snapshot membership, encounter bounds and a nonzero effective damage, healing, consumed prevention, tanking or support contribution before completion. Late joins qualify for the next encounter. Other objective kinds credit the authenticated successful actor. Per-objective root-event keys deduplicate and clamp counts; one completion key grants rewards once.

Loot tables explicitly select all guaranteed entries, independent declared per-entry probabilities or one entry using positive relative weights. Never infer rates from grade. Ordinary equipment remains personal. Explicit shared drops offer equal-priority claim/pass: at deadline the server uniformly shuffles eligible claimants once, persists that order and awards its first claimant. Missing choice is pass; all-pass creates no winner. Delivery retries reuse the recorded result and never reroll.

Party and guild invitations require creation time, configured expiry, version and one of pending, accepted, declined, revoked or expired. Only a pending unexpired invitation can transition. Only its recipient accepts or declines; an authorized inviter or leader may revoke. Acceptance rechecks membership, capacity, authority and encounter locks, then atomically commits membership and terminal invitation state. Retries return the stored result. Expiry checks use authoritative time even before cleanup jobs run.

A standard dungeon group is one tank, one healer and three DPS. Raid size is a content parameter; prototype at ten players before assuming larger sizes. Party invitations, leadership, kicks and loot settings are server-owned. Lock disruptive membership changes during an encounter where they could alter loot rights or reset mechanics.

An encounter creates a reward-eligibility snapshot from participating players and party membership, then tracks actual eligible contribution. Healing, tanking and support count; raw damage alone is not eligibility. Joining at the final hit, hitting a friendly summon or standing outside encounter bounds cannot create a full reward.

Use personal reward ownership for ordinary instanced equipment initially. Tradable world materials and explicit shared drops use declared roll rules. Items enter durable reward delivery when bags are full. A unique encounter-completion key prevents reconnects or repeat death events from awarding the same reward twice. Loot corpse expiry never deletes an already awarded item.

For open-world creatures, reserve first valid engagement for an individual or group, with clearly advertised public-event exceptions. Participation credit and skinning rights are distinct. A player cannot skin a creature while another eligible player still has an unclaimed loot reservation. Resource-node claim rules are defined on the profession page.

Death, recovery and travel safety

PvE death imposes repairable durability loss, not loss of levels or talent points. A defeated character chooses a legal recovery option; the server validates revival state and destination. A resurrection does not erase cooldown debt or restore consumed items. Encounters define wipe/reset behavior, including owned summons and ground effects.

Travel, recall and instance entry fail safely when a character is in combat, crafting under a locked transaction, carrying a prohibited objective or crossing into an unavailable server. Durable ownership remains with one process. Explicit loading transitions are acceptable initially; seamless travel is not a prerequisite for a large world.

Guilds, banks and communication

Guild commands use exact payload variants for invite, remove, rank edit, bank deposit, item withdrawal and currency withdrawal. Rank edits carry a matching rank ID and typed replacement policy; a caller cannot grant authority it does not possess. Invitation accept/decline/revoke variants carry the invitation ID and expected revision and do not masquerade as granted guild permissions.

Withdrawal limits count committed nonreversed outgoing item units and currency per member over the configured rolling window. Deposits, failed operations and retries do not consume or refill allowance; rank changes do not reset usage. Check and reserve allowance, transfer ownership or currency, and write the audit record atomically. Zero limits forbid that withdrawal kind; the window must be positive.

Guild permissions distinguish invite, remove, rank-edit, bank deposit, item withdrawal and currency withdrawal. Apply per-rank limits and audit logs. A withdrawal is the same transactional inventory operation as personal trading; it is not a special unaudited pathway.

Provide party, raid, guild, local and private chat with rate limits, mute/block/report and moderation history. Do not expose hidden players through nearby-player chat queries. Private-message and trade restrictions should have explicit anti-spam policies. Voice chat is optional and not a dependency of class mechanics.

Content tools

Execution and reconstruction fixtures pin a FixtureWorldContext: authoritative time/tick, owner epoch, deterministic collision configuration, initial visible/NPC/group state and a server-test seed. The initial command occurs at t=0; finite typed follow-up steps record movement, repeated commands, cancellation/interruption, respec, disconnect/reconnect, death or transfer at nondecreasing origin-relative offsets. Server-test control events are never gameplay RPCs. Talent assertions explicitly compare legal/illegal graph results.

Design-complete content contracts: EncounterCondition and NpcAbilityRule define finite server scheduling without arbitrary scripts. Navigation requires bounded same-floor paths, a path-point limit and a positive replan interval. complete_reset_v1 clears encounter-owned state and restores home NPC state while preserving player cooldowns, debt, consumed items and durable transactions. Actual kits, maps and handlers are later Briarwake implementation work.

Fixture specifications declare a typed scenario, exact content/rules revisions, typed assertions and explicit run evidence. Assertions cover resources, cooldowns, statuses, casts, scope, idempotency and combat results. Unexecuted specifications have no runner or completion time; only an actual runner may record passed/failed. Documentation validation never substitutes for executing a gameplay fixture.

Create versioned editors or importers for abilities, statuses, talents, items, recipes, NPC kits, loot tables and encounter triggers. Validate stable IDs, missing references, caps, mutually exclusive options, illegal target relations, invalid resource types and impossible progression before a server accepts a content package.

Every enabled ability needs a successful validation fixture, a legal execution fixture, an invalid-target fixture and a reconnect/state-reconstruction fixture. Every behavior-changing talent needs at least one positive and one exclusion test. Reusing an effect operation is desirable; inventing an unrestricted scripting language for every small content change is unnecessary.

Security, operations and support

Administrative scope is finite and default-deny: support is read-only; moderator handles moderation/restrictions; game_master handles progression/inventory edits; economy_operator handles freezes/reversals; release_operator handles restore. Every grant also requires an explicit realm scope and immutable audit action; there is no wildcard role or client-granted authority.

Appeals record pending/approved/rejected state and an authorized reviewer/time for terminal decisions. Approval creates a separate audited action and preserves original history. Backups identify one consistent economic checkpoint and SHA-256 artifact digests; restore completion requires verified integrity and reconciliation. Migrations record planned/validated/applied/failed state and a declared rollback plan. Actual service integration, retention configuration, backup restore and rollback exercises remain future work.

Server logs must identify the action ID, account/character owner, content version and inventory/ledger versions for important changes. Record enough detail to investigate duplicates and economy exploits without logging passwords or unnecessary personal data. Keep administrative grants auditable and permission-limited.

Plan account recovery, login/session expiry, bans and appeals, chat moderation, backup restoration, database migrations, deployment rollback and support tools. Test a restore, not just creation of backup files. Exact hosting capacity and operational cost remain unmeasured.

A rollback must distinguish restoring corrupted state from undoing legitimate completed trades. Do not rewind only currency while leaving the purchased item in circulation. Emergency market or crafting suspension is a service flag with a user-visible reason, not an undocumented data edit.

Implementation order

Stage Concrete completion gate
1. Combat kernel Authority, targeting, movement, effect ordering, numerical tests and duplicate-action handling
2. Role slice One tank, healer and DPS kit work from level 10 through a small encounter; no optional talent supplies a mandatory role function
3. Playstyle proof Two builds in one specialization change measured action/resource/positioning decisions; two specializations visibly differ
4. Item transactions Loot, equip, inventory, durability, trade and full-bag delivery survive concurrent requests and process restarts
5. Profession slice One gathering/manufacturing chain produces useful band-1, band-3 and band-6 output; commissions use bound customer cores safely
6. Secondary systems Summons, songs, shapeshifting, damage debt and faction-exclusive coverage are validated
7. Scale and usability Automated network load plus human playtests, economic simulation, accessibility and support procedures
8. Content expansion Bind and certify remaining catalog entries; do not equate data count with a tested launch roster

A prototype can begin with Warrior, Bard and Mage because all are shared, then add Cleric and Necromancer to test exclusive-side differences. Faction test parties must also prove shared Bard/Shaman/Druid/Monk healers can complete every required healing/cleansing mechanic.
