Phase: 6. Complete roster and beginner certification · Completion milestone: Full-roster opening and kit certification

Objective and scope

Certify every healing specialization with sustainable resources and complete role services.

Scope: Cleric/Bard shared kits; High Priest, Oracle, Justicar, Minstrel, Grovekeeper, Spiritmender and Lifewalker. Completion requires every documented cohort ability, not a selected enabled subset. Excludes adjacent gameplay families and production certification beyond this epic’s stated acceptance criteria.

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    Every healer uses Mana plus only its declared signature state and has affordable direct/group recovery, cleanse and out-of-combat revival.

  2. AC 2

    Effective healing/consumed prevention qualify generators; overhealing and unused shields cannot farm state.

  3. AC 3

    Links, chains, wards, seeds and melee-assisted healing obey recipient caps, eligibility and unique-target policies.

  4. AC 4

    Every documented cohort ability has bound behavior and passes legal, invalid-target and reconstruction fixtures; both factions have common-class healer coverage.

Verification and blockers

Completion evidence required: Future all-healer service matrix, sustained encounter traces and executed ability-fixture results.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ticket planning

Use BW-049 as the parent epic. Link each future ticket to the AC links above and state the evidence it will produce. Split work into demonstrable behaviors; one criterion may require several tickets. Epic completion still requires every criterion, verified evidence and completed hard prerequisites.
