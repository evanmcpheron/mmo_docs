Phase objective and completion

Establish reproducible runtime evidence, a versioned content-production route and durable ownership independently of combat. Verify orthographic camera framing and initial visual readability alongside headless development.

1/6 complete · 16.7%

Phase status: In Progress. Complete only when all 6 epics below are Complete with verified evidence.

The durable retry/crash probe is executed against a real candidate store. The author/import route produces a reproducible reviewed package and rejects invalid content. A reproducible 3D reference scene verifies camera projection, target readability and initial visual/performance settings.

Integrated completion milestone: Executable ownership proof. Its full prerequisites and integration evidence must also pass; individual components do not prove their integration.

Hard dependencies are required for completion. Recommended predecessors are scheduling preferences. Other phases are not global start barriers. How to read this roadmap.

Epics in this phase

  1. BW-001 · Reproducible client and headless project Complete

    Prepare and create the first native C++ Unreal Engine 5 project with reproducible client and dedicated-server execution from zero.

  2. BW-002 · Authority boundaries and content admission In Progress Dependency-ready

    Make documented state ownership and enabled-content compatibility enforceable boundaries.

  3. BW-003 · Repeatable runtime fixtures and diagnostics Not Started

    Produce attributable runtime evidence from the first experiments.

  4. BW-097 · Orthographic camera and visual-readability proof Not Started

    Verify a character-following camera that starts in an elevated angled orthographic view, smoothly scrolls into a close third-person perspective, and orbits the character on middle-mouse drag, while preserving readable 3D gameplay and world picking.

  5. BW-095 · Versioned content authoring and import workflow Not Started

    Make documented content producible and reviewable through the same contracts used by runtime admission.

  6. BW-018 · Durable ownership and mutation recovery probe Not Started

    Prove economic consistency before building asset-dependent features.

Open an epic for its full scope, dependencies, acceptance criteria, verification and ticket planning.

Direct prerequisites from other phases: None
