Development roadmap overview

A dependency and evidence map for Briarwake, an Unreal Engine 5 native C++ MMORPG. This documentation owns gameplay contracts and progress; evanmcpheron/BriarwakeUnreal owns the newly versioned native bootstrap. Completion depends on scoped executed evidence rather than planning or historical engine results.

Start with the next actionable epic below, or browse a phase index and open an individual epic for its scope, dependencies, acceptance criteria and evidence. The order is an engineering recommendation; phases are review groups, and independent work can advance whenever its own hard prerequisites are met.

Sequencing decisions

  • Authority and the first network proof
  • Replication grows with executable semantics
  • Prediction before broad usability judgments
  • Small durability proof early; live assets later
  • Stable boundary contracts, incremental binding
  • Commitment before effects; representative mechanics before volume
  • Two meanings of playable
  • Local scopes before distributed handoff
  • One asset boundary; parallel service branches
  • Operational capability follows the value at risk
  • Camera and art foundations before asset volume

Open decisions

  • Engine, platform and deployment candidates
  • Capacity, security and recovery budgets
  • Concrete world and starter records
  • Runtime bindings and complete release inventory
  • Prices, sources and conversion graph
  • Measured balance, usability and content limits
  • Incremental topology and comparable branch orders
  • Concrete item-use timing bindings
  • Rated entry eligibility and normalized build selection
  • Camera, asset conventions and supported visual quality
