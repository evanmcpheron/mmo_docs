Detailed epics

BW-036 · Authorized fishing sessions Not Started

Objective: Resolve fishing catches from a validated interaction instead of a client-selected reward.

Scope: Cast timing, eligible output tables, interruption and life-skill progress; authored fishing locations come with world content.

Hard dependencies: BW-034

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Only an active authorized cast within the authored timing tolerance can resolve its catch table.
  2. A cast resolves once; duplicate catch claims and expired sessions produce no extra item or skill progress.
  3. Invalid timing, interruption and unavailable interaction scopes return explicit failure without consuming unrelated assets.

Completion evidence required: Future timing-boundary, replay and full-bag catch tests with authoritative session records.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Fishing rules; Fishing products

BW-038 · Profession mastery and activation changes Not Started

Objective: Apply bounded production choices without refill, rounding or switching exploits.

Scope: Mastery trees, free idle-station reallocation, dormant learned progress and 24-hour activation cooldown.

Hard dependencies: BW-037

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. One point per ten skill levels reaches 30 points against 45 available ranks, respecting branch prerequisites.
  2. Savings/channel/yield effects stay within 12%/15%/10% caps; rare cores are never saved or duplicated.
  3. Each required input consumes at least one; fractional remainder accounting gives equivalent totals across batch sizes.
  4. Accepted jobs retain their authorized mastery snapshot; switching during active work rejects and deactivation removes active benefits without deleting learned skill.

Completion evidence required: Future all-branch allocation tests, batch-equivalence/property tests and active-job switching/restart scenarios.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Mastery and activation

BW-040 · Owned mobile pets and summon commands Not Started

Objective: Support controlled mobile companions within shared simulation and output budgets.

Scope: Summon lifetime/capacity, attack/follow/hold/recall, navigation, owner attribution and command feedback; kit expansion is BW-050.

Hard dependencies: BW-028, BW-045, BW-027

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. One four-unit owner capacity/output pool permits one elite or eligible lesser servants, including the resolved two-unit Bonewright exception; atomic replacement never doubles capacity or aggregate output.
  2. Commands reject illegal targets and paths; follow/hold/recall behave visibly without wall crossing.
  3. Death, loss of owner/scope and respecialization remove or reconstruct only the permitted owned summons.
  4. Pet attacks retain root attribution and cannot recursively generate extra rewards or resource budgets.

Completion evidence required: Future capacity, blocked-path, ownership-loss and command/state-reconstruction fixtures plus multiplayer demonstration.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Pets and capacity; Gravecaller; Beastmaster

BW-041 · Bounded static combat deployables Not Started

Objective: Make traps, totems and linked combat objects behave as finite owned effects.

Scope: Placement, trigger, expiry, relocation, owner ceilings and explicit links; portable profession utilities are BW-061.

Hard dependencies: BW-045, BW-027

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Totemist uses four total combat totems and at most three links including shared-class objects; relocation cannot preserve duplicate old objects.
  2. Saboteur devices obey the three-active-device policy and each device triggers at most once.
  3. Illegal placement, cross-scope targeting and over-capacity requests reject without free effects or lost committed resources.
  4. Expiry, owner loss, encounter reset and reconstruction preserve the same bounded object/trigger state.

Completion evidence required: Future linked-layout, trigger-race, capacity and scope/reconstruction fixture results.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Static objects; Totemist; Saboteur

BW-042 · Validated combat forms Not Started

Objective: Change form without duplicating attributes, resources or equipment power.

Scope: Druid shared and specialization forms, transition eligibility, role defense, presentation and invalid-action cleanup.

Hard dependencies: BW-022, BW-028, BW-027

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Only eligible forms and actions activate; tank health/mitigation requires the valid defensive form.
  2. Form attacks use one normalized equipped-weapon budget and the declared resource pool.
  3. Changing form grants no extra current health, cooldown reset, duplicate aura or resource refill.
  4. Visual state and available actions match the authoritative form after rejection, death and reconnect.

Completion evidence required: Future form-transition matrix, budget/resource tests and reconstructed client presentation evidence.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Form budgets; Wildguard and Feral

BW-044 · Persistent delayed-damage debt Not Started

Objective: Prove deferred damage remains owed through build and session changes.

Scope: Ironbody ledger, bounded delay/clearing, lethal resolution and debt display; ordinary damage transfer already belongs to BW-011.

Hard dependencies: BW-027, BW-028, BW-045

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. At most 30% of eligible post-absorb damage is delayed over six seconds, with no second Armor reduction or re-delay.
  2. An authorized Balance spend clears only the declared ledger fraction and cannot treat debt as prevented damage.
  3. Death timing is correct when debt overlaps new damage; disconnect, form/build changes and leaving combat never erase outstanding debt.
  4. The interface displays debt separately from health and shields and resumes the same remaining schedule after recovery.

Completion evidence required: Future lethal overlap, respec/disconnect/restart ledger fixtures and matching server/client timelines.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Validate a durable timed liability before enabling its tank kit; ordinary health snapshots alone cannot prove debt reconstruction.

Related documentation: Resolution order; Ironbody

BW-046 · Periodic effect budget transformations Not Started

Objective: Allow extension, spreading and consumption without duplicating remaining periodic output.

Scope: Disease harvest/acceleration, Burning consumption and healing-seed maturation/consumption; ordinary ticking is BW-014.

Hard dependencies: BW-045

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Consuming a selected remaining periodic budget removes that source once and cannot preserve future ticks as free output.
  2. Extension/acceleration respects declared total-per-application budgets and fixed baseline tick policies.
  3. Mature healing seeds cannot award both the consumed recovery and a duplicate scheduled tick.
  4. Repeated, expired and immune-target cases preserve caps, owner attribution and resource-generation exclusions.

Completion evidence required: Future tick-boundary, simultaneous consume/refresh and repeated-harvest traces with conserved effect budgets.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Related documentation: Periodic and proc rules; Plaguebringer; Grovekeeper

BW-096 · Integrated advanced-mechanic multiplayer probe Not Started

Objective: Validate interacting state families and their representative network workload before broad kit certification multiplies them.

Scope: A bounded local multiplayer integration/workload package using representative summons, static objects, statuses/periodic transforms, forms, songs/signature state and delayed-damage debt. Full-roster balance, cross-process advanced recovery and production capacity remain separate gates.

Hard dependencies: BW-040, BW-041, BW-042, BW-043, BW-044, BW-046, BW-083

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Independent clients exercise the implemented state families together using a versioned representative package, legal owner/object limits and a declared workload; combined actions preserve effective-result attribution, resource/output caps and outstanding debt.
  2. Late join, interest re-entry, authorization loss and latency/loss/reordering recover coherent authorized current state without duplicate entities/effects or hidden-source disclosure; client feedback agrees with authoritative results.
  3. Repeatable density and join/departure runs retain hardware/configuration, tick percentiles, entity/subscription counts, traffic, queues and memory; legal summon limits and overlapping actionable hazards are represented in the workload. Client captures and frame measurements include real summon/form assets and overlapping telegraphs in the selected render view.
  4. A reviewed report accepts a bounded tested workload and records constraints and follow-up gates before broad kit certification. Unresolved correctness or blocking integration failures prevent completion; these measurements make no production-concurrency promise.

Completion evidence required: Executed combined-mechanic multi-client scenarios, impairment and density traces, packet/projection inspection and a reviewed workload/constraint report tied to exact game and content revisions.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: BW-083 measures the direct-action kernel; isolated family fixtures cannot establish their combined traffic and timing behavior. A small integrated probe retires that risk before cohort expansion while BW-089 and BW-090 retain final recovery and capacity responsibility.

Related documentation: Representative network workloads; Interest and relevant hazards; State-family boundaries; Selected 3D pixel-art direction; Rendering verification

BW-081 · Private instance membership and lifecycle Not Started

Objective: Provide isolated group-owned encounter spaces with controlled admission, departure and reset.

Scope: Runtime instance identity, party admission, protected entry/exit, encounter locks and instance cleanup in a single process. Dungeon/raid content and separate-process scheduling remain separate.

Hard dependencies: BW-016, BW-030, BW-080

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. Two parties entering the same content receive distinct runtime instances; entities, effects, targeting and chat/interest queries cannot cross the boundary.
  2. Admission checks authoritative membership, capacity, eligibility and encounter state; retries cannot create duplicate occupancy or bypass a lock.
  3. Leaving, disconnecting and rejoining resolve to the configured instance and safe location; an unavailable or closed instance fails safely.
  4. A genuine reset clears encounter-owned hazards, threat, summons and eligibility state without refunding player consumables, resetting cooldown/debt or repeating durable rewards.
  5. Closing an empty or completed instance leaves no orphaned controllable entities or pending ownership claim.

Completion evidence required: Required future proof: two-party isolation/admission tests, reconnect and unavailable-instance cases, reset/conservation fixtures and lifecycle diagnostics showing cleanup.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Instance isolation is a gameplay/security capability before it is a deployment topology; validate it locally before adding cross-process failure modes.

Related open decisions: Incremental topology and comparable branch orders

Related documentation: Party and encounter ownership; Encounter reset; Travel safety

BW-082 · Fenced cross-process character handoff Not Started

Objective: Transfer a character between two authoritative processes while preserving one owner and committed economic state.

Scope: Explicit source suspension, checkpoint, one-use destination proof, epoch claim/revocation and destination snapshot for the currently enabled simple character state. Comprehensive advanced-state certification follows separately.

Hard dependencies: BW-027, BW-080

Recommended predecessors: BW-081, BW-087

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. During handoff new source actions stop; pending economic operations settle or reject before the checkpoint, and committed operations remain durable.
  2. Only the bound destination can consume an unexpired one-use proof for its exact character/checkpoint/epochs; replay, wrong destination and stale epoch reject without spawning another owner.
  3. At every failure boundary one process/epoch retains or recovers authority; the source cannot mutate the character or grant rewards after the destination claim.
  4. Destination state preserves location, current build, long cooldowns and the documented enabled-state checkpoint policy, then reconstructs an owner-private snapshot and independently filtered public view.
  5. Source/destination crash, timeout and disconnect scenarios return the character to a known legal authority/location without duplicating inventory or reversing completed trades.

Completion evidence required: Required future proof: two-process fault-injection traces at each handoff boundary, duplicate/expired/wrong-destination proofs, stale-owner reward attempts and inventory/receipt reconciliation across retries.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Reserve scope and epoch contracts early, but postpone distributed handoff until local transitions and durable fencing can be observed. One-owner results are required; message delivery is not described as exactly once.

Related open decisions: Incremental topology and comparable branch orders

Related documentation: Transfer protocol; Durable operations; Disconnect recovery

BW-088 · Versioned deployment, database migration and rollback Not Started

Objective: Deploy and replace the small initial service/world deployment without incompatible state or partial economic rollback.

Scope: Reproducible release artifacts/configuration, safe admission/drain, database migrations, compatibility checks and rollback/reconciliation. Automatic fleet scaling and independent microservices are not required.

Hard dependencies: BW-087

Recommended predecessors: BW-086

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. A release identifies the exact client/server artifacts, configuration, content/rules/database versions and supported compatibility; unsupported peers/content reject before mutation.
  2. Planned maintenance stops or redirects admission, settles/rejects outstanding economic work and reports service availability instead of silently dropping committed outcomes.
  3. A migration records planned/validated/applied/failed state and a declared rollback/reconciliation plan; incompatible or failed migration cannot publish an apparently healthy release.
  4. A deployment and rollback exercise preserves operation receipts and both sides of completed transfers/trades across restart; it never independently rewinds combat and currency ownership.
  5. A fresh environment can reproduce the tested release and health diagnostics; failed startup does not accept economic commands.

Completion evidence required: Required future proof: staged deploy/update/drain/failed-migration/rollback exercises using the real database, artifact/version manifests, compatibility rejection and post-rollback conservation reports.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Use a modest deployable system while interfaces remain separable. Operational reproducibility and rollback are required before expansion; fleet orchestration waits for evidence of need.

Related open decisions: Engine, platform and deployment candidates

Related documentation: Deployment and migration operations; Version change control; Small initial architecture

BW-091 · Content-version migration and readiness admission Not Started

Objective: Safely replace enabled gameplay content without silently repurposing IDs or leaving illegal builds/items.

Scope: Content/rules/database version distinction, affected-record validation, build refund/revalidation and rollout compatibility. Authoring all abilities, recipes and world content belongs to their feature epics.

Hard dependencies: BW-022, BW-024, BW-088

Recommended predecessors: None

Acceptance criteria (AC)

  1. A content release preserves compatible stable IDs and uses explicit migration for changed meaning; deleted IDs cannot silently identify unrelated content.
  2. Changed talent/ability meaning identifies affected builds and either validates them or applies the documented safe refund/revalidation policy atomically.
  3. Affected item/effect records and equipped budgets are checked before admission; incompatible references or unresolved required bindings keep the package/entry disabled.
  4. Numeric balance changes include affected item/build simulations and clear patch notes linked to exact revisions; structural fixture validity cannot mark runtime evidence passed.
  5. A rejected content update retains the prior valid version; rollout/rollback retains durable economic results and produces an inspectable reconciliation record.

Completion evidence required: Required future proof: compatible and breaking content-update fixtures, real migration/refund/revalidation results, invalid-package rejection and staged rollback evidence with affected-record and balance reports.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: The version boundary is established at project admission. Full migration behavior becomes testable after real builds/items exist, before broad live content changes make recovery expensive.

Related open decisions: Runtime bindings and complete release inventory

Related documentation: Content change control; Readiness gates; Content admission
