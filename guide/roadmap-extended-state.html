Phase objective and completion

Prove the remaining state families, local isolation, simple distributed handoff and safe versioned updates.

0/12 complete · 0%

Phase status: Not Started. Complete only when all 12 epics below are Complete with verified evidence.

Family fixtures and initial transfer/update proofs pass; the comprehensive advanced-state regression is BW-089, not inferred here. The combined-mechanic multiplayer/workload probe passes before broad kit certification.

Integrated completion milestone: Bounded state and world evolution proof. Its full prerequisites and integration evidence must also pass; individual components do not prove their integration.

Hard dependencies are required for completion. Recommended predecessors are scheduling preferences. Other phases are not global start barriers. How to read this roadmap.

Epics in this phase

  1. BW-036 · Authorized fishing sessions Not Started

    Resolve fishing catches from a validated interaction instead of a client-selected reward.

  2. BW-038 · Profession mastery and activation changes Not Started

    Apply bounded production choices without refill, rounding or switching exploits.

  3. BW-040 · Owned mobile pets and summon commands Not Started

    Support controlled mobile companions within shared simulation and output budgets.

  4. BW-041 · Bounded static combat deployables Not Started

    Make traps, totems and linked combat objects behave as finite owned effects.

  5. BW-042 · Validated combat forms Not Started

    Change form without duplicating attributes, resources or equipment power.

  6. BW-044 · Persistent delayed-damage debt Not Started

    Prove deferred damage remains owed through build and session changes.

  7. BW-046 · Periodic effect budget transformations Not Started

    Allow extension, spreading and consumption without duplicating remaining periodic output.

  8. BW-096 · Integrated advanced-mechanic multiplayer probe Not Started

    Validate interacting state families and their representative network workload before broad kit certification multiplies them.

  9. BW-081 · Private instance membership and lifecycle Not Started

    Provide isolated group-owned encounter spaces with controlled admission, departure and reset.

  10. BW-082 · Fenced cross-process character handoff Not Started

    Transfer a character between two authoritative processes while preserving one owner and committed economic state.

  11. BW-088 · Versioned deployment, database migration and rollback Not Started

    Deploy and replace the small initial service/world deployment without incompatible state or partial economic rollback.

  12. BW-091 · Content-version migration and readiness admission Not Started

    Safely replace enabled gameplay content without silently repurposing IDs or leaving illegal builds/items.
