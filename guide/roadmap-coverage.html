Open implementation decisions and assumptions

All 29 recorded canonical contract questions are resolved in the current design revision. These items concern future implementation choices, authored content and evidence, with an explicit resolution gate.

Engine, platform and deployment candidates

Open decision: Select and pin a tested Unreal Engine 5 revision, native C++ compiler/toolchain, supported platforms, build and dedicated-server targets and Unicode-data revisions. Inventory the owner's available replication plugins and verify each one's license, compatible engine versions, network capabilities and limits before choosing integration; the supplied MultiplayerSessions Steam plugin has baseline compile/widget checks, with actual networking coverage still unverified. The owner-selected game repository now exists; trusted identity, persistence, hosting and deployment remain future work.

Resolve by: BW-001 demonstrates actual installation, new C++ project and tested build targets; BW-004/BW-013 evaluate builtin replication and compatible owner-provided plugins against connection and disclosure requirements; BW-018/BW-086/BW-088 verify persistence, provider and deployment choices before external play.

Affects BW-001, BW-018, BW-020, BW-086, BW-088, BW-004, BW-013 · Authority and networking; Creation and beginner journey

Capacity, security and recovery budgets

Open decision: Approve concrete per-endpoint lifetimes/limits/retention, expected workload, tick/tail-latency/bandwidth budgets, recovery point/time and release admission envelope. Prototype rates and 20-second grace are starting settings, not established achievements.

Resolve by: Before the relevant security/restore/load epic is Complete; final release cannot accept unnamed or unmeasured targets.

Affects BW-008, BW-019, BW-083, BW-086, BW-087, BW-090, BW-093, BW-094 · Authority and networking; Validation and evidence; Supporting gameplay

Concrete world and starter records

Open decision: Author finite functional maps, appearance assets, starter instructions, NPC kits, loot tables, recovery/exit locations and required XP allocations within existing contracts. No names, lore, branching narrative system or detailed new geography are inferred here.

Resolve by: Representative records before BW-039; full-roster starter and level 11–60 content before BW-052/BW-053, and reviewed release-content manifest before BW-092.

Affects BW-020, BW-033, BW-039, BW-052, BW-053, BW-069, BW-070, BW-074 · Creation and beginner journey; Supporting gameplay; Readiness and remaining work

Runtime bindings and complete release inventory

Open decision: Prototype profiles do not establish the named secondary ability/talent behavior. The supplied 257 recipes and sample items are representative, not the launch inventory. Decide concrete compatible bindings and complete useful content within the documented class/profession scope.

Resolve by: BW-047–051 and BW-062 certify the intended full scope; BW-092 rejects missing required roster/content rather than silently shrinking the game.

Affects BW-047, BW-048, BW-049, BW-050, BW-051, BW-062, BW-091, BW-092 · Execution contracts; Readiness and remaining work; Profession requirements

Prices, sources and conversion graph

Open decision: Concrete vendor values, recipe conversion values, source distributions, starter XP allocation and economic tuning need authoring and tests. Published fees, six bands and profession relevance targets remain prototype constraints, not measured health.

Resolve by: Before enabling the relevant vendor/conversion content; economy/profession measurement in BW-072 and final integration in BW-092.

Affects BW-026, BW-035, BW-037, BW-059, BW-062, BW-072 · Economy; Profession requirements; Item salvage and value constraints

Measured balance, usability and content limits

Open decision: Approve balance deviations and launch content bounds using equal-budget role/build/faction tests, actual progression, supported input devices and accessible visual feedback. Twelve classes, 37 specializations and level cap 60 remain requirements; a smaller technical slice is not permission to omit them.

Resolve by: Representative proof before expansion; BW-071–074 and BW-092 resolve release-blocking findings using documented test standards.

Affects BW-032, BW-052, BW-062, BW-068, BW-070, BW-071, BW-072, BW-073, BW-074, BW-092 · Governing design decisions; Validation and evidence; Interface and accessibility; Readiness and remaining work

Incremental topology and comparable branch orders

Assumption: A small deployment with explicit loading transitions can validate the required game before fleet complexity. Full-state conservative recovery is sufficient under the documented baseline; no exact lost-tick replay, unrestricted rewind or seamless travel requirement is added. Social/profession branches can move earlier if team capacity supports them.

Resolve by: Revisit after BW-083/BW-082 measurements and before BW-090 capacity approval; change ordering while retaining IDs if evidence contradicts the assumption.

Affects BW-012, BW-027, BW-080, BW-081, BW-082, BW-083, BW-089, BW-090 · Authority and networking; Validation and evidence

Concrete item-use timing bindings

Open decision: Authored consumable effect records specify effects, channels and category/recipient restrictions. Any unspecified consumption, interruption/refund and treatment-lock start timing needs an explicit reviewed runtime binding before that item is enabled; do not infer it from animation or let duplicate use choose a favorable policy.

Resolve by: Before BW-075 is Complete; preserve published durations, caps, mode exclusions and authoritative conservation.

Affects BW-075, BW-062 · First Aid effect contracts; Item effect definitions

Rated entry eligibility and normalized build selection

Open decision: Rated combat requires effective level 60 and a 118-point budget, but the reference does not specify minimum actual entry level or how a below-cap entrant selects legal normalized talents, role services and ability unlocks. Decide whether entry requires actual level 60 or supports lower-level entrants with an explicitly reviewed temporary build/ability policy. Preserve the required normalization and ordinary earned progression; this audit selects neither alternative.

Resolve by: Approve before BW-067 can be Complete and before BW-068 admits rated participants. Test admitted-level entry/exit/reconnect, temporary mode selections and unchanged ordinary build/progression; do not infer match initialization or respawn policy from ordinary build application.

Affects BW-067, BW-068, BW-073, BW-094 · Rated normalization; Earned progression and loadouts; Normalized runtime state

Camera, asset conventions and supported visual quality

Open decision: Pin the tested Unreal Engine version and supported platforms, orthographic pitch/framing, zoom, aspect/minimum-window policy, model/material/animation conventions, environment presentation and client performance budgets. These choices require evidence; no fixed dimensions or special rendering technique is implied.

Resolve by: BW-097 verifies the camera/readability and display baseline; BW-098 establishes repeatable asset conventions; BW-099 reviews complete environments; BW-074/BW-090 approve release visual accessibility and performance across supported quality tiers.

Affects BW-097, BW-098, BW-099, BW-028, BW-074, BW-090, BW-094 · Selected 3D pixel-art direction; Rendering, camera and input; Rendering verification

Explicitly deferred or optional scope

  • Neutral/cross-faction exchanges, war logistics, timed auctions and financial instruments are outside the baseline economy; neither faction depends on enemy supply. Faction-local fixed-price listings and buyer requests remain required.
  • Race/faction transfers and class changes are not launch capabilities. Optional cosmetic account collections grant no account-wide combat power or profession throughput.
  • Housing, territory, sieges, authored geography/lore and long offline manufacturing are outside the current documented baseline. This roadmap requires functional maps/assets/content, without inventing worldbuilding.
  • Voice chat and seamless travel are optional, not prerequisites. Explicit transitions and a small deployment satisfy the initial topology; larger raid sizes and fleet growth require authored limits and measurements.
  • No required catalog family is silently deferred beyond the full-game gate. Incomplete bespoke content remains disabled during earlier slices and must be bound/certified for the intended full roster.
