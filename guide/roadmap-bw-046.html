Objective and scope

Allow extension, spreading and consumption without duplicating remaining periodic output.

Scope: Disease harvest/acceleration, Burning consumption and healing-seed maturation/consumption; ordinary ticking is BW-014.

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    Consuming a selected remaining periodic budget removes that source once and cannot preserve future ticks as free output.

  2. AC 2

    Extension/acceleration respects declared total-per-application budgets and fixed baseline tick policies.

  3. AC 3

    Mature healing seeds cannot award both the consumed recovery and a duplicate scheduled tick.

  4. AC 4

    Repeated, expired and immune-target cases preserve caps, owner attribution and resource-generation exclusions.

Verification and blockers

Completion evidence required: Future tick-boundary, simultaneous consume/refresh and repeated-harvest traces with conserved effect budgets.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ticket planning

Before creating or revising tickets, apply the mandatory reuse assessment. Inspect existing owners and evidence, then map every criterion to the additional work and verification required.

Use BW-046 as the parent epic. Link each future ticket to the AC links above and state the evidence it will produce. Split work into demonstrable behaviors; one criterion may require several tickets. Epic completion still requires every criterion, verified evidence and completed hard prerequisites.
