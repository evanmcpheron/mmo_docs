Phase: 7. Player exchanges and profession services · Completion milestone: Transactional services and profession breadth

Objective and scope

Apply owned consumables through bounded item-use and treatment rules.

Scope: Alchemy recovery/defense effects, food preparation, shared cooldown categories and First Aid channels/recipient locks; manufacturing outputs remain with crafting.

Dependencies and downstream work

Hard prerequisites

These epics must be Complete before this epic can be completed or meaningfully verified.

  • BW-017 · Death revival and solo recovery Not Started
  • BW-021 · Inventory wallet and guaranteed delivery Not Started

Recommended predecessors

Scheduling guidance; these are not completion gates.

  • BW-037 · Quoted synchronous personal crafting Not Started

Required by

These epics directly depend on this capability.

  • BW-062 · Six-band profession and output certification Not Started
  • BW-089 · Advanced-state transfer and recovery certification Not Started

Acceptance criteria

  1. AC 1

    Each admitted item-use timing policy commits one owned item and one permitted effect; duplicate requests and concurrent trade/equip use cannot create free or repeated output.

  2. AC 2

    First Aid follows the authored six-second interruptible channel and 60-second recipient treatment lock; damage interruption, concurrent healers and retries cannot bypass the lock or duplicate ticks.

  3. AC 3

    Food/preparation and consumable categories obey their authored caps and fixed level-band effects; old low-band supplies do not scale from a high-level consumer’s maximum resources.

  4. AC 4

    Mode restrictions, invalid targets and unavailable items produce explicit rejection; UI shows timing, shared cooldown and treatment availability before use.

Verification and blockers

Completion evidence required: Executed consumption, interruption, category and concurrent-treatment fixtures with item/receipt and effective-output traces; reviewed timing bindings for item-use policies.

Recorded evidence: None. Required evidence above describes future verification.

Blockers: None recorded; see hard prerequisites.

Ticket planning

Before creating or revising tickets, apply the mandatory reuse assessment. Inspect existing owners and evidence, then map every criterion to the additional work and verification required.

Use BW-075 as the parent epic. Link each future ticket to the AC links above and state the evidence it will produce. Split work into demonstrable behaviors; one criterion may require several tickets. Epic completion still requires every criterion, verified evidence and completed hard prerequisites.

Ordering rationale: Crafting a numerical item record does not prove that consuming it obeys channel, category and recipient restrictions. Validate item use separately before certifying all profession outputs.

Related open decisions: Concrete item-use timing bindings
