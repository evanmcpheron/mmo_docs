    Briarwake Study 01

    This 56-color palette is a proposed reference created for these examples. It is not an owner-approved master palette. Four entries per material provide a common shadow-to-light structure. Materials may share colors in future revisions; a 56-entry library does not mean using all colors on one sprite.

    Keep quiet ground within a narrow value band. Lift actors through silhouette and local contrast rather than saturation alone. Danger uses shape, motion and a clear edge alongside color; never depend solely on red-versus-green. Magical colors should not become ordinary ground noise.

    Material ramps

    Read each ramp from shadow to light. Grass is lower-frequency ground; leaves carry stronger grouped shapes above it. Earth and leather differ in contrast and surface treatment even when their hue families overlap. Metal and water share cool relationships but have different reflection geometry. Fire and magic have restricted bright activation pixels.

    RampProduction treatmentAvoid
    Grass / leavesGrass 2–3 values per tile; leaf highlights join canopy lobesBright isolated stipple
    Bark / woodBroad upright grain on bark; long straight planes on cut woodOne-pixel stripes on every plank
    Stone / brickFlat broken facets; repeated masonry joints align at seamsSoft airbrushed rocks
    Cloth / leatherBroad folds versus firm stitched edgesA highlight for every thread
    MetalHard edge and small bright reflectionLarge white fills
    WaterShort horizontal accents; animated pattern continuityDense white waves on every cell
    Fire / magicBright core at activation; darker recoveryPermanent full-screen brightness

    Load and preserve the palette in Aseprite

    1. Download the local GPL palette. In the palette options, load it; verify the first and last color against this specification. Save a named project palette copy.
    2. Use RGB mode for layered authoring unless an indexed workflow is required. RGB permits deliberate color sampling; check the export’s distinct opaque colors afterward.
    3. In indexed mode, reserve a transparent index and review palette remapping before changing entries. A palette reorder can alter an indexed image if indices are remapped incorrectly.
    4. Use contiguous ramp selections for controlled recolor variants. Preserve light order and material distinctions. Review colors against representative ground.
    5. Do not paste third-party art into the study palette or use color reduction as a substitute for rights clearance. Record any added color as a proposed palette revision.

    Regional and lighting variation

    Forests emphasize leaves, wood and earth; mountains emphasize stone with cooler shadows; marshes use lower-value grass and subdued water; coastlines use pale earth and cool water; settlements warm wood and brick; ruins mute stone beneath leaf accents. These are ramp relationships, not new canonical places.

    Lighting examples reuse one map and alter its presentation. Dusk and rain reduce contrast, night preserves actor edges, snow shifts broad ground values and torchlight introduces a bounded warm area. Derived lighting colors are preview outputs and are exempt from the master-source palette check; do not sample them back into ordinary source sprites.

    Palette acceptance

    Before adopting a new master palette, compare terrain repetition, all skin ramps, cloth/armor differentiation, night readability and danger indicators. Submit a named revision with affected assets, a remap table and rollback plan. Owner approval must name the exact palette version. A documentation commit alone cannot grant that approval.
