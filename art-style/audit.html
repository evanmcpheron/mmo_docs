    License and AI-workflow review

    No license text governing the requested paid character art was available in the inspected tree. Redistribution, public sheet previews, derivative use and AI-assisted workflow permission therefore remain unverified. Do not publish those sheets or feed them to a generation system based on purchase alone. Obtain the actual license revision and, where ambiguous, written rights-holder clarification.

    All new study pixels were generated from first-party integer drawing recipes without ingesting or copying third-party artwork. Technical diagrams and all scene assets are original documentation examples. This does not declare a redistribution license on behalf of any third-party author or imply owner production approval. Repository-wide first-party licensing remains an owner decision.

    Rebuild and evidence commands

    The full commands apply in the complete documentation repository. Focused local authoring can use build_manual_indexes.py --art-only and --art-only --check. Full repository gates remain mandatory in CI. The focused checks do not claim to validate unrelated historical pages.

    python3 tools/create_art_references.py
python3 tools/create_art_references.py --check
python3 tools/build_manual_indexes.py
python3 tools/build_manual_indexes.py --check
python3 tools/validate_documentation.py
python3 tools/validate_art_bible.py
python3 tools/check_phase_integration.py
python3 tools/check_feature_coverage.py
python3 -m unittest discover -s tools/tests -v
