    Regional art direction

    Six environmental families built with common native scale and reusable construction.

    Regional identity without new canon

    These standards organize ancient forests, mountains, frontier settlements, marshlands, coastlines and forgotten ruins. Existing documentation uses Elderwood, Cairnstep, Thornmere, Siltfen, Galehaven and Hollowpeak as authored region references; their historical pages are context, not proof that these new study maps are canonical locations. The new scenes are unnamed layout studies.

    All regions share 16×16 terrain, the same character density and upper-left form shading. Change the dominant material, silhouette rhythm, object ratio and atmosphere. Do not create separate pixel scales for each biome or invent approved settlements, factions or historical events.

    Shared combat-clearance rule

    Preserve quiet ground around actors and attack tells. Tall foreground props belong at boundaries or in deliberate occlusion groups. Dark regions still need readable character edges. Weather must not hide route boundaries or danger. Every region should be reviewed in one-character, party and crowded-objective compositions.

    A region kit starts with base terrain, full binary transition family, one path, water edge where relevant, three major prop silhouettes and a small landmark kit. Add texture variants after those modules connect successfully.

    Reuse before expansion

    The six scenes use the same source atlas and object library. That proves compatible scale and layout. Their specific colors, vegetation mix and architecture remain proposed. Final region approval should replace or refine study tiles while keeping their grid and source/export contracts intact.

    Regional review deliverables

    • Native-size map recipe and enlarged preview, with atlas IDs and named props.
    • Palette relationships and any proposed remap, plus a comparison against the current master palette.
    • Terrain continuity tests, readable hazards and an entrance/landmark plan.
    • At least one meaningful Living Region state change with unchanged boundary coordinates.
    • Crowded character and effects tests; weather and night review where the region requires them.
