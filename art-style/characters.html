    Character anatomy, races and bases

    A reusable approximately 32-pixel humanoid within a shared 64×64 animation cell.

    Construction measurements

    Use the following as proposed construction measurements for new artwork, not measurements of unavailable purchased sprites. The study body’s head occupies about 11×11 pixels including hair. Neck and shoulders lead into a 10–13-pixel torso width; arms are about 3 pixels wide; legs use two separated 3-pixel columns. Hands and feet are compact 2–4-pixel groups. Overall standing height is approximately 32 pixels.

    At this scale, face identity comes from head contour, hair, skin ramp and one- or two-pixel feature placement. Do not add a detailed face that forces an enlarged head or destroys body clearance. Hair volume can extend beyond the body bound, but it must leave room for headwear.

    Base-body contract

    The base body defines contact (32,48), head placement, shoulder and wrist attachment regions and action registration. Document any intentional body movement per frame. Clothes follow that body; they do not recenter the frame. Keep standing feet stable at the contact line, allowing the expected one-pixel walk bob.

    A base must be tested nude or in a neutral underlayer across all facing directions before outfits are commissioned. Check separated limbs in silhouette. Skin palette variants should preserve value order. The blank and study Aseprite templates provide a starting coordinate contract, not approved race anatomy.

    Human, Goblin and Demon evidence

    The inspected game commit contains project configuration and editor integration, but no Human, Goblin or Demon sheets, hairstyles, cloaks, outfits or creator guides. Therefore this edition cannot verify race appearance, body dimensions, anatomy differences, sheet size, frame durations or layer compatibility. No horns, ears, limb proportions or skin colors are presented as measured race facts.

    When the owner supplies licensed local sources, inventory each race’s native canvas, visible bounds, contact, action/facing coverage, palette, layer order and creator notes. Keep paid sheets private until redistribution and AI-workflow terms are checked. Race redesign is a separate approval decision; retain equipment and animation compatibility unless a reviewed new base contract replaces it.

    Character-to-world checks

    Place the body at a doorway, chair, workbench, stair, bridge and tree root at 1×. A head or weapon must not disappear against ordinary ground. Large enemies may exceed the humanoid canvas but retain the same one-pixel density. Avoid enlarging one race by resampling the source.

    Equipment margins must cover attacks and tools without changing origin. If a long spear genuinely exceeds 64×64, propose a larger shared action cell or an independently registered equipment export, with explicit offsets and a tested timeline. Do not crop tips silently.

    Character source checklist

    • 64×64 transparent cell, contact and action-specific bounds recorded.
    • Layer names and attachment regions consistent across all facings.
    • Head, torso, hands, feet and weapon remain distinct at 1×.
    • No substituted diagonal poses or accidental trim offsets.
    • Reference status and race evidence remain explicit.
