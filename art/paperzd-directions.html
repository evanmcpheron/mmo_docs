Four-facing rule

Recommended initial mapping: N→North, NE→North, E→East, SE→East, S→South, SW→South, W→West, NW→West. At a sector boundary keep the previous facing until a small configurable hysteresis is exceeded; idle preserves the last valid facing. Aim may override movement only under a declared action rule. These are design choices, not engine defaults.

One pose clock

UCharacterAppearanceComponent receives a presentation snapshot: action, four-facing, shared sequence/time, appearance revision. The body and supported overlays resolve compatible pose frames together. PaperZD integration is conditional on inspecting its installed source/sequence/AnimBP APIs. Do not name guessed native Blueprint nodes as working APIs.

Gameplay and notifies

GAS/server action windows authorize hits, costs and catches. Animation notifies may play footsteps, a splash or impact visual, but a missing notify must not cancel a committed reward or create a duplicate one. Remote animation consumes authoritative state with local visual interpolation.

Future eight-facing expansion

Create a versioned direction-capability matrix for every body, outfit, hair, cloak, tool and action. New directional frames must exist for compatible combinations before enabling them. Keep four-facing fallback for old appearances. Migrate catalog keys without changing owned item IDs or ability mechanics.

Regression fixture

Test each action with a hat, cloak and long tool; change equipment during idle and interrupt an action mid-frame. Reconnect in another zone. Verify shared pose, stable pivots and no gameplay dependency on the animation graph. True diagonal art, six-facing art and eight-facing art are separate authoring work—not synthetic claims about four sheets.
