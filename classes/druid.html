Wildguard · TANK

Mechanical identity

Enter the tank form, generate Resolve through real defensive work and spend it before large incoming hits.

Resource contract: 100 Resolve; eligible mitigated enemy hit +10, maximum once per second; defensive spend 30; active tank form required; no resource from zero-damage friendly effects.

Approach A

Anchored bear: strong self-defense and controlled area threat while holding position.

Approach B

Mobile guardian: use defensive movement and ally coverage with reduced stationary mitigation.

Real cost of the choice: Anchoring is less flexible; mobile coverage gives up some personal defense. Humanoid utility does not preserve full tank mitigation.

Equipment scaling: Agility. Sustained resource: Mana. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Feral · DPS

Mechanical identity

Build Combo Points through claw attacks and decide between immediate finishing damage and maintained bleeds.

Resource contract: Energy plus five owner Combo Points; Raking Claw +1; Savage Finish consumes 1–5; form changes preserve no additional duplicate resource pool.

Approach A

Bleeding pursuit: maintain bleeds while moving between positional opportunities.

Approach B

Focused predation: spend more points in short direct windows against one target.

Real cost of the choice: Bleeds are slower on fragile enemies; direct windows require uptime and careful resource pooling.

Equipment scaling: Agility. Sustained resource: Energy. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.
