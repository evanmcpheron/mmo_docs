Monk

Class identity, unlocks, specialization loops, contrasting builds and complete node tables.

Both sidesLeather armor3 specializations

Energy regenerates; eligible martial actions generate Chi, which is capped and expires by explicit policy.

Starting primary: Agility. Weakness to retain: Ranged pressure and difficult timing windows.

Reading the catalog

Ability profiles are numerical prototypes. Named secondary behavior and resource hooks still need binding and tests. Free-text intent cannot override the global execution rules. Talents below are not a verified in-game implementation.

Ironbody · TANK

Mechanical identity

Use a defensive stance to delay a bounded part of damage, then spend Balance to reduce the debt; delayed damage is still owed.

Resource contract: 100 Balance; successful defense +10 once per second; 30 Balance clears 25% of current ledger; delay at most 30% of eligible post-absorb damage over six seconds; no re-delaying debt ticks.

Approach A

Debt management: prepare mitigation and clear part of the ledger before a dangerous overlap.

Approach B

Reactive countering: earn Balance through timed defense and accept a smaller delayed-damage pool.

Real cost of the choice: A large ledger risks delayed death; reactive defense is less reliable when timing fails.

Equipment scaling: Agility. Sustained resource: Energy. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Windfist · DPS

Mechanical identity

Alternate distinct martial actions to preserve Flow while generating and spending Chi.

Resource contract: Five Chi; generator +1; finisher cost three; Flow is the last three distinct action families with a six-second window, not a second full energy bar.

Approach A

Sustained combinations: preserve varied chains and distribute spending.

Approach B

Burst sequences: build a short complete sequence and spend heavily on a bounded finisher.

Real cost of the choice: Sustained sequences lose peak burst; burst is punished by interruption and target loss.

Equipment scaling: Agility. Sustained resource: Energy. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Lifewalker · HEALER

Mechanical identity

Use direct recovery and close-range techniques to build Living Chi; choose whether entering melee is safe.

Resource contract: Five Living Chi; effective primary heal +1; eligible martial action +1 only if an ally is injured; major heal costs three; Mana is the sustained resource.

Approach A

Martial healing: gain extra Chi from safe attacks while an ally needs recovery.

Approach B

Protective meditation: heal at short range and preserve a stationary channel for predictable damage.

Real cost of the choice: Martial healing risks melee exposure; meditation loses damage contribution and mobility.

Equipment scaling: Intellect. Sustained resource: Mana. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.
