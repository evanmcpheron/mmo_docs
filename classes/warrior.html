Warrior

Class identity, unlocks, specialization loops, contrasting builds and complete node tables.

Both sidesPlate armor3 specializations

Rage comes from validated martial generators; receiving damage grants no baseline Rage. Guard and other specialization meters follow their explicit resource contracts.

Starting primary: Strength. Weakness to retain: Ranged reach and magical defense.

Reading the catalog

Ability profiles are numerical prototypes. Named secondary behavior and resource hooks still need binding and tests. Free-text intent cannot override the global execution rules. Talents below are not a verified in-game implementation.

Bulwark · TANK

Mechanical identity

Build Guard through actually blocked attacks; spend it on timed mitigation or a counterstrike. Face the next physical hit rather than holding one passive stance indefinitely.

Resource contract: 100 Guard; +10 on a real block or effective interception, once per second; 25 for a defensive spend; lose 10/second after six seconds out of combat.

Approach A

Mobile interception: preserve frontal defense while advancing and use Interpose to protect an ally who cannot move.

Approach B

Static defense: hold a small area, pool Guard and answer a predictable tank strike with a stronger personal response.

Real cost of the choice: Mobile protection loses some stationary mitigation; stationary specialization loses rescue reach. Neither removes baseline tanking.

Equipment scaling: Strength. Sustained resource: Rage. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Armsmaster · DPS

Mechanical identity

Create up to three Openings with deliberate attacks; consume them on a high-value target while using parry windows selectively.

Resource contract: Three Openings per caster on the current target; primary generator adds one; spender uses one to three; eight-second duration, refreshed only by eligible hits.

Approach A

Precision dueling: remain close, build Openings and delay the finisher for an exposed target.

Approach B

Reach and control: spend Openings on line pressure and movement disruption while switching targets.

Real cost of the choice: Focused timing raises priority-target output but reduces multi-target coverage; reach gives up peak single-target pressure.

Equipment scaling: Strength. Sustained resource: Rage. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Berserker · DPS

Mechanical identity

Build Frenzy with connected weapon sequences, then decide between spending it on an area attack and sustaining a risky offensive window.

Resource contract: Five Frenzy; one per eligible weapon action, at most once per second; area spender consumes three; lose one every two seconds after four seconds without a hit.

Approach A

Controlled aggression: smaller incoming-damage penalty and defensive recovery at high Frenzy.

Approach B

Pursuit aggression: chain short legal repositions and spend Frenzy during multi-target pressure.

Real cost of the choice: Safer offense sacrifices peak burst; pursuit needs uptime and takes more damage when careless.

Equipment scaling: Strength. Sustained resource: Rage. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.
