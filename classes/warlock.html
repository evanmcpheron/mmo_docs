Evil side onlyCloth armor3 specializations

Demonologist · DPS

Mechanical identity

Direct one major demon and build Authority through combined actions rather than unattended pet damage.

Resource contract: 100 Authority; owner and demon hit within two seconds grants 10, once per two seconds; combined attack spends 30; one four-capacity demon replaces the lesser summon.

Approach A

Coordinated offense: plan owner/demon attacks and spend Authority in synchronized windows.

Approach B

Protective contract: use the same command budget for capped interception and wards.

Real cost of the choice: Offense sacrifices protection; protection reduces damage. Losing the demon disrupts both but does not remove the owner's basic spell.

Equipment scaling: Intellect. Sustained resource: Mana. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Hellcaller · DPS

Mechanical identity

Build Infernal Debt through attacks, then discharge it while managing a capped nonlethal personal cost.

Resource contract: 100 Debt; ordinary generator +10 once per action; discharge spends 30 or 60; self-payment capped at 10% maximum health and cannot take the caster below 35% maximum health.

Approach A

Controlled furnace: maintain a limited field and repay Debt frequently.

Approach B

Risked discharge: pool Debt for a larger attack with explicit health/defense exposure.

Real cost of the choice: Controlled repayment loses peak burst; larger debt risks death from enemy pressure even though the self-cost itself is nonlethal.

Equipment scaling: Intellect. Sustained resource: Mana. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Pactbinder · DPS

Mechanical identity

Maintain one contract clause; create progress through paid, successful actions and decide when to settle it.

Resource contract: One active clause, five progress; paid eligible action adds one, at most once per second; settlement consumes all; changing clause clears progress; self-damage alone cannot generate more progress.

Approach A

Personal contract: spend more of the contract budget on individual damage.

Approach B

Shared liability: fund capped allied protection and debuffs at a personal-output cost.

Real cost of the choice: Personal output loses support; allied value depends on recipients acting and taking relevant damage.

Equipment scaling: Intellect. Sustained resource: Mana. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.
