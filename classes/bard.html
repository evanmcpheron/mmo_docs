Minstrel · HEALER

Reviewed resource rule: Minstrel healing uses Mana and one three-unit Harmony pool. Balm Chorus spends two Harmony; Encore of Mercy spends three. Maintained healing songs use their authored Mana costs and retain their own durations. Clear Harmony extends those song durations; No Lost Notes reduces their Mana cost. Neither talent adds a Cadence pool or refunds committed channel costs.

Mechanical identity

Maintain up to two ordinary song slots, heal targets actively and spend Harmony on group recovery without requiring precise audio timing.

Resource contract: Three Harmonies; +1 on an effective primary heal, at most once per second; group recovery consumes two and finale three. Mana is primary; Cadence is the performance meter.

Approach A

Moving chorus: move with allies, use short restorative phrases and frequent song transitions.

Approach B

Prepared haven: establish longer recovery patterns and hold an emergency finale.

Real cost of the choice: Mobile healing trades peak sustained throughput; prepared healing loses value when allies scatter.

Equipment scaling: Intellect. Sustained resource: Mana. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Skald · DPS

Mechanical identity

Generate Cadence in melee, then choose personal finishing damage or a temporary group enhancement.

Resource contract: 100 Cadence; Iron Verse +10 on contact; War Chant upkeep four/second, Thunderbeat cost 25, finisher 40; strongest-category group buff only.

Approach A

Solo skald: more personal weapon output and reduced allied amplification.

Approach B

Battle chorus: spend more time and Cadence on measurable ally enhancement.

Real cost of the choice: Personal damage scales less with coordinated allies; group support performs poorly when allies cannot act. Neither route requires another player to complete solo content.

Equipment scaling: Agility. Sustained resource: Cadence. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Virtuoso · DPS

Mechanical identity

Build a short sequence of offensive note families, then resolve it into a bounded sonic effect.

Resource contract: Store the last three qualifying note-family IDs; repeated identical notes do not advance the sequence; resolving a finale clears it; maximum one weakened echo per original attack.

Approach A

Focused solo: narrow line attacks and direct single-target final notes.

Approach B

Echo control: use a field and disruptive notes to support clustered combat.

Real cost of the choice: Narrow attacks lose coverage; echo fields need positioning and cannot recursively repeat their own damage.

Equipment scaling: Intellect. Sustained resource: Cadence. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.
