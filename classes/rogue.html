Class identity, unlocks, specialization loops, contrasting builds and complete node tables.

Both sidesLeather armor3 specializations

Energy regenerates over time; combo points are per-target or class-state by explicit spec policy.

Starting primary: Agility. Weakness to retain: Area damage exposure and vulnerability when pinned.

Reading the catalog

Ability profiles are numerical prototypes. Named secondary behavior and resource hooks still need binding and tests. Free-text intent cannot override the global execution rules. Talents below are not a verified in-game implementation.

Assassin · DPS

Mechanical identity

Prepare a poison/positioning window and spend Combo Points on a timed finisher rather than attacking from stealth indefinitely.

Resource contract: Five Combo Points on the owner, not a target; eligible generator +1; finisher spends 1–5. Stealth itself grants no points and cannot be retoggled in combat to farm an opener.

Approach A

Poison attrition: maintain poison, accept slower damage and use defensive re-entry windows.

Approach B

Opening burst: commit positional attacks and finish during a short marked weakness.

Real cost of the choice: Poison pressure is slower; burst is weaker when forced out of position or its opening is denied.

Equipment scaling: Agility. Sustained resource: Energy. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Duelist · DPS

Mechanical identity

Alternate attacks with counters to build Advantage; spend it only when the exchange creates an opportunity.

Resource contract: Five Advantage; +2 from a successful active parry or +1 from two distinct qualifying attacks, once per second; finisher consumes three; no gain from self-created attacks.

Approach A

Counter specialist: read incoming attacks and turn a successful parry into a finisher.

Approach B

Mobile exchanges: build through varied weapon actions and short positional changes.

Real cost of the choice: Counter play loses value without incoming eligible attacks; mobile play gives up the strongest counter reward.

Equipment scaling: Agility. Sustained resource: Energy. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Saboteur · DPS

Mechanical identity

Place a small device set, control an approach and trigger it deliberately rather than spawning unrestricted traps.

Resource contract: Three active devices, separate from cosmetic objects but within the class combat-object ceiling; placement cost 20 Energy, trigger cost 10; each device triggers once.

Approach A

Prepared field: deploy fewer stronger devices and wait for enemies to enter the planned area.

Approach B

Mobile sabotage: use shorter-lived devices and reposition between activations.

Real cost of the choice: Prepared damage is avoidable by moving away; mobile devices have lower individual impact.

Equipment scaling: Agility. Sustained resource: Energy. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.
