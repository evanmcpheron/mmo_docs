Stormcaller · DPS

Mechanical identity

Alternate elemental attacks and release Stormcharge through a line, field or priority-target strike.

Resource contract: 100 Stormcharge; alternating eligible elements +15, repeated element +5; spender 30; major discharge 60; one gain per root action, not per target.

Approach A

Focused discharge: pool for a high-value target with limited chaining.

Approach B

Distributed storm: spend through fields and capped chains against groups.

Real cost of the choice: Focus loses area output; field damage requires enemies to remain inside the area.

Equipment scaling: Intellect. Sustained resource: Mana. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Spiritmender · HEALER

Mechanical identity

Create temporary links with direct heals and choose a limited path for later recovery.

Resource contract: Maximum four linked allies per caster; a link lasts ten seconds; chain jumps within three units, maximum four targets; no target visited twice in one chain.

Approach A

Close kinship: heal a compact group through efficient short chain jumps.

Approach B

Focused memory: spend links on protection and direct recovery of threatened recipients.

Real cost of the choice: Chains lose value when allies spread; focused protection reduces group throughput.

Equipment scaling: Intellect. Sustained resource: Mana. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Totemist · DPS

Mechanical identity

Allocate four static-object capacity units and define at most three links; placement and timing determine damage and support.

Resource contract: Four total combat totems including shared-class totems; at most three explicit links; placement cost 4% mana; relocation follows its own cooldown and cannot duplicate old objects.

Approach A

Anchored constellation: retain a stable layout for sustained area effects.

Approach B

Portable pattern: relocate a smaller layout more often with lower peak output.

Real cost of the choice: Anchoring loses value against movement; portability sacrifices stationary uptime and throughput.

Equipment scaling: Intellect. Sustained resource: Mana. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.
