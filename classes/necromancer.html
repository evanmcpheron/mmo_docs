Necromancer

Class identity, unlocks, specialization loops, contrasting builds and complete node tables.

Evil side onlyCloth armor3 specializations

Soul Essence is earned from applied abilities and capped events, never unlimited disposable minion loops.

Starting primary: Intellect. Weakness to retain: Minion disruption and ramp-up time.

Reading the catalog

Ability profiles are numerical prototypes. Named secondary behavior and resource hooks still need binding and tests. Free-text intent cannot override the global execution rules. Talents below are not a verified in-game implementation.

Gravecaller · DPS

Mechanical identity

Allocate one summon-capacity pool and command coordinated attacks; resources come from successful combat, not dismiss/resummon loops.

Resource contract: Four capacity units; elite costs four, lesser costs one; all sources share the same aggregate pet budget. Commands cost 20 Soul Essence; basic owner attacks generate 10, once per action.

Approach A

Single sovereign: one elite controlled servant, fewer commands and concentrated target pressure.

Approach B

March of bones: up to four lesser servants with divided output and coordinated positional coverage.

Real cost of the choice: Elite control is vulnerable to disabling one pet; a squad costs more attention and has weaker individuals, not four full pet budgets.

Equipment scaling: Intellect. Sustained resource: Mana. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Plaguebringer · DPS

Mechanical identity

Maintain diseases, collect bounded Contagion from actual ticks and choose between extending, spreading and consuming them.

Resource contract: 100 Contagion; +5 from a qualifying disease tick, global owner limit once per second; spread costs 25; acceleration costs 30; harvest consumes the selected target's remaining disease budget once.

Approach A

Patient decay: longer diseases with preserved total-per-application budgeting and lower maintenance frequency.

Approach B

Rapid infection: shorter diseases and faster damage realization at greater mana and refresh pressure.

Real cost of the choice: Long effects lose value on short-lived targets; rapid infection is more demanding and easier to exhaust.

Equipment scaling: Intellect. Sustained resource: Mana. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Soulreaper · DPS

Mechanical identity

Mark one enemy, build Essence through direct and channel attacks, then choose a spender or defensive reserve.

Resource contract: 100 Soul Essence shared with class; marked direct hit +10, channel tick +5; ordinary spender 30; major spender 60; no resources from self-damage or friendly summons.

Approach A

Repeated extraction: smaller frequent spends and controlled life drain.

Approach B

Planned requiem: pool Essence for a telegraphed finisher while retaining enough for a ward.

Real cost of the choice: Frequent spending loses burst; pooling risks interruption and reduces immediate self-protection.

Equipment scaling: Intellect. Sustained resource: Mana. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.
