Cleric

Class identity, unlocks, specialization loops, contrasting builds and complete node tables.

Good side onlyMail armor3 specializations

Devotion is earned by valid healing, protection and sacred offensive interactions; no out-of-combat farming.

Starting primary: Intellect. Weakness to retain: Heavy physical pressure and spell interruption.

Reading the catalog

Ability profiles are numerical prototypes. Named secondary behavior and resource hooks still need binding and tests. Free-text intent cannot override the global execution rules. Talents below are not a verified in-game implementation.

Execution rules · 118-point progression · Open gates

High Priest · HEALER

Mechanical identity

Use inexpensive direct healing for routine damage, preserve Devotion for group recovery and keep emergency healing available.

Resource contract: Five Devotion; one per effective heal of a different ability family from the previous qualifying heal; spend three on group healing; Mana remains the primary budget.

Approach A

Efficient recovery: use varied small heals and time the larger prayer for predictable group damage.

Approach B

Emergency recovery: hold resources and a burst heal for a threatened target, accepting higher mana use.

Real cost of the choice: Efficient preparation has less instant recovery; emergency specialization is less sustainable.

Equipment scaling: Intellect. Sustained resource: Mana. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Oracle · HEALER

Mechanical identity

Place wards before damage and earn Foresight only when they prevent real damage; spend it on the next predicted danger.

Resource contract: Five Foresight; one per consumed shield event above 1% recipient maximum health, once per second; spend three; no resource for unused or refreshed wards.

Approach A

Concentrated forecasting: protect one marked recipient with larger timed wards.

Approach B

Distributed forecasting: divide protection among several allies before an area pulse.

Real cost of the choice: Single-target wards waste value when damage changes targets; distributed wards are weaker on a tank spike.

Equipment scaling: Intellect. Sustained resource: Mana. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Inquisitor · DPS

Mechanical identity

Apply evidence marks through sacred attacks, then choose when to consume them for a verdict.

Resource contract: Three evidence marks per caster/target; primary direct hit adds one; finisher consumes all; 12-second expiry.

Approach A

Sustained testimony: keep marks active and use measured single-target casts.

Approach B

Area testimony: use a witness field and consume marks on clustered opponents.

Real cost of the choice: Sustained damage has slower burst; area specialization loses efficiency on isolated enemies.

Equipment scaling: Intellect. Sustained resource: Mana. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.
