Oathkeeper · TANK

Mechanical identity

Generate Oathmarks through real protection and spend them on threat or mitigation; place defensive zones where the group can actually use them.

Resource contract: Five Oathmarks; one for an eligible block/prevention event, once per second; spend two; no marks from standing idle in a zone.

Approach A

Area protection: stay within a compact Vigil Circle and plan damage-reduction windows.

Approach B

Moving protection: redirect capped damage and move protection between allies instead of defending one location.

Real cost of the choice: Large area efficiency declines when the party moves; mobile rescue reduces personal stationary mitigation.

Equipment scaling: Strength. Sustained resource: Mana. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Crusader · DPS

Mechanical identity

Build Conviction and target Seals with weapon actions, then consume Seals on a prepared finisher.

Resource contract: 100 Conviction shared with class; +10 eligible generator; three target Seals maximum; finisher spends 30 Conviction and available Seals.

Approach A

Single-target verdict: maintain one marked opponent and concentrate resource spending.

Approach B

Sweeping judgment: spread marks through a cone and divide a spender across several opponents.

Real cost of the choice: Target focus loses cleave; spreading loses priority-target peak damage and takes setup.

Equipment scaling: Strength. Sustained resource: Mana. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Justicar · HEALER

Mechanical identity

Alternate targeted recovery with optional close-range holy attacks; reserve Testament for expensive recovery windows.

Resource contract: Five Testament; one from an effective primary heal or eligible attack while an ally is injured, once per second; spend three on group recovery. Mana sustains healing.

Approach A

Melee-assisted healing: earn Testament from safe weapon contact between heals.

Approach B

Ranged protection: remain with vulnerable allies and build through effective healing instead.

Real cost of the choice: Melee contribution risks positional exposure; ranged play gives up martial damage and accelerated resource generation.

Equipment scaling: Intellect. Sustained resource: Mana. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.
