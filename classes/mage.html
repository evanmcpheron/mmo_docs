Pyromancer · DPS

Mechanical identity

Build Heat while applying Burning; decide between consuming Heat now and preserving setup for a later detonation.

Resource contract: 100 Heat; Kindling Spear +10, Flash Ember +5 on valid hits; ordinary spender costs 30; major finisher consumes available Heat above 30; no gain from consumed remaining DoT damage.

Approach A

Ignition burst: consume Heat and selected Burning on one priority target; reapply the burn afterward.

Approach B

Sustained combustion: preserve Burning and spend Heat on limited spreading/area pressure.

Real cost of the choice: Detonation creates stronger short windows but downtime afterward; sustained play needs targets to live and remain reachable.

Equipment scaling: Intellect. Sustained resource: Mana. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Frostbinder · DPS

Mechanical identity

Apply Chill, then choose between consuming it for damage and maintaining space with slows and areas.

Resource contract: Five Chill per caster/target; primary frost hit adds one, at most once per action; finisher consumes three; ten-second expiry; root follows global diminishing returns.

Approach A

Focused shatter: consume Chill frequently for direct damage against a priority target.

Approach B

Zone control: retain Chill pressure and invest in larger, weaker control fields.

Real cost of the choice: Shatter loses persistent control; zoning loses immediate single-target output. Bosses receive the authored damage alternative instead of roots.

Equipment scaling: Intellect. Sustained resource: Mana. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Arcanist · DPS

Mechanical identity

Build Arcane Charges with Glyph Bolt and trade burst against rapidly increasing mana cost when spending charges.

Resource contract: Four Arcane Charges; Glyph Bolt +1; Charge Lance consumes selected 1–4 charges; damage 0.8P +0.35P per charge, mana cost 3% +2% per charge; charges expire after ten seconds out of combat.

Approach A

Charged burst: accumulate four charges, then release Charge Lance from a prepared position.

Approach B

Efficient circulation: spend one or two charges, recover mana between windows and favor limited mobile follow-ups.

Real cost of the choice: Maximum charges improve burst but consume mana disproportionately; frequent smaller spends reduce peak output.

Equipment scaling: Intellect. Sustained resource: Mana. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.
