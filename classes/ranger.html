Ranger

Class identity, unlocks, specialization loops, contrasting builds and complete node tables.

Both sidesMail armor3 specializations

Focus regenerates and is restored by certain aimed attacks; companions use bounded server AI.

Starting primary: Agility. Weakness to retain: Tight terrain and close-range pressure.

Reading the catalog

Ability profiles are numerical prototypes. Named secondary behavior and resource hooks still need binding and tests. Free-text intent cannot override the global execution rules. Talents below are not a verified in-game implementation.

Marksman · DPS

Mechanical identity

Build Aim while stationary and consume it on measured shots; movement is permitted but has an explicit opportunity cost.

Resource contract: Three Aim; gain one per uninterrupted second preparing an attack, maximum three; moving removes one per second unless a talent permits limited retention; no random accuracy bonus.

Approach A

Anchored marksman: hold position for stronger deliberate shots and planned narrow volleys.

Approach B

Mobile precision: preserve a limited amount of Aim through brief repositioning at reduced stationary bonuses.

Real cost of the choice: Anchoring is vulnerable to hazards and melee pressure; mobility sacrifices peak stationary damage.

Equipment scaling: Agility. Sustained resource: Focus. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Beastmaster · DPS

Mechanical identity

Build Bond with successful owner/pet coordination and spend it on commands, while retaining functional owner attacks.

Resource contract: 100 Bond; +10 when both owner and pet hit an eligible target within two seconds, once per two seconds; command costs 25; one active four-capacity companion.

Approach A

Offensive bond: pet and owner focus one priority target with timed combined attacks.

Approach B

Guarding bond: use more commands for pet interception and protection while reducing direct pet offense.

Real cost of the choice: Offense loses defensive command availability; guarding loses damage and cannot replace a player tank.

Equipment scaling: Agility. Sustained resource: Focus. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.

Pathfinder · DPS

Mechanical identity

Combine a ranged attack, a close-range technique and traps with legal repositioning; select safe routes rather than permanent kiting immunity.

Resource contract: Three Waypoint charges; gain one after moving at least two units between two eligible attacks, once per three seconds; spend one for an enhanced follow-up; ordinary movement alone grants none.

Approach A

Ambush route: plan traps and use short melee pressure when the target reaches them.

Approach B

Running route: prioritize mobile shots and defensive waypoints.

Real cost of the choice: Ambush requires preparation; running shots lose stationary and trap burst value.

Equipment scaling: Agility. Sustained resource: Focus. Class, specialization and talent bonuses use the same modifier order, rather than separate additive implementations.
