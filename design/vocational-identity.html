Meaningful specialization without an irreversible beginner mistake.

Character creation contract

Committed requirement: one and only one primary vocation is selected for every playable character. The creator shows an active choice’s typical actions, pace/difficulty, products, world contribution, combat synergy and boundaries. Unsupported exclusive costumes are not part of the promise.

Committed requirement: select one permanent combat class through UCombatDisciplineDefinition and one permanent faction separately from the changeable vocation. Every available class supports every available vocation. Combat level/XP, class abilities and purchased combat talents cannot change when retraining a vocation.

Base proficiency versus specialization

All characters can fight, explore, complete quests, gather common materials, fish at basic spots and use essential NPC services. The chosen vocation controls advanced techniques, superior outcome control, specialist recipe permissions and its own quest/mastery graph. NPC commissions provide required cross-vocation goods at attainable authored prices.

Recommended initial change policy

QuestionProposed explicit policy
When may a character change?After the introduction and three varied vocation contracts. This is an activity commitment, not a real-time daily/login gate.
Where?An appropriate trainer; the server validates range, eligibility and pending transactions.
What is retained?All items, permanent quest outcomes, faction allegiance, Living Regions credit and combat progression remain. Every trained vocation retains its own XP, derived level, mastery XP/points, purchased skills/talents, learned recipes, techniques and accomplishments.
What is inactive?Advanced abilities/techniques requiring the old active vocation are disabled; items remain subject to their ordinary use/bind rules.
What is newly granted?A never-trained vocation gets one Level-1 record at its defined initial XP and introductory mentor access. A returning vocation restores its saved record, not Level 1. Starter-kit and one-time unlock/mastery outcomes cannot replay.
Cost?An explicit affordable retraining contract and a published currency fee; exact fee is a tuning decision still to author. No punitive item wipe.
Cooldown?Complete three varied contracts in the new vocation before another switch. No mandatory calendar wait.
Atomic validation?No pending trade/craft/transfer or unresolved durable work. Commit active VocationId, keyed progression, any existing authored retraining debit, change receipt and outbox together. Rebuild runtime permissions from that committed revision before enabling vocation actions; runtime handles are not stored in the transaction.
Other characters?Additional slots may offer another vocation, but alts are never required for the story or mastery.

Migration and exploit tests

Switch away and back, reconnect during confirmation, replay the change and try an old specialist recipe. Old progress remains; inactive permissions cannot be used; free kit/recipe grants do not repeat. A content update must not reinterpret a stored vocation ID by array position.

Remaining decisions

The exact fee, contract list, full mastery-node graphs and real pacing need authoring and playtesting. The initial policy is a recommended choice, not a preexisting engine feature or a user-approved final balance table.

Server-authoritative ChangeVocation contract

ChangeVocation(Envelope, TargetVocationId, TrainerId) is a proposed Briarwake operation routed by the existing owned controller/NPC service through UDurableStateClient; it is not an Unreal API or a new transaction framework. Envelope uses OperationType=ChangeVocation, RequestId, authenticated character scope, LeaseEpoch, ExpectedRevisions and ContentVersion. Its semantic digest includes TargetVocationId, TrainerId, the decision revisions and pinned content, not renewed transport credentials.

Keep the established provisional trainer policy above: introduction and three varied contracts, an appropriate trainer, the authored affordable retraining contract/fee, and three varied contracts before another switch. Exact fee/content remains unapproved tuning. Do not invent another fee, cooldown, penalty, prerequisite or human-player dependency. Missing required authored policy/definitions blocks that integration test rather than making up values.

  1. Authenticate the character; reject impersonation. Look up (character scope, ChangeVocation, RequestId) before current trainer/eligibility/revision checks. Return an authorized matching committed receipt unchanged; a changed semantic payload returns RequestIdentityConflict. A fresh lease may read an older receipt; it cannot authorize a new write with an old epoch.
  2. For a new mutation, validate the current authoritative session lease and live trainer interaction/range, the existing retraining eligibility and fee policy, and the requested available UVocationDefinition. Same active target returns AlreadyActive with no fee, progression change or repeated grants. Prepare required versioned definitions before entering the commit boundary.
  3. Within the existing transaction lock/compare the character and any affected fee aggregate in deterministic order. Recheck LeaseEpoch, ExpectedRevisions, content, trainer authorization and eligibility. Reject IncompatiblePendingOperation for pending trade/craft/transfer or unresolved value/XP work; do not copy a snapshot while another durable result is unknown.
  4. Stage the full next Record_Character, preserving Progression.Combat and every existing Progression.Vocations entry. If TargetVocationId has never been trained in a validated schema, add one entry at InitialXP with derived Level 1 and defined initial values. Otherwise reuse its saved entry byte-for-byte; do not rerun its starter or earned unlock grants.
  5. Set only VocationId to the target. Compute the target permission plan from its committed-history candidate and authored definitions. Validate unique grants and preserve class abilities/talents, faction, inventory items, permanent quest outcomes and Living Regions contributions. The only permitted unrelated debit is the already-authored retraining fee, if applicable.
  6. Commit the staged record, one Revision increment, any existing authored debit, permanent domain uniqueness guards, Record_CommandReceipt and outbox atomically. The receipt records old/new vocation IDs and exact committed revisions. No receipt, fee, history or active selector may commit alone.
  7. After commit, rebuild only vocation-sourced permissions/modifiers on the server from the new revision; gate vocation commands until hydration is complete. Publish the permitted public identity subset and owner-only histories via the existing outbox/projection route. Clients ignore older state revisions; an old receipt is historical evidence, not a command to restore its old active vocation.

Active permissions, independent awards and recovery

LearnedRecipeIds, purchased skills/talents, techniques and accomplishments stay in each inactive entry. Specialist availability requires both current VocationId and the newly active saved progression/definition prerequisites. Remove or disable only handles/modifiers sourced exclusively from the former vocation; preserve class/equipment/general-activity grants. Deduplicate by stable grant source and authored ability/effect identity. Widgets and copied client permission lists are never authority.

Revalidate active vocation permission on authoritative ability activation and crafting acceptance/commit, including delayed requests with old UI or grant handles. Switching cannot make an old specialist recipe legal. Preserve general gathering, basic fishing, combat and essential NPC alternatives available to every character. Rebuilds are deterministic set reconciliation, not additive reward delivery.

CombatXP and targeted VocationXP are independently authored, validated and stored. Quest rewards may specify both separately under one unique completion transaction. Purchased combat talents spend only combat points; vocation purchases spend only that vocation’s mastery points. An accepted activity names its target VocationId and content/revision context; serialize/reconcile its award before switching, never redirect delayed XP to a different active vocation.

Before commit an interruption leaves the original active vocation and histories intact. After commit, a lost response/reconnect/restart resolves through the same receipt and outbox. Repeated delivery cannot grant starter items, mastery points or modifiers again. Two competing changes with the same expected Revision yield one commit and one StaleRevision rejection; no two active vocations. Replaying an earlier accepted change after a later switch returns its original receipt without changing current state.

Respawn, logout/reconnect and per-character zone transfer hydrate all vocation histories from Record_Character, then rebuild the active permission set once. Fence the old zone and reject late commands under its old epoch. Never save progression in the Pawn, UI, Data Asset or a second progression owner.

Permanent allegiance alongside vocation histories

FactionId and CombatDisciplineId are permanent; VocationId is the one changeable active vocation. Previously trained vocation histories remain intact. The faction catalog now has stable entries; its political/organization standings are not vocation mastery.
