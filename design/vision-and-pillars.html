Vision and balanced pillars

Design each feature to connect at least two pillars without forcing every player into every activity.

Five connected pillars

PillarImmediate valueConnection
Combat progressionReadable action challenges, gear choices and combat talentsSalvage/defense supplies profession and region objectives.
World developmentPermanent authored stages and useful servicesDifferent vocation products, clues and encounters advance shared projects.
ExplorationClues, paths, discoveries and original placesUseful sites, ingredients, lore and threat information support other activities.
Gathering and craftingMeaningful material/technique choices in short varied loopsEquipment, food, remedies and supports change outing preparation and world results.
Social playCooperation, sharing, identity and recognitionParties, trade and guild projects enrich existing access rather than gate it.

Avoid compulsory chores

No mandatory daily attendance, punitive offline crop decay, idle helper farming, player-only essential service, vocation-only critical quest ingredient or population quota may block the main route. A calm session may still include decisions and movement; combat is not required in every single five-minute activity.

What changes and what does not

A permanent bridge changes traversal and services. Regional danger can renew through explicitly authored events. Personal discovery remains individually meaningful after shared progress. Do not implement unbounded terrain simulation or erase major improvements to force repetition.

Quality measures

Observe time to first meaningful choice, variety of moment-to-moment activity, clarity of failure, remaining personal effort, solo completion rate and use of voluntary cooperation. Record results separately at 1/2/5/10/30 connected clients. The session-duration examples are targets to test.

Production boundary

Create the smallest connected playable route before producing six regions. All five vocations remain final design requirements; showing three early prototype choices is honest staging, not cancellation of the other two.

Planned game behavior is not runtime evidence. Read sources and limitations.
