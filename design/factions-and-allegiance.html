Taxonomy and source of truth

Authored documentation contracts. Selection flags describe the requested launch configuration, not a running server. Deployment remains blocked until cooked-content and solo-game checks pass.

Political allegiance is mandatory and permanent. Civic reputation, personal faction standing, regional pressure and hostile NPC affiliation are separate facts with separate owners.

Hearthward League

A human-led federation of frontier towns: defended homes, restored roads and open trade. Its survey charters can dispossess earlier occupants and treat sacred places as unused land.

Stable ID: faction.hearthward-league. Join the Thornmere road effort, investigate a missing caravan, negotiate a sacred crossing, contribute to North Road, choose a personal settlement policy and uncover Covenant interference at the Split Bell.

Briarbound Clans

Woodland guardians and ancestral communities: stewardship, negotiated access and protection of sacred places. Blanket exclusion can leave neighboring families without winter supplies.

Stable ID: faction.briarbound-clans. An apprentice steward at Mosswake Sanctuary finds a waystone survey cut across protected roots. Mentor Ashren asks who may consent to passage. Preserve the grove or negotiate supervised access; converge on the Bellroot warnings. Concept only: production quests, dialogue, map placements and mentor art are not authored.

Ironfang Warbands

Orc-like martial clans seek political recognition and ancestral territory. Korra advocates witnessed agreements; aggressive captains favor reprisals. Neither ancestry nor species determines moral worth.

Stable ID: faction.ironfang-warbands. At Stonewake Camp near Stonehollow, Korra asks a new witness to recover ancestral boundary stones before an aggressive captain retaliates against a caravan. Recognition versus reprisal leads toward the Quiet Foundry inscriptions. Concept only: production quests, dialogue, map placements and mentor art are not authored.

Tidebound Compact

Independent coastal communities defend maritime autonomy, navigation and negotiated trade. Unrestricted private commerce can evade responsibility for inland damage.

Stable ID: faction.tidebound-compact. At Galehaven Free Quay, Sera asks a new navigator to investigate disputed salvage from the old sea road. Free passage versus collective stewardship leads to the Stormglass Beacon and common waystone mystery. Concept only: production quests, dialogue, map placements and mentor art are not authored.
