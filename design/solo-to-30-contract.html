Solo-to-30 parity specification

Preserve essential access and stable credited progress while making cooperation useful.

What parity does and does not mean

Committed requirement: one human can complete the required narrative, essential crafting tiers, profession mastery and critical regional improvements. Comparable personal time-to-value is the target; identical completion speed and identical social experience are not promised.

NPC baseline supplies, commissions, alternate objectives and constrained mechanic helpers fill real dependencies. Optional guild chat or player trade naturally requires other people; the story must not require successfully performing those social actions.

Recommended population-sensitive project model

Separate essential project work from optional reinforcements. EssentialTarget = 12 illustrative contribution units, fixed and achievable through every character’s available routes. OptionalTarget(n) = 8 + 2 × ceil(log2(n + 1)), where n is clamped to 1–30 engaged distinct accounts from a bounded 24-hour server window.

At stage activation, persist n, policy version, EssentialTarget, OptionalTarget and objective conversion rules. Never retune an active stage because of logins/logouts. More accounts cannot lower a target. There is no deadline or offline decay; optional reinforcement remains finite and can also be finished alone.

An engaged account must have completed multiple distinct eligible actions; repeated logins, chat or movement alone do not count. Count once per account, not per character. This reduces simple alt inflation but is not proof against multi-account abuse; record abuse tests and operational limits.

Personal effort and late arrival

The personal-value milestone is four varied accepted contribution units in the test model, independent of live population. After the permanent project closes, a separately authored maintenance/research contract offers equivalent personal vocation/reputation value without undoing construction. A stale donation to a completed stage rejects before debit; the player reviews the new objective rather than being silently charged for a different one.

Counterexamples and protections

CounterexampleRequired handling
Log in many accounts to change the current goalNo effect: active target is persisted and frozen.
Raise n before activationOptional target can only rise, never fall; essential target stays fixed.
Everyone leaves a busy-stage projectNo lost credit, deadline or permanent decay; remaining objectives retain an absolute solo route.
Project completes before a new player contributesOffer separate personal contracts and discoveries; never re-close the bridge.
A helper completes content while the player is idleStop assistance/eligibility after explicit inactivity policy; no independent helper reward generator.
Adding players changes a boss mid-attackChoose an authored mechanics variant at a safe encounter boundary; never rewrite mechanics in the middle of a cast.
An essential ingredient is specialist-onlyProvide an accessible commission/base-skill/quest alternative and prove it in a one-human fixture.

What the included simulation proves

tools/simulate_population_scaling.py verifies only integer targets, monotonicity, frozen-stage behavior and illustrative progress arithmetic. It does not simulate human enjoyment, AI, network load, UE performance or measured session durations. Real timings and population capacity remain Not run.
