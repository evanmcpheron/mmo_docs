Identity, responsibilities and boundaries

Committed combined responsibility: Assess an eligible mature stand → select timber → craft a support → restore a grove or reinforce a roadside structure.

Recommended mentor: Orris Vale, grove and bridge steward. This is original proposed lore, not a supplied NPC asset.

Choose one primary vocation at character creation. Base fighting, exploring, common gathering, basic fishing and essential NPC services remain available to everyone.

Short session: 5–10 minute target

Assess an eligible mature stand → select timber → craft a support → restore a grove or reinforce a roadside structure.

Select straight-grain timber for structural reliability or flexible wood for an authored bow/trap recipe. Restoration decisions preserve a viable future resource site.

Pacing is a playtest target. Record movement, at least two distinct interactions and the player’s decision; do not claim measured duration.

Expedition: 15–30 minute target

Investigate a corrupted grove, collect appropriate timber, choose bridge or defensive supports and protect a restoration crew through a bounded encounter.

Use the same connected starter route with authored objective/location variation. Risk rewards useful alternatives, not the only required profession tier.

Mastery and skill branches

Recover regional joinery patterns, restore authored groves and complete the ridge-watch construction sequence. Do not require unrestricted terrain editing.

  • Assess: recognize usable grades and protected stands.
  • Join: select support/bow/furniture techniques.
  • Restore: recover bounded groves and fortify eligible road projects.

Combat connection and calmer path

Create bows, traps and defensive supports; join forest-route protection. Traps use normal authoritative encounter/loot credit.

Assess and harvest an approved mature stand, craft furniture/supports and restore a designated grove stage without a defense encounter.

Definition and transaction wiring

Character record owns active vocation and its mastery ledger. Authoritative outing/production receipts advance it. Combat XP, vocation XP and region reputation are never inferred from the same untyped counter. Advanced permissions revalidate the currently active vocation.

Contribution to the world

Bridge timbers, watchpost supports, grove restoration and route defense are distinct accepted contribution types.

One human can complete every essential milestone through personal objectives, base proficiency and affordable NPC alternatives. Human specialists improve choice and efficiency rather than mandatory access.

Failure and recovery

Protected saplings cannot be harvested; shared restoration permissions prevent a rival overwriting progress. Replanting is an authored state change, not a real-time ecosystem simulation.

Persist the operation receipt in the same transaction as its effects. On a lost response, query or retry the same request identity. A successful receipt returns the original result; a changed payload under that identity is rejected. A UI timeout is not proof of a database rollback.
