Engineering

Purpose, supply chain, mastery choices, level-band relevance and representative recipes.

ManufacturingSkill 1–30030 mastery pointsBoth factions

Bounded combat consumables, attachment configurations, profession tools and field stations.

Purpose and interdependence

Input chain: Mining and Forestry, directly or through trade.

Meaningful specialization: Devices provide limited consumable actions; attachments redistribute reserved item budget; field services improve out-of-combat convenience. All users obey shared cooldowns and rated-PvP restrictions.

A character may hold two manufacturing and two gathering professions; all three life skills are available to everyone. Active-profession switching retains learned progress but has a 24-hour activation cooldown. Mastery reallocation while idle at a station is separate.

Band entry recipes are available at skill 1, 51, 101, 151, 201 and 251. Outputs use fixed item levels or fixed effects; they do not become obsolete because the artisan must first reach skill 300. The 30-minute first-useful-output goal requires playtesting with ordinary materials and no wealthy alternate character.

Profession mastery branches

Five nodes per branch, three ranks each. Earn one profession mastery point per ten skill levels. Total budget: 30 points out of 45 ranks. Each later node requires one rank in the previous node of the same branch. These points cannot be spent in character combat trees.

Ceilings are shared: 12% eligible common-material savings, 15% faster channels and 10% additional common gathering yield. Rare cores are never saved. Configuration nodes unlock equivalent-budget options, not a free quality multiplier. The named branch restricts bonuses to its product family; a general Handling node never improves every profession at once.

Acquisition, demand and safeguards

Training supplies basic access. Advanced recipes may be tradeable unlocks or activity rewards, but no rare pattern is the sole useful entry-level product. NPC services remain available for essential repair/recovery so an empty market cannot halt normal gameplay.

Completed crafts, enhancement replacement, commissions, low-level customers and controlled common-material demand provide reasons to trade. A cosmetic product is labeled cosmetic; it is not presented as a combat upgrade. Profitability remains an economy simulation and playtest question.

Acceptance criteria

Test a useful product or gathering sale in all six bands; both factions; deterministic progress; item version conflicts; illegal material ownership; interrupted channels; mastery caps; profession deactivation; and full-bag delivery. Equipment users must not need to hold this profession to receive its allowed combat benefits.
