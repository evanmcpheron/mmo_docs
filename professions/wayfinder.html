Identity, responsibilities and boundaries

Committed combined responsibility: Read tracks → choose a safe or direct path → inspect a landmark → report a useful route to the village.

Recommended mentor: Tavin Reed, road cartographer. This is original proposed lore, not a supplied NPC asset.

Choose one primary vocation at character creation. Base fighting, exploring, common gathering, basic fishing and essential NPC services remain available to everyone.

Short session: 5–10 minute target

Read tracks → choose a safe or direct path → inspect a landmark → report a useful route to the village.

Reveal a safer supply route or prioritize a high-risk reconnaissance lead. Personal knowledge and public route access are credited separately.

Pacing is a playtest target. Record movement, at least two distinct interactions and the player’s decision; do not claim measured duration.

Expedition: 15–30 minute target

Follow three authored clues into Elderwood, find a hostile camp, overcome a skirmish or complete a slower investigation and map Willow Cut.

Use the same connected starter route with authored objective/location variation. Risk rewards useful alternatives, not the only required profession tier.

Mastery and skill branches

Compile the six-region field atlas, resolve route clues and complete a regional legendary-hunt investigation. Later arrivals can still earn every critical personal discovery.

  • Observe: interpret clue categories and tracks.
  • Navigate: compare authored route cost, danger and water access.
  • Report: publish permitted map information and regional threat evidence.

Combat connection and calmer path

Scout threats, expose lairs and prepare encounters. Combat rewards follow the common discipline/participation rules, not a special scout-only XP ledger.

Map known-safe paths, interpret landmarks and report trail conditions without fighting the most dangerous camp.

Definition and transaction wiring

VocationId vocation.wayfinder; definition DA_Vocation_Wayfinder.

Three valid clues → personal discovery receipt + eligible shared-route report

Character record owns active vocation and its mastery ledger. Authoritative outing/production receipts advance it. Combat XP, vocation XP and region reputation are never inferred from the same untyped counter. Advanced permissions revalidate the currently active vocation.

  • Discovery and report contract

Contribution to the world

Discover routes, locate resource pockets, deliver authorized threat reports and enable new patrol/merchant options.

One human can complete every essential milestone through personal objectives, base proficiency and affordable NPC alternatives. Human specialists improve choice and efficiency rather than mandatory access.

Failure and recovery

Wrong clue order explains the missing lead; a public shortcut does not retroactively award personal discovery. Reentry cannot replay a discovery reward.

Persist the operation receipt in the same transaction as its effects. On a lost response, query or retry the same request identity. A successful receipt returns the original result; a changed payload under that identity is rejected. A UI timeout is not proof of a database rollback.
