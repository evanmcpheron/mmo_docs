Executed scope versus required gameplay proof

Run records are in verification/faction-checks.json and verification/faction-test-output.txt. The baseline full-guide strict coverage gate remains intentionally failing because original unrelated requirements are incomplete; no coverage statuses are downgraded or marked complete to make it pass.

tools/validate_factions.py validates authored IDs, taxonomy, dependency closure, graph/branch/solo rules and deployment readiness. tools/tests/faction_model.py is a SQLite relational conformance fixture, not an Unreal implementation or production backend. Authentication and complete objective receipts are trusted fixture inputs, inventory uses quantities rather than real instances, and worker jobs are not clients.

Not executed here

Unreal native/UHT, Blueprint compile, cooked-content validation, real dedicated server, production database service, original save migration, actual1/2/5/10/30network clients, AI navigation and combat, party/trade integration and timed process crash recovery are not_run. They require the authorized Briarwake companion implementation and verified assets/toolchain.

No FPS, bandwidth, real client capacity or production readiness claim follows from static HTML tests, source validation or SQLite worker success.

Enabling Briarbound, Ironfang or Tidebound

Keep new-selection false. Author the faction’s reserved map/spawn with authenticated neutral fallback, real root graph and all dialogue/scene/localization keys, mentor/cast placements, services, critical rewards and profession-neutral/solo routes. Preserve historical definition/version IDs. Supply current relationship rows and complete initial standings for every registered faction.

Resolve all dependency IDs against the cooked catalog; validate guard/merchant/guest context and onward safe transfer. Use four-facing shared starter appearance unless a separate sprite audit proves additional art. Confirm selection and continuation policies are independent. Run both schema/deployment gates, character/save/quest migrations, solo full story, opposed neutral cooperation and thirty-client contention/admission tests. Record evidence per faction before switching its selected configuration; never alter C++ branches to recognize its display name.

FQA-01 — Create, persist and inspect Hearthward identity

Runtime status: not_run. Prerequisites: Authorized Briarwake implementation through the indicated phase; UE5.8.3/toolchain, verified Paper2D/PaperZD and session plugin compatibility, authenticated durable service, isolated test realm, cooked Thornmere/Elderwood, source catalog1/policy1/schema3, test accounts with at least2 slots. No live player saves.

Expected: Exactly one faction and class, one active vocation with one saved history. One starter sword and40copper under faction fixture, no other class/vocation reset. Observer sees badge, not account identity/standings/history/tokens. Restart retains original character and grants no kit again.

Offline evidence scope: test_atomic_creation_replay_and_starter_once; test_private_public_fields_do_not_leak; test_create_fault_before_and_after_commit use SQLite values/receipts, not real UI, item instances or restart processes.

  1. Start dedicated Thornmere and the frontend client. Create a character named Road Witness with faction.hearthward-league, discipline.vanguard and vocation.hearthkeeper plus the common four-facing outfit. Read and confirm the permanent faction/class warning.
  2. Verify arrival at spawn.thornmere.hearthward-arrival, charter desk anchor(24,18). Inspect owner identity/history and public PlayerState from a second observer account.
  3. Record CharacterId, starter source receipt, item instance IDs and character revision. Disconnect after acknowledged creation; restart service/zone and log back in.
