Roadmap adjustments

Keep the source phase numbering, but introduce minimal NPC supply services at Phase 13 and durable starter project contracts at Phase 15. Move Slice B acceptance to Phase 19 so Wayfinder cartography is actually available. Full service/region systems extend the same owners later.
