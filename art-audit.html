What is and is not known

The brief describes Mana Seed-style p1 locomotion, p2 work, p3 fishing, p4 other actions and layers 0bas, 1out, 2clo, 4har, 5hat, 6tla and 7tlb. These naming conventions are provisional until the actual archive and creator guides are inspected. They are not a license or proof of a particular pose.

sources/source-art-inventory.csv intentionally contains headers and zero source-file rows. A fabricated row would imply inspection that did not occur.

Appearance contract

Logical slots are Head, Chest, Legs, Hands, Feet, Waist, Shoulders, Cloak, MainHand, OffHand, Necklace, Ring1, Ring2, Trinket1, Trinket2. This is gameplay equipment, not a promise of 15 independent overlays. Chest maps legacy Top, Legs maps Pants; Shoes→Feet, Gloves→Hands, Belt→Waist.

Start from one verified full outfit with compatible body, hair, hat, cloak and tool layers. Audit body × palette × outfit × hairstyle × headwear × cloak × weapon × action × facing. An unsupported cosmetic combination returns AppearanceUnavailable or uses a declared neutral fallback; it must never invalidate legitimately owned gear.
