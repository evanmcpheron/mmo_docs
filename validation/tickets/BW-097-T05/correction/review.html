Visual findings and limits

At actual gameplay scale, corrected hair/skin, torso/arms, legs/boots and blue/purple clothing remain distinguishable. Warm barrel hoops and brown wood regain midtones; stair faces regain reflected form. Head/ears are a few pixels and facial detail is inherently limited. The corrected combined image is substantially easier to read than original T05; superiority over source PBR remains an owner decision.

Outline contrast is reduced most at shallow gaps. Paving is no longer globally darkened by repeated albedo; its repeating source normal/texture pattern still competes with the small character. Leaf/roof intersections remain busy where actual geometry overlaps. These residual details are disclosed, not hidden by resolution changes or blur.

Final-source hard [0.22,0.55,1], balanced [0.25,0.65,1] and lifted [0.4,0.8,1] captures cover both zooms and day/warm/cool plus swatches. Balanced is provisional: hard loses shadow separation; lifted flattens shade. It still needs owner approval.

Direct specular lobes, metallic energy redistribution and transmission are intentionally omitted by the local toon material. Existing environment reflections preserve dark form; this does not implement T07 reflection features. The edge buffer and indirect lighting/reflections use geometric normals; texture normals affect custom direct diffuse lighting. This keeps texture microdetail out of geometric edge selection and is a disclosed difference from source PBR.

384x216 plus existing 1px T04 guard, no AA selected, nearest presentation. 3840x2160 gameplay images are a GPU-rendered 10x nearest presentation. Native 4K toon material image uses 4x MSAA with outlines/highlights off and is supplemental detail only, never pixel/stability proof.

32 consecutive 30Hz samples per path; loop wrap restarts. One screen-space sample cannot reveal hidden geometry. No final packaging, other hardware or performance-budget acceptance is claimed.
