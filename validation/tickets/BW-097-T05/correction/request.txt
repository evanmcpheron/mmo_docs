# Codex Task — BW-097-T05 Rendering Correction and Visual Fidelity

## 1. Assignment

Correct the current **BW-097-T05 outline, convex-highlight, and toon-lighting implementation** so that Briarwake moves substantially closer to the approved visual direction.

**This is a corrective implementation task, not a new feature or a roadmap redesign.**

The current T05 output is visually unacceptable. It introduces excessive dark edges, loses material and character detail, and produces much harsher lighting than the desired reference.

Your responsibility is to investigate the actual causes, fix the T05-owned rendering problems, and generate reproducible evidence demonstrating the improvements.

## 4. Desired visual result

Use the enhanced target image to guide the following achievable T05 characteristics.

### A. Clear but restrained outlines

The target emphasizes characters and major object boundaries without outlining every internal texture feature.

Desired:

- Readable character silhouettes
- Clear boundaries around barrels, crates, and important architecture
- Controlled edge contrast
- Approximately one internal pixel for qualifying silhouette edges
- No dense black pattern across every cobblestone
- No visually distracting outlines across foliage and roof textures

Do not remove legitimate object outlines merely to reduce edge counts.

### B. Rich, readable toon shading

The target contains recognizable material colors and a meaningful range of light and shadow.

Desired:

- Dark shadows that retain readable form
- Visible midtones
- Controlled bright highlights
- Meaningful warm/cool separation
- Recognizable stone, wood, fabric, vegetation, and skin colors
- No large regions crushed to uniform black by the toon shader

Maintain the stylized appearance without destroying the original albedo information.

### C. Character readability

At normal gameplay zoom, the character must remain visibly recognizable.

Preserve:

- Head and hair
- Face/ear silhouette where represented by the available pixels
- Torso and arms
- Separate legs and boots
- Clothing and equipment boundaries
- Distinction from the ground and nearby props

The character should be easier to interpret with T05 enabled than with T05 disabled, or any remaining readability compromise must be explicitly reviewed.

### D. Visual hierarchy

Characters and major objects must remain distinguishable from decorative surface detail.

The current cobblestones produce excessive competing contrast.

The target has environmental detail, but the strongest edge and lighting distinctions serve the larger forms.

T05 should control how the renderer emphasizes geometry. It must not turn every normal discontinuity into a high-contrast feature.

### E. Pixel-art presentation

Preserve the existing 384×216 internal rendering policy, nearest-neighbor presentation, and stable camera behavior.

Do not increase resolution or introduce image blurring to conceal defects.

The output should have deliberate low-resolution edges, stable pixel widths, and coherent shading.

## 5. Diagnose the current implementation before tuning

Do not start by arbitrarily changing shader uniforms until the screenshot looks subjectively better.

Identify the actual cause of each visual regression.

### 5.1 Outline algorithm

Inspect `outlines.gdshader`.

Investigate:

- Reversed-Z depth assumptions
- Inverse orthographic projection
- View-space reconstruction
- Depth-buffer sample positions
- Normal-buffer decoding
- Neighbor comparison orientation
- Farther-pixel contour ownership
- Silhouette classification
- Interior edge detection
- Convex/concave classification
- Alpha composition
- The effect of `gap_pixels`
- The effect of `normal_threshold`

Determine which rendered pixels are actual silhouettes and which are interior geometric or material details.

Specifically investigate whether adjacent cobblestones, foliage surfaces, roof elements, and thin props are being classified as silhouette boundaries too frequently.

Capture the actual depth-only and normal-only diagnostic evidence.

If the defect arises from geometry classification, fix that classification instead of compensating with a global color adjustment.

### 5.2 Toon shader

Inspect `toon.gdshader`.

Determine why the post-T05 result is much darker and visually harsher than the pre-T05 image.

Inspect the relationship between:

- `ALBEDO`
- `DIFFUSE_LIGHT`
- Direct illumination
- Ambient illumination
- Shadow attenuation
- Band thresholds
- Band values
- Godot's shader lighting semantics
- Output color space

The current custom `light()` function explicitly uses `ALBEDO` while accumulating diffuse light.

Verify whether this is mathematically appropriate in the pinned Godot renderer, including whether material albedo is subsequently applied again.

Do not assume either outcome without an isolated material test.

Check whether the shader is applying excessively dark bands or losing ambient contribution.

Compare several sensible band configurations against actual material swatches and character surfaces.

Avoid selecting new band values solely from one screenshot.

### 5.3 Material preservation

Inspect `outline_materials.gd`.

The current toon conversion constructs ShaderMaterials from imported StandardMaterial3D instances.

Verify exactly which material inputs survive that conversion.

In particular, determine the visual consequences of preserving albedo while changing or discarding:

- Normal-map response
- Metallic response
- Roughness
- Specular behavior
- Transparency or alpha-scissor settings
- Double-sided/culling behavior
- UV scaling and texture sampling

Not every source-PBR property must remain physically accurate under toon lighting.

However, removing a material property must be intentional and supported by visual evidence.

Do not discard information unnecessarily if it materially improves shape recognition.

Any local adaptation must remain reversible and T05-scoped.

### 5.4 Compositing and edge contrast

The current outline shader mixes a dark outline color into the sampled screen image.

Investigate whether unconditional, fully opaque replacement with a nearly black color creates unnecessary contrast and apparent filled regions.

Evaluate appropriate outline color, strength, and composition behavior.

Do not reduce outline opacity indiscriminately if doing so makes important silhouettes disappear.

Test controlled alternatives, including context-aware contrast if technically justified within T05.

The result must still satisfy the ticket's one-pixel silhouette criteria.

### 5.5 Pixel-resolution artifacts

Determine whether any observed fragmentation comes from:

- Excessive geometric detail at low resolution
- Texture filtering
- Misclassified edges
- Lighting thresholds
- Antialiasing configuration
- Outline width or sampling
- Incorrect camera correction

Preserve the approved T02/T04 behavior unless a regression is demonstrated.

Document inherent low-resolution limitations rather than inventing corrections for effects outside T05.

## 7. Correct the renderer incrementally

Use the diagnostic evidence to implement targeted corrections.

### Pass A — Restore lighting readability

Correct identified problems in the toon shader.

The resulting toon-only image should maintain the large-scale forms, recognizable colors, and character detail of the source image while displaying intentionally stylized light transitions.

Avoid washed-out lighting and crushed shadow regions.

Keep bright, medium, and dark surfaces distinguishable.

### Pass B — Correct silhouette emphasis

Fix excessive false positives and overly harsh edge composition.

Prioritize character silhouettes, major object boundaries, and meaningful overlapping shapes.

Suppress unnecessary interior outlines where the detection method incorrectly promotes minor detail.

Maintain actual one-internal-pixel contour measurements.

### Pass C — Correct convex highlights

Ensure highlights are selected from appropriate convex geometry rather than generic normal changes.

Check bright and dark surfaces, architectural corners, characters, and thin objects.

Highlights must not produce uniform bright noise across detailed surfaces.

### Pass D — Combine and verify

Combine the corrected effects.

Check that lighting does not mask outlines and that highlights do not create undesirable false edges.

Evaluate character readability, material separation, and visual hierarchy.

Keep the configuration simple and maintainable.

Do not build a new renderer architecture when the existing shader pipeline can be corrected.
