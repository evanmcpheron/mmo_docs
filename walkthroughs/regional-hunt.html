Fixture and readiness

First integrated checkpoint: Phase 24. Stable fixture identifiers: threat.bellroot / encounter.rootbound-leader / region.elderwood.

encounter.elderwood.road-ambush.01 spawns two briar-struck sentries, with an accessible solo alternative to simultaneous switches. Reward fixture is 25 combat XP and one eligible loot roll.

All sample quantities and timings are controlled fixtures. Actual authored definitions must match the scenario before running it.

Configure and connect

Open the normal character-selection and Thornmere route. Assign the relevant definitions from DA_GameCatalog and place the station/NPC/site/encounter named by this scenario. Initialize durable records once through the controlled fixture setup; never manufacture rewards in the UI.

Ownership and user journey

ABriarwakeEnemy and ABriarwakeAIController execute behavior on the zone server. An encounter coordinator within the zone owns the current EncounterInstanceId and reward-eligible participants.

Record_CommandReceipt and character reward records commit encounter rewards. Record_Region stores persistent danger milestones; ordinary transient enemy positions need not be durable.

Remote enemies replicate position, action/telegraph and health presentation. WBP_Loot shows each character’s authorized results; the corpse is not the inventory owner.

Worked execution

  1. Investigate approved clues and resolve an authored camp objective; persist the danger-stage change.
  2. Select the one-human leader mechanics with limited NPC aid or the measured group variant at encounter activation.
  3. Defeat the leader, commit personal milestone/eligible rewards and update patrol and merchant-route projections.

Command and projection sequence

Player input / UMG
  → owned Controller command (custom project API)
  → authoritative validation: identity, lease, action, range
  → durable boundary: revision + idempotency + domain uniqueness
  → atomic state/receipt commit
  → current snapshot / notification
  → UI observer and cosmetic PaperZD/audio response

Concurrent case

Late arrivals use the encounter’s join/scaling policy; damage and targets do not oscillate from momentary logins.

Reconnect and server restart

Recover completed danger transitions after restart; recurring threats renew by explicit policy without destroying permanent construction.
