Quick diagnostic

Ask where the fact survives a zone crash, which one writer can commit it, how a client requests change, what revision/receipt prevents replay and how a new actor rebuilds its view. If the answer is “the widget” or “the animation finished” for money, items, quests or regional stages, the design is incorrect.
